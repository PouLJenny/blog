Research in requirements engineering has produced an extensive body of knowledge, but there are four areas in which the foundation of the discipline seems weak or obscure. This article shines some light in the "four dark corners," exposing problems and proposing solutions. We show that all descriptions involved in requirements engineering should be descriptions of the environment. We show that certain control information is necessary for sound requirements engineering, and we explain the close association between domain knowledge and refinement of requirements. Together these conclusions explain the precise nature of requirements, specifications, and domain knowledge, as well as the precise nature of the relationships among them. They establish minimum standards for what information should be represented in a requirements language. They also make it possible to determine exactly what it means for requirements engineering to be successfully completed.
需求工程的研究已经积累了大量知识，但这一学科的基础中有四个领域显得薄弱或晦暗不明。本文试图照亮这"四个暗角"，揭示其中的问题并提出解决方案。我们将说明：需求工程中涉及的所有描述都应当是对环境的描述。我们将说明：某些控制信息对于健全的需求工程是必需的；我们还将阐明领域知识与需求精化之间的紧密关联。这些结论合在一起，解释了需求（requirements）、规约（specifications）与领域知识（domain knowledge）的确切性质，以及它们彼此之间关系的确切性质。它们为"需求语言中应当表示哪些信息"确立了最低标准。它们还使我们有可能准确判定：需求工程成功完成究竟意味着什么。

1. INTRODUCTION

1. 引言

There was a time when the epigram "requirements say what the system will do and not how it will do it" summarized all of requirements engineering. That time is long past. Research in requirements engineering has now produced a body of knowledge including terminology, methods, languages, tools, and issues acknowledged to be critical.

曾经有一个时代，"需求说明系统将做什么，而不说明它如何做"这句格言就概括了需求工程的全部。那个时代早已过去。如今，需求工程研究已经产生了一整套知识体系，包括术语、方法、语言、工具，以及公认关键的各类议题。

Despite this progress, we have identified four areas in which the foundation of the discipline seems weak or obscure. This article attempts to shine some light in these dark corners, exposing the problems and proposing some solutions. We are led to these conclusions:

尽管有这些进展，我们仍识别出四个领域，其中该学科的基础显得薄弱或晦暗不明。本文试图照亮这些暗角，揭示问题并提出一些解决方案。我们得出如下结论：

(1) All the terminology used in requirements engineering should be grounded in the reality of the environment for which a machine1 is to be built.

(2) It is not necessary or desirable to describe (however abstractly) the machine to be built. Rather, the environment is described in two ways: as it would be without or in spite of the machine and as we hope it will become because of the machine.

(3) Assuming that formal descriptions focus on actions, it is essential to identify which actions are controlled by the environment, which actions are controlled by the machine, and which actions of the environment are shared with the machine. All types of actions are relevant to requirements engineering, and they might need to be described or constrained formally. If formal descriptions focus on states, then the same basic principles apply in a slightly different form.

(4) The primary role of domain knowledge in requirements engineering is in supporting refinement of requirements to implementable specifications. Correct specifications, in conjunction with appropriate domain knowledge, imply the satisfaction of the requirements.

（1）需求工程中使用的全部术语，都应当扎根于"将为之构建机器1的那个环境"的现实之中。

（2）描述待建机器（无论多么抽象地描述）既非必要，也不可取。相反，应当以两种方式描述环境：一是它在没有机器时、或不管机器如何动作时会呈现的样子；二是我们希望它因为机器而变成的样子。

（3）假定形式化描述以动作（action）为中心，那么关键在于识别：哪些动作由环境控制，哪些动作由机器控制，以及环境的哪些动作与机器共享。所有类型的动作都与需求工程相关，都可能需要被形式化地描述或约束。如果形式化描述以状态为中心，那么同样的基本原则以略有不同的形式同样适用。

（4）领域知识在需求工程中的首要作用，是支撑把需求精化为可实现的规约。正确的规约结合恰当的领域知识，蕴涵需求得到满足。

1Throughout this article we use "system" only to refer to a general artifact that might have both manual and automatic components, such as an "airline reservation system." Whenever we are referring to the computer-based artifact that is the target of software development, we use the more precise term "machine."
1全文中我们仅用"系统（system）"指称一种可能同时包含人工部分与自动部分的一般性造物，例如"航空订票系统"。凡是指软件开发目标——那个以计算机为基础的造物——时，我们使用更精确的术语"机器（machine）"。

The four areas of inquiry have not been chosen randomly. Together they explain the precise nature of requirements, specifications, and domain knowledge, as well as the precise nature of the relationships among them. They establish minimum standards for what information should be represented in a requirements language. They also make it possible to determine exactly what it means for requirements engineering to be successfully completed. The final section of the article summarizes these recommendations and elaborates how they can be applied to the wide range of systems and needs served by requirements engineering.

这四个探究领域并非随意选取。它们合在一起，解释了需求、规约与领域知识的确切性质，以及它们彼此之间关系的确切性质。它们为"需求语言中应当表示哪些信息"确立了最低标准。它们还使我们有可能准确判定：需求工程成功完成究竟意味着什么。本文最后一节总结这些建议，并详述如何把它们应用于需求工程所服务的各类系统与需求。

The purposes of this article are not to promote any particular notation, method, or tool, still less to propose new ones. Its purposes are to clarify the nature of requirements engineering, to show the importance of certain information that is often absent or implicit, and to solve some persistent foundational problems. These results can be used to evaluate and improve current and future approaches to requirements engineering.

本文的目的不是推广任何特定的记法、方法或工具，更不是提出新的记法、方法或工具。其目的在于澄清需求工程的性质，展示某些常被遗漏或隐含的信息之重要性，并解决若干长期存在的基础性问题。这些结果可用于评估和改进当前及未来的需求工程方法。

This work is limited to the formal aspects of requirements engineering. It does not cover the sociological aspects of requirements engineering, such as how to communicate with customers or resolve their conflicts.

本工作仅限于需求工程的形式化方面。它不涉及需求工程的社会学方面，例如如何与客户沟通或化解他们之间的冲突。

There are many additional motivations and ramifications not covered in this article [Jackson 1995b]. Although this article uses many brief and partial examples, the same ideas have also been used to work through a complete case study [Jackson and Zave 1995]. In this article the ideas are more fully related to ongoing research in requirements engineering.

还有许多本文未涵盖的动机与推论 [Jackson 1995b]。虽然本文使用了许多简短的、局部的示例，但同样的思想也已被用于完整走通一个案例研究 [Jackson and Zave 1995]。在本文中，这些思想与需求工程的持续研究联系得更为充分。

2. GROUNDING FORMAL REPRESENTATIONS IN REALITY

2. 把形式化表示扎根于现实

Any formal representation uses primitive terms with no inherent formal meaning. In requirements engineering, the meaning of these terms lies in the real world, and the validity of any formal assertion relies on it. Here is a hypothetical dialogue between two requirements engineers working on an information system for a university:

任何形式化表示都会使用一些本身不具备形式化含义的原始术语（primitive term）。在需求工程中，这些术语的含义存在于真实世界，任何形式化断言的有效性都依赖于此。以下是两位需求工程师为某大学信息系统工作时的一段假想对话：

Able: Two important basic types are student and course. There is also a binary relation enrolled. If types and relations are formalized as predicates, then

Able： 两个重要的基本类型是 student 和 course。还有一个二元关系 enrolled。如果把类型和关系形式化为谓词，那么

$$\forall s\;\forall c\,(\mathit{enrolled}(s,c) \Rightarrow \mathit{student}(s) \wedge \mathit{course}(c)).$$
$$\forall s\;\forall c\,(\mathit{enrolled}(s,c) \Rightarrow \mathit{student}(s) \wedge \mathit{course}(c)).$$

Baker: Do only students enroll in courses? I don't think that's true.

Able: But that's what I mean by student!

Baker： 只有学生才选课吗？我认为这不成立。

Able： 但这正是我所说的 student 的意思！

If a student is a person who is enrolled in a course at this university, then Able's assertion is nearly vacuous. If a student is a person who has matriculated at the university and not yet graduated or withdrawn, then Able's assertion is a strong constraint on who can take courses at the university.

如果"学生"指的是在本校选修了某门课程的人，那么 Able 的断言几乎是空洞的。如果"学生"指的是已在本校注册入学、尚未毕业也未退学的人，那么 Able 的断言就是对"谁可以修读本校课程"的一条强约束。

The only way to establish the meaning of a primitive term is to provide an informal explanation of it. This explanation must be clear and precise; it must be written down; and it must be maintained as an essential part of the requirements documentation. We call such an explanation a "designation."2

确立一个原始术语含义的唯一途径，是为它提供一段非形式化的解释。这段解释必须清晰而精确；必须被写下来；并且必须作为需求文档不可或缺的一部分加以维护。我们把这样的解释称为"指称（designation）"。2

2In logic, a designation is called an "interpretation." We avoid the word "interpretation" because it is highly overloaded in computing. It also carries the unfortunate connotation that the logic is real and important, while any correspondence it might have to the world is casual and incidental.
2在逻辑学中，指称被称为"解释（interpretation）"。我们回避"interpretation"一词，因为它在计算领域中被严重过载使用。它还带有一种不幸的言外之意：仿佛逻辑才是真实而重要的，而它与世界之间的任何对应关系都只是随意而附带的。

It is well known that pinning down the real-world meaning of a primitive term can be extremely difficult—it can be fraught with subtleties and ambiguities. Everyone with experience in requirements engineering has, in some situation, used a term freely for a long time before noticing that its meaning is unclear. Yet very few requirements methods address this problem by enforcing the presence of a designation for every primitive term.

众所周知，钉死一个原始术语在真实世界中的含义可能极其困难——其中充满微妙之处与歧义。凡有需求工程经验的人都遇到过这样的情形：长期随意使用某个术语，之后才发觉它的含义并不清楚。然而，极少有需求方法通过强制要求"每个原始术语都必须有指称"来应对这个问题。

A notable exception is the A-7 method. In an early version [Heninger 1980; Parnas and Clements 1986], for every input or output data item a form must be filled out, and the form requires a "description" entry which is a designation of the data item. A later version [Parnas and Madey 1995; van Schouwen et al. 1992] says that "the association between the physical quantities of interest and their mathematical representations must be carefully defined" and claims to have demonstrated that "the use of prose in specifications—a major source of ambiguity and imprecision—can be limited to the environmental-quantity descriptions only."

一个值得注意的例外是 A-7 方法。在其早期版本中 [Heninger 1980; Parnas and Clements 1986]，每一个输入或输出数据项都必须填写一张表格，而表格中要求填写"描述（description）"一栏，这一栏就是该数据项的指称。较晚的版本 [Parnas and Madey 1995; van Schouwen et al. 1992] 指出，所关注的物理量与其数学表示之间的对应关系必须被仔细定义，并宣称已经证明：规约中散文式表述——歧义与不精确的一大来源——可以被限制在仅用于描述环境量的范围内。

Another good example is the textual convention for Z recommended by Wordsworth [1992]. Each schema has text preceding it to explain (designate) the variables declared in it and text following it to explain (paraphrase) the assertions made about those variables.

另一个好例子是 Wordsworth [1992] 推荐的 Z 语言文本约定。每个 schema 前有一段文字用于解释（指称）其中声明的变量，后有一段文字用于解释（改述）关于这些变量所作的断言。

The remainder of this section points out additional benefits of the use of designations. The examples show how designations provide new perspectives on old problems and answer some not-so-obvious questions.

本节余下部分指出使用指称的其他益处。这些例子表明，指称如何为老问题提供新视角，并回答一些并不显而易见的问题。

The Difference between Assertion and Definition.

断言与定义之别

The difference between assertion and definition is well known [Brachman et al. 1983]. However, the concept of a designation provides a slightly new perspective on it.

断言与定义之间的差别是众所周知的 [Brachman et al. 1983]。不过，"指称"这一概念为它提供了一个略微新颖的视角。

The assertion

如下断言

$$\forall s\,(\mathit{student}(s) \Rightarrow \exists c\;\mathit{enrolled}(s,c))$$
$$\forall s\,(\mathit{student}(s) \Rightarrow \exists c\;\mathit{enrolled}(s,c))$$

presupposes a designation of student (perhaps as a person who has matriculated at the university and not yet graduated or withdrawn) and a designation of enrolled. The assertion constrains the real world by expressing a relationship between two real-world phenomena. It might be true or false and should be validated before it is used. The definition

预设了 student 的一个指称（也许是：已在本校注册入学、尚未毕业也未退学的人），以及 enrolled 的一个指称。该断言通过表达两类真实世界现象之间的关系而约束了真实世界。它可能为真也可能为假，在使用前应当加以验证（validate）。而如下定义

$$\mathit{student}(s) \;\stackrel{\text{def}}{\equiv}\; \exists c\;\mathit{enrolled}(s,c)$$
$$\mathit{student}(s) \;\stackrel{\text{def}}{\equiv}\; \exists c\;\mathit{enrolled}(s,c)$$

precludes a designation of student. It extends the formal vocabulary without constraining the real world in any way. It might be useless or misleading, but it cannot be false.

则排除了对 student 的指称。它扩展了形式化词汇表，却丝毫没有约束真实世界。它可能无用或具有误导性，但不可能为假。

In some logical systems the assertion and the definition would be considered equivalent, and in most logical systems the same inferences could be made from both of them. They are different because of designations; if designations are ignored then there is no real difference between them.

在某些逻辑系统中，这条断言与这条定义会被视为等价；而在大多数逻辑系统中，从二者可以作出相同的推理。它们之所以不同，正是因为指称；若忽略指称，二者之间就没有真正的区别。

Goal Regress.

目标回溯

Requirements engineering is about the satisfaction of goals [Dardenne et al. 1993]. But goals by themselves do not make a good starting point for requirements engineering. To see why, consider a project to develop a computer-controlled turnstile guarding the entrance to a zoo [Jackson and Zave 1995].

需求工程关乎目标的满足 [Dardenne et al. 1993]。但目标本身并不构成需求工程的良好起点。要看清缘由，可以设想一个项目：为守卫动物园入口开发一台计算机控制的旋转闸门 [Jackson and Zave 1995]。

If the engineers are told that the goal of the system is to deny entrance to people who have not paid the admission charge, they may decide that the goal has been stated too narrowly. Is not the real goal to ensure the profitability of the zoo? Should they consider other ways of improving profits, such as cutting costs? What if there is more money to be made by closing the zoo and selling the land? And what is the goal of profit? If the goal of profit is the happiness of the zoo owner, would religion or devotion to family be more effective? Obviously there is something wrong here. Almost every goal is a subgoal with some higher purpose. Both engineering and religion are concerned with goal satisfaction; what distinguishes them is their subject matter.

如果工程师被告知系统的目标是阻止未付门票费的人进入，他们可能会认为这个目标陈述得太狭窄了。真正的目标难道不是确保动物园盈利吗？他们是否应当考虑其他改善利润的途径，比如削减成本？如果关闭动物园并出售土地能赚更多的钱又如何？那么利润的目标又是什么呢？如果利润的目标是园主的幸福，那么宗教或对家庭的奉献是否更有效？显然这里出了问题。几乎每一个目标都是某个更高目的之下的子目标。工程与宗教都关心目标的满足；把它们区分开来的是各自的主题范围。

The engineers should be told, in addition to the goal, that the subject matter is the zoo entrance. This information should take the form of designations of phenomena observable at the zoo entrance, such as visitors, coins, and the action of entering the zoo. These designations circumscribe the area in which alternative goal satisfaction strategies can be considered, at the same time that they provide the basis for formal representation of requirements.

除了目标之外，还应当告诉工程师：主题范围是动物园入口。这一信息应当采取"对动物园入口处可观察现象的指称"这种形式，例如访客、硬币，以及进入动物园这一动作。这些指称圈定了可以考虑替代性目标满足策略的范围，同时也为需求的形式化表示提供了基础。

Identity.

同一性

The concept of identity is known to be an important aspect of formal representation schemes [Khoshafian and Copeland 1990]. But formal notions of identity and distinguishability are useless if they do not correspond reliably to the real world. Designations help pin down their real-world meanings.

同一性（identity）概念被公认为形式化表示方案的一个重要方面 [Khoshafian and Copeland 1990]。但如果形式化的同一性与可区分性概念不能可靠地对应到真实世界，它们就毫无用处。指称有助于钉死它们在真实世界中的含义。

Consider, for example, a specification of a bounded buffer in temporal logic [Wing 1990]. This particular specification includes the constraint that for each message m currently placed on the input channel and for each previously placed message m', m and m' are not identical. Wing's discussion says, "This property is . . . an assumption of the environment. This assumption is essential to the validity of the specification."

例如，考虑用时序逻辑给出的一个有界缓冲区规约 [Wing 1990]。该规约中包含这样一条约束：对于当前放入输入通道的每一条消息 m，以及此前放入的每一条消息 m'，m 与 m' 不同一。Wing 的讨论说，这条性质是……对环境的一条假设；这条假设对该规约的有效性至关重要。

Since the term message is ambiguous, it is not clear what this assumption means or how to satisfy it. If message refers to the message content, then the same bit pattern cannot be transmitted twice. This interpretation seems stronger than intended or necessary, but without a designation there is no way to rule it out.

由于 message 一词有歧义，这条假设究竟意味着什么、如何才能满足它，都不清楚。如果 message 指的是消息内容，那么同一个比特模式就不能被传输两次。这种解读似乎比意图或必要的更强，但在没有指称的情况下，无法将其排除。

A good designation of the term message might include information about message creation and usage:

对 message 一词的一个好指称，可能包含关于消息创建与使用的信息：

A process calls the procedure send-msg for the purpose of transmitting data to some other process. A message is the result of a particular call to send-msg and contains the data to be transmitted.
某个进程调用过程 send-msg，目的是把数据传输给另一个进程。一条 message 是对 send-msg 的某次特定调用的结果，其中包含待传输的数据。

Now it is clear that each call to send-msg creates a distinct message, regardless of content. Furthermore, if a call to send-msg includes placing the message on the input channel of the bounded buffer, then the buffer's messages m and m' are guaranteed to be distinct because each is inevitably the result of a distinct call.

现在就清楚了：对 send-msg 的每一次调用都创建一条互异的消息，与内容无关。此外，如果对 send-msg 的一次调用包含把该消息放入有界缓冲区的输入通道，那么缓冲区中的消息 m 与 m' 必然互异，因为每一条都不可避免地是某次互异调用的结果。

3. IMPLEMENTATION BIAS

3. 实现偏置

Requirements are supposed to describe what the desired machine does, not how it does it. More precisely, requirements are supposed to describe what is observable at the interface between the environment and the machine, and nothing else about the machine. To say anything else about the machine is regarded as implementation bias.

需求本应描述所期望的机器做什么，而不是它如何做。更确切地说，需求本应描述在环境与机器之间的接口处可观察到的东西，而不涉及机器的其他任何方面。对机器说任何别的东西，都被视为实现偏置（implementation bias）。

State is used frequently in specifications and specification languages. Very few machines are simple or specialized enough to be specified entirely without state. This causes a problem: specifying a machine in terms of its states appears to introduce serious implementation bias, because its states are internal and not directly observable at the interface between the machine and its environment.

状态（state）在规约和规约语言中被频繁使用。极少有机器简单或专门到可以完全不用状态来规约。这带来一个问题：以状态来规约一台机器，似乎会引入严重的实现偏置，因为机器的状态是内部的，在机器与其环境的接口处不可直接观察。

The next subsection reviews some previously proposed solutions to this problem. Then we propose a completely different way of looking at requirements specification that eliminates the problem altogether. A third subsection expands on the differences and their consequences.

下一小节回顾此前针对该问题提出的一些解决方案。随后我们提出一种看待需求规约的全然不同的方式，它把这个问题整个消除掉。第三小节进一步展开这些差异及其后果。

3.1 Previous Approaches to the Problem

3.1 此前针对该问题的方法

Property-oriented specification techniques [Wing 1990] seek to avoid the use of explicit internal state by stating the minimal constraints on the machine state in the form of assertions. Algebraic and axiomatic techniques are the major techniques in this category. Despite eloquent justifications of this approach [Liskov and Zilles 1975], many people consider it difficult to use, and model-oriented techniques are gaining in popularity.

面向性质（property-oriented）的规约技术 [Wing 1990] 试图通过以断言形式陈述对机器状态的最小约束，来避免使用显式的内部状态。代数技术与公理化技术是这一类中的主要技术。尽管有人对这种方法作出雄辩的辩护 [Liskov and Zilles 1975]，许多人仍认为它难以使用，而面向模型（model-oriented）的技术正日益流行。

Model-oriented specification techniques represent the system state as abstractly as possible, using mathematical structures such as sets, relations, and tuples. Thus model-oriented specifications run a greater risk of implementation bias. In attempting to escape this dilemma, Jones [1990] defines implementation bias as follows:

面向模型的规约技术使用集合、关系、元组等数学结构，尽可能抽象地表示系统状态。因此，面向模型的规约面临更大的实现偏置风险。为摆脱这一困境，Jones [1990] 对实现偏置作出如下定义：

A model-oriented specification is based on an underlying set of states. The model is biased (with respect to a given set of operations) if there exist different elements of the set of states which cannot be distinguished by any sequence of operations.
一个面向模型的规约建立在一个底层状态集合之上。若该状态集合中存在若干不同元素，无法通过任何操作序列加以区分，则该模型（相对于给定的一组操作而言）带有偏置。

For example, a telephone switch may allow a subscriber to enter telephone numbers from which he or she is protected—calls to the subscriber from those numbers will not go through. If the telephone numbers blocked by a subscriber appear only in this requirement, then representing them as a set would be unbiased by Jones' definition. Representing them as a list would be biased by the same definition, because a list would contain ordering information that could not be observed by using any machine function.

例如，某电话交换机可能允许用户输入若干电话号码，使自己免受这些号码的打扰——来自这些号码的呼叫将不会接通。如果用户屏蔽的电话号码只出现在这一条需求中，那么按 Jones 的定义，把它们表示为集合就是无偏置的。而按同一定义，把它们表示为列表则是有偏置的，因为列表会包含次序信息，而这种信息无法通过使用任何机器功能观察到。

Jones' recommendation is to use only states that are unbiased by his definition. But trouble arises because the definition is with respect to a given set of operations. For real machines, the requirements change and expand frequently. It is possible that a new function will be required of the telephone switch, allowing the subscriber to play back his or her blocked numbers in the order entered. After this change, a set representation will be inadequate, and a list will be necessary. Thus Jones' recommendation brings freedom from implementation bias into conflict with maintainability.

Jones 的建议是：只使用按其定义无偏置的状态。但麻烦在于，这个定义是相对于给定的一组操作而言的。对真实的机器来说，需求经常变更和扩展。完全可能会有一项新功能被要求加入该电话交换机：允许用户按输入顺序回放自己屏蔽的号码。在这一变更之后，集合表示就不够用了，必须使用列表。于是，Jones 的建议使"免于实现偏置"与"可维护性"发生了冲突。

Lamport's solution to the problem of implementation bias focuses on the exact relationship between the specification and an implementation that satisfies it [Lamport 1989]. In his method, the machine has an unspecified state space S. Each state component $c_i$ used in the specification is actually a function $c_i: S \rightarrow V_i$ from the state space to a set of values $V_i$.

Lamport 针对实现偏置问题的解法，聚焦于规约与满足该规约的实现之间的确切关系 [Lamport 1989]。在他的方法中，机器拥有一个未加规定的状态空间 S。规约中使用的每个状态分量 $c_i$ 实际上是一个从状态空间到值集合 $V_i$ 的函数 $c_i: S \rightarrow V_i$。

The formal meaning of any specification is a temporal-logic formula in which the state components $c_i$ are existentially quantified variables. The specification is satisfied by any implemented machine with any state space whatsoever, provided that there exist functions $c_i$ on the state space that satisfy the temporal-logic formula. The specification does not bias the implementation because a "specification state" $(c_1(s), c_2(s), \ldots, c_n(s))$ can look completely different from an "implementation state" $s \in S$.

任何规约的形式化含义都是一条时序逻辑公式，其中状态分量 $c_i$ 是被存在量化的变量。只要在该状态空间上存在满足这条时序逻辑公式的函数 $c_i$，则无论实现出来的机器具有怎样的状态空间，该规约都被满足。规约不会给实现带来偏置，因为"规约状态"$(c_1(s), c_2(s), \ldots, c_n(s))$ 可以与"实现状态"$s \in S$ 看起来完全不同。

Also note that, since the $c_i$ can be many-to-one functions, many implementation states can map to the same specification state. This accommodates the realistic situation in which a single specification action is implemented by dozens or even thousands of separate implementation actions.

还要注意，由于 $c_i$ 可以是多对一的函数，许多实现状态可以映射到同一个规约状态。这适应了这样一种现实情形：一个规约层面的动作由数十甚至数千个各自独立的实现层面的动作来实现。

3.2 Requirements Exist Only in the Environment

3.2 需求只存在于环境之中

A portion of the real world becomes the "environment" of a development project because its current behavior is unsatisfactory in some way. The developers propose to build a computer-based machine and connect it to the existing environment in such a way that the behavior of the environment becomes satisfactory. Although we are accustomed to think of machine inputs and outputs, it is important to realize that those inputs and outputs are phenomena in the environment. If they were not part of the environment, then they could not possibly connect the machine to the environment or affect the behavior of the environment.

真实世界的某一部分之所以成为某个开发项目的"环境"，是因为它当前的行为在某种意义上不能令人满意。开发者提议构建一台以计算机为基础的机器，并把它连接到既有环境中，使环境的行为变得令人满意。虽然我们习惯于设想机器的输入与输出，但重要的是要认识到：那些输入与输出都是环境中的现象。如果它们不属于环境的一部分，就根本不可能把机器与环境连接起来，也不可能影响环境的行为。

From this perspective, all statements made in the course of requirements engineering are statements about the environment. The primary distinction necessary for requirements engineering is captured by two grammatical moods. Statements in the "indicative" mood describe the environment as it is in the absence of the machine or regardless of the actions of the machine; these statements are often called "assumptions" or "domain knowledge." Statements in the "optative" mood describe the environment as we would like it to be and as we hope it will be when the machine is connected to the environment. Optative statements are commonly called "requirements." The ability to describe the environment in the optative mood makes it unnecessary to describe the machine.

从这一视角看，需求工程过程中所作的一切陈述都是关于环境的陈述。需求工程所必需的首要区分，由两种语法语气来刻画。以"陈述式（indicative）"语气作出的陈述，描述环境在没有机器时、或不管机器如何动作时的样子；这类陈述常被称为"假设（assumptions）"或"领域知识（domain knowledge）"。以"祈愿式（optative）"语气作出的陈述，描述我们希望环境成为的样子，也即我们期望在机器连接到环境后它会呈现的样子。祈愿式陈述通常被称为"需求（requirements）"。正是"能够以祈愿式语气描述环境"这一点，使得描述机器成为不必要。

This perspective avoids the problem of implementation bias because no statements are made about the proposed machine. The specification state is not a description of the state of the machine, but rather a description of the state of the environment. The specification might be compromised by poor designations or invalid indicative statements, but it cannot overconstrain the implementation.

这一视角避免了实现偏置问题，因为它不对拟建机器作任何陈述。规约状态不是对机器状态的描述，而是对环境状态的描述。规约可能因为糟糕的指称或无效的陈述式语句而受损，但它不会过度约束实现。

Some of our examples are given in Gries and Schneider's equational logic [Gries and Schneider 1993]. Time and temporal properties are encoded in logic as follows [Zave and Jackson 1993].

我们的部分示例采用 Gries 与 Schneider 的等式逻辑给出 [Gries and Schneider 1993]。时间与时序性质在逻辑中按如下方式编码 [Zave and Jackson 1993]。

We assume that actions are atomic and sequential. They are represented by distinct individuals satisfying the predicate action(a). The binary predicate earlier($a_1$, $a_2$) establishes a nondense total order (with an initial member) on actions.

我们假定动作是原子的、顺序的。它们由满足谓词 action(a) 的互异个体来表示。二元谓词 earlier($a_1$, $a_2$) 在动作上确立一个非稠密的全序（带有一个初始成员）。

Because the action sequence is nondense, we can postulate a unique pause between each adjacent pair of actions during which the state has changed in response to the first action, cannot change again until the next action, and can be observed. These pauses are formalized as individuals satisfying the predicate pause(p). begins(a, p) means that action a begins pause p, i.e., precedes it immediately in the temporal sequence. ends(a, p) means that action a ends pause p, i.e., succeeds it immediately in the temporal sequence.

由于动作序列是非稠密的，我们可以在每一对相邻动作之间假设一个唯一的*停顿*（pause）：在此期间状态已经响应前一个动作发生了改变，在下一个动作到来之前不会再改变，并且可以被观察。这些停顿形式化为满足谓词 pause(p) 的个体。begins(a, p) 表示动作 a 开启停顿 p，即在时间序列中紧接在它之前。ends(a, p) 表示动作 a 终止停顿 p，即在时间序列中紧接在它之后。

Note that a specific earlier predicate, a specific begins predicate, and a specific ends predicate combine to represent a single behavior or trace. In other words, each model that satisfies the specification represents one behavior that satisfies the specification. Because most specifications are satisfied by many behaviors, they are also satisfied by many different models.

注意，一个具体的 earlier 谓词、一个具体的 begins 谓词和一个具体的 ends 谓词，组合起来表示单条行为或轨迹。换言之，满足该规约的每个模型表示一条满足该规约的行为。由于大多数规约被许多条行为所满足，它们也被许多不同的模型所满足。

For a simple example of indicative and optative properties, consider a single-customer banking environment with designations of three action types: deposit(a, m) means that a is an action in which amount m is deposited; withdrawal-request(a, m) means that a is an action in which a withdrawal of amount m is requested; and withdrawal-payout(a, m) means that a is an action in which amount m is paid out as a withdrawal. Deposits and withdrawal requests are performed by the customer, while withdrawal payouts are performed by the machine (control of actions will be discussed in detail in the next section).

作为陈述式性质与祈愿式性质的一个简单示例，考虑一个单客户的银行环境，其中指称了三种动作类型：deposit(a, m) 表示 a 是一个存入金额 m 的动作；withdrawal-request(a, m) 表示 a 是一个请求取款金额 m 的动作；withdrawal-payout(a, m) 表示 a 是一个作为取款支付金额 m 的动作。存款与取款请求由客户执行，而取款支付由机器执行（动作的控制将在下一节详细讨论）。

The current account balance is part of the state of the environment—it is a legal debt owed by the bank to the customer. Formally, balance(b, p) means that during pause p the balance is amount b. An indicative property of this environment is that, at any time, the balance is equal to the sum of the amounts of all the previous deposits, minus the sum of the amounts of all the previous withdrawal payouts:

当前账户余额是环境状态的一部分——它是银行欠客户的一笔法律债务。形式化地，balance(b, p) 表示在停顿 p 期间余额为金额 b。该环境的一条陈述式性质是：在任意时刻，余额等于此前所有存款金额之和减去此前所有取款支付金额之和：

$$\begin{aligned}(\forall\, b,\, p \;\mid\; :\;\; & \mathit{balance}(b,p) \;\equiv\; \\ & (b = (+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \mathit{deposit}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m) \\ & \quad - (+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m)))\end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned}(\forall\, b,\, p \;\mid\; :\;\; & \mathit{balance}(b,p) \;\equiv\; \\ & (b = (+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \mathit{deposit}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m) \\ & \quad - (+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m)))\end{aligned}$$

This property uses the uniform quantification of equational logic, in which

这条性质使用了等式逻辑的统一量化记法，其中

$$(*i \mid R(i) : P(i))$$
$$(*i \mid R(i) : P(i))$$

denotes the accumulation of values P(i), using operator $*$, over all values i for which predicate R(i) holds. The "accumulation" of $\forall$ is a conjunction; the "accumulation" of $\exists$ is a disjunction; and the "accumulation" of $+$ is an arithmetic sum. We have also extended earlier to cover pauses as well as actions.

表示对所有使谓词 R(i) 成立的取值 i，用算子 $*$ 对值 P(i) 作累积。$\forall$ 的"累积"是合取；$\exists$ 的"累积"是析取；$+$ 的"累积"是算术求和。我们还把 earlier 扩展到既覆盖动作也覆盖停顿。

An important optative property of this environment is that a withdrawal request leads to a withdrawal payout, provided that the requested amount does not exceed the current balance:

该环境的一条重要祈愿式性质是：一次取款请求导致一次取款支付，前提是所请求的金额不超过当前余额：

$$\begin{aligned}(\forall\, a,\, m,\, p,\, b \;\mid\; & \textit{withdrawal-request}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{ends}(a,p) \wedge \mathit{balance}(b,p) \\ & \wedge\; b \geq m : \\ & (\exists\, a' \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a',m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,a')))\end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned}(\forall\, a,\, m,\, p,\, b \;\mid\; & \textit{withdrawal-request}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{ends}(a,p) \wedge \mathit{balance}(b,p) \\ & \wedge\; b \geq m : \\ & (\exists\, a' \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a',m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,a')))\end{aligned}$$

A requirements method should provide for specification of both indicative and optative properties and should make a clear distinction between them. For example, the recent version of the A-7 method [Parnas and Madey 1995; van Schouwen et al. 1992] features two relations NAT and REQ. NAT documents the constraints on the values of environmental quantities imposed by nature and previously installed systems; it is in the indicative mood. REQ documents further constraints on the environmental quantities to be imposed by the machine; it is in the optative mood.

一种需求方法应当同时支持陈述式性质与祈愿式性质的规约，并在两者之间作出清晰区分。例如，A-7 方法的较晚版本 [Parnas and Madey 1995; van Schouwen et al. 1992] 具有 NAT 与 REQ 两个关系。NAT 记录由自然界以及既有已安装系统对环境量取值所施加的约束，它属于陈述式语气。REQ 记录将由机器对环境量施加的进一步约束，它属于祈愿式语气。

3.3 Consequences

3.3 后果

The idea that a requirements specification should include a model of the environment has been familiar since the early 1980s [Balzer and Goldman 1979; Jackson 1978; 1983; Lehman 1980; Zave 1982]. To the best of our knowledge, we are the first to propose that requirements should contain nothing but information about the environment.

"需求规约应当包含一个环境模型"这一想法自 1980 年代初期以来已为人熟知 [Balzer and Goldman 1979; Jackson 1978; 1983; Lehman 1980; Zave 1982]。但据我们所知，我们是最早提出"需求应当只包含关于环境的信息"的。

The proposed shift in perspective might not seem to make much difference. For example, we have mentioned two properties of the banking system: the balance is the sum of the deposits minus the sum of the withdrawal payouts, and a withdrawal request leads to a withdrawal payout if the balance is large enough. We have said that the first property is indicative, that the second is optative, and that the balance is part of the environment state. But the formal representations of these properties would fit equally well into a more conventional setting in which the balance is considered to be machine state and in which both properties describe requirements on the machine.

所提议的视角转换看上去也许没什么大不同。例如，我们提到了银行系统的两条性质：余额等于存款之和减去取款支付之和；以及若余额足够大，则取款请求导致取款支付。我们说过第一条是陈述式的，第二条是祈愿式的，而余额是环境状态的一部分。但这些性质的形式化表示，放进一个更传统的设定中也同样贴切——在那种设定里，余额被视为机器状态，而两条性质都描述对机器的需求。

One consequence of the difference concerns the popular subject of "domain knowledge" [Iscoe et al. 1991; Jarke et al. 1992]. If specification state is considered to be machine state, then the problem of implementation bias forces the specification to be minimal—there must be nothing that is not necessary to carry out the currently proposed machine functions. When specification state is understood to be environment state, however, then we are free to collect and record interesting information about the environment even before we are sure it will be needed. This freedom is clearly necessary for collecting libraries of reusable information about important portions of the real world. Given the thin spread of domain knowledge [Curtis et al. 1988], any true and relevant indicative statement is a contribution.

这一差别的一个后果，涉及"领域知识"这个热门话题 [Iscoe et al. 1991; Jarke et al. 1992]。如果规约状态被视为机器状态，那么实现偏置问题就迫使规约必须最小化——不得包含任何对执行当前所提议的机器功能而言非必需的东西。然而，当规约状态被理解为环境状态时，我们就可以自由地收集并记录关于环境的有趣信息，哪怕还不确定它们是否会被用到。这种自由对于建立关于真实世界重要部分的可复用信息库显然是必需的。鉴于领域知识分布之稀薄 [Curtis et al. 1988]，任何真实且相关的陈述式语句都是一种贡献。

Another consequence of the difference concerns designations. Obviously, an implemented machine often maintains internal state that mimics the current environment state. The imitation is usually imperfect, however, because there may be delay, errors, and factors in the real world that the machine does not know about.

这一差别的另一个后果涉及指称。显然，一台实现出来的机器往往维护着模仿当前环境状态的内部状态。然而这种模仿通常是不完美的，因为可能存在延迟、错误，以及机器并不知晓的真实世界因素。

For example, an inventory control machine for a warehouse gets reports of receive and ship actions, each having a bin and quantity attribute. From these reports the machine maintains an internal model of the contents of the warehouse. However, due to theft, breakage, mishandling, update delays, clerical errors, and other misfortunes, it is extremely likely that the quantity actually present in a warehouse bin at a particular time is not exactly the same as the quantity indicated by the machine's model.

例如，某仓库的库存控制机器会收到 receive（入库）与 ship（出库）动作的报告，每条报告都带有货位与数量属性。机器根据这些报告维护一个关于仓库内容的内部模型。然而，由于盗窃、破损、错拿错放、更新延迟、文书错误以及其他种种不幸，某一时刻某个仓库货位中实际存在的数量，极有可能与机器模型所显示的数量并不完全一致。

Section 5 discusses how errors and unknown factors can be managed (Bubenko [1983] has treated the subject of update delays). In the meantime, we can point out that components of the machine state cannot be designated. Designations refer to the real world, and the machine state may have no direct correspondence to the real world.

第 5 节将讨论如何管理错误与未知因素（Bubenko [1983] 已处理过更新延迟这一主题）。眼下我们可以指出：机器状态的分量无法被指称。指称指向真实世界，而机器状态可能与真实世界并无直接对应。

In practice, effective specification usually requires many auxiliary terms that would be awkward to designate and that may correspond more closely to the eventual machine state than designated terms. These can always be introduced and grounded in reality by defining them using designated terms. For example, if needed for the requirements of the inventory control machine, the expected-quantity-in-bin can be defined using the designated terms receive(a, s, b), meaning that a is an action in which quantity s for bin b is received by the warehouse, and ship(a, s, b), meaning that a is an action in which quantity s from bin b is shipped from the warehouse:

在实践中，有效的规约通常需要许多辅助术语，它们不便于用指称给出，且可能比被指称的术语更贴近最终的机器状态。这些术语总是可以通过用已指称的术语来定义它们，从而被引入并扎根于现实。例如，若库存控制机器的需求需要用到 expected-quantity-in-bin（货位预期数量），它可以用已指称的术语 receive(a, s, b)（表示 a 是一个仓库收到数量 s 入货位 b 的动作）与 ship(a, s, b)（表示 a 是一个从货位 b 发出数量 s 的动作）来定义：

4. CONTROL OF ACTIONS

4. 动作的控制

In requirements engineering we are always concerned with two actors or agents: the environment and the machine. In any situation with two or more agents, it is always of primary importance to understand issues of control. If a requirements language or method is deficient in representation of actions or control—as many are—then it is necessary to augment it or compose it with other formal languages better suited to the job.

在需求工程中，我们始终面对两个行为者或代理（agent）：环境与机器。在任何存在两个或更多代理的情形下，理解控制方面的问题总是首要之事。如果某种需求语言或方法在动作或控制的表示上有欠缺——许多确实如此——那就有必要对其加以扩充，或者把它与更胜任此事的其他形式化语言组合起来使用。

The first subsection presents two expressive capabilities that are needed for requirements engineering and shows how they are used. The second subsection comments on their presence or absence in several well-known requirements languages or methods; their absence causes a variety of difficulties.

第一小节给出需求工程所需的两项表达能力，并展示它们如何被使用。第二小节评述这两项能力在若干知名需求语言或方法中的有无；它们的缺失会引发各种各样的困难。

4.1 Two Necessary Expressive Capabilities

4.1 两项必需的表达能力

A sufficiently expressive requirements language must provide for the declaration of a finite collection of "action types." These action types partition all possible actions.

一种足够有表达力的需求语言，必须支持声明一个有穷的"动作类型（action type）"集合。这些动作类型对全部可能的动作构成一个划分。

Each action type is either "environment controlled" or "machine controlled," indicating which agent controls, performs, or takes responsibility for actions of that type.3 If actions of a type are sometimes environment-controlled and sometimes machine-controlled, then the type can be divided into two subtypes.

每个动作类型要么是"环境控制的（environment controlled）"，要么是"机器控制的（machine controlled）"，以表明由哪个代理控制、执行该类型的动作或对其负责。3 如果某类型的动作有时由环境控制、有时由机器控制，那么可以把该类型划分为两个子类型。

3The term "machine-controlled" may seem to indicate a departure from the principle that requirements say nothing about the machine, but the lapse is only terminological. We would prefer to use "active" (actively controlled by the environment) and "passive" (passively done to the environment), where a passive action must be machine-controlled because it is not performed by the environment, and there is no other relevant agent to perform it. The "active/passive" terminology is properly centered on the environment, but unfortunately the meaning of these words is much harder to remember.
3"机器控制的"这一说法看似偏离了"需求不谈论机器"这一原则，但这一失误仅仅是术语上的。我们更愿意使用"主动的（active）"（由环境主动控制）与"被动的（passive）"（被动地施加于环境）：被动动作必定是机器控制的，因为它不由环境执行，而又没有别的相关代理来执行它。"主动／被动"这套术语恰当地以环境为中心，但遗憾的是这两个词的含义要难记得多。

Each action type is also either "shared" or "unshared." If it is shared, then it is part of the real world that is shared between (belonging to, observable by) both the machine and environment. If it is unshared, then it is private to the environment and unobservable by the machine.

每个动作类型还要么是"共享的（shared）"，要么是"非共享的（unshared）"。若它是共享的，则它属于机器与环境之间共享的（同时归属于双方、可被双方观察的）那部分真实世界。若它是非共享的，则它为环境所私有，机器无法观察。

Obviously an action cannot be both machine-controlled and unshared. This leaves three possible combined action categories: environment-controlled/unshared, environment-controlled/shared, and machine-controlled/shared. A requirements language must be able to express which category each action type belongs to. The importance of this information should be self-evident. The implemented machine must perform the machine-controlled actions and not the environment-controlled ones. The implemented machine can observe and make use of its knowledge of the shared actions, while the unshared actions are beyond its reach.

显然，一个动作不可能既是机器控制的又是非共享的。这就留下了三种可能的组合类别：环境控制／非共享、环境控制／共享、机器控制／共享。需求语言必须能够表达每个动作类型属于哪一类。这一信息的重要性应当是不言自明的。实现出来的机器必须执行机器控制的动作，而不得执行环境控制的动作。实现出来的机器可以观察共享动作并利用对它们的知识，而非共享动作则是它够不着的。

The second necessary expressive capability is the full ability to state constraints on actions in all three categories. The requirements engineer should be able to make assertions about all actions in both the indicative and optative moods. If a kind of property can be expressed about actions in one category, then it should be possible to state the same property of actions in other categories.

第二项必需的表达能力，是能够完整地对全部三类动作陈述约束。需求工程师应当能够以陈述式和祈愿式两种语气对所有动作作出断言。如果某一类性质可以针对某一类别的动作表达出来，那么就应当能够针对其他类别的动作陈述同样的性质。

To show why the second expressive capability is necessary, we wish to give examples of almost all the possibilities suggested above. In some formalisms, however, it can be difficult to separate different kinds of properties and difficult to determine exactly which actions are constrained by a property. So, for the purpose of giving illustrative examples, we shall use a formal language in which both of these are straightforward.

为说明第二项表达能力何以必需，我们希望给出上面提到的几乎全部可能性的示例。然而在某些形式体系中，很难把不同种类的性质分离开，也很难确定某条性质究竟约束了哪些动作。因此，为了给出示例，我们将使用一种在这两方面都很直截了当的形式化语言。

Buchi automata are equivalent in expressive power to linear-time temporal logic. In linear-time temporal logic any property can be expressed as a combination of safety and liveness properties [Alpern and Schneider 1987]. Safety properties can be paraphrased as "something bad must not happen," and liveness properties can be paraphrased as "something good must happen."

Büchi 自动机在表达能力上等价于线性时序逻辑。在线性时序逻辑中，任何性质都可以表达为安全性（safety）与活性（liveness）性质的组合 [Alpern and Schneider 1987]。安全性性质可以改述为"坏事不得发生"，活性性质可以改述为"好事终将发生"。

Alpern and Schneider show that the structure of a Buchi automaton reveals whether it expresses a "pure" safety property, a "pure" liveness property, or a combination of the two. A nontrivial safety property is specified by an automaton with no nonaccepting states, but with at least one state having no out-transition on at least one action type in its vocabulary. It asserts that in a state with missing out-transitions, actions of the missing types do not occur. In other words, it constrains certain action types by prohibiting their occurrence in certain states. A nontrivial liveness property is specified by an automaton with no missing out-transitions, but with at least one nonaccepting state. It asserts that, whenever a nonaccepting state is reached, actions must eventually occur to cause a transition to an accepting state. In other words, it constrains certain action types by requiring them to occur in certain states.

Alpern 与 Schneider 表明，Büchi 自动机的结构可以揭示它表达的是"纯粹的"安全性性质、"纯粹的"活性性质，还是二者的组合。一条非平凡的安全性性质，由这样的自动机刻画：它没有非接受状态，但至少有一个状态在其字母表的至少一个动作类型上没有出边。它断言：在缺少出边的状态中，缺失类型的动作不会发生。换言之，它通过禁止某些动作类型在某些状态下发生来约束这些动作类型。一条非平凡的活性性质，由这样的自动机刻画：它没有缺失的出边，但至少有一个非接受状态。它断言：一旦到达某个非接受状态，就必须最终发生某些动作以促成向接受状态的转移。换言之，它通过要求某些动作类型在某些状态下必须发生来约束它们。

First consider a real-world environment consisting of a gate, a physical turnstile guarding the gate, a coin slot, and the customers who wish to enter the gate. The desired machine is a computer-based controller for the gate complex.

首先考虑一个真实世界环境，它由一道闸口、一道守卫该闸口的实体旋转闸门、一个投币口，以及希望进入闸口的顾客组成。所期望的机器是这套闸口系统的计算机控制器。

There are five important action types in the gate complex. A pay action occurs whenever a customer puts enough money in the coin slot to be allowed to enter the gate. A push action occurs whenever a customer pushes the turnstile and thereby starts it moving. An enter action occurs whenever a customer pushes the turnstile through to its home position, thus entering the gate. These three action types are environment-controlled; pays and pushes are shared with the machine, while enters are not. In addition, there are machine-controlled lock and unlock actions. Five properties of interest are specified formally in Figure 1.

闸口系统中有五种重要的动作类型。每当顾客在投币口投入足够的钱以被允许进入闸口时，就发生一次 pay 动作。每当顾客推动旋转闸门并因而使其开始转动时，就发生一次 push 动作。每当顾客把旋转闸门一直推到其初始位置从而进入闸口时，就发生一次 enter 动作。这三种动作类型是环境控制的；pay 与 push 与机器共享，而 enter 不共享。此外还有机器控制的 lock 与 unlock 动作。图 1 形式化地给出了五条值得关注的性质。

G1 is an indicative safety property, expressed in terms of the action types push and enter. It says that the turnstile is constructed so that pushes and enters alternate strictly, beginning with a push. Since both enters and pushes are constrained, G1 appears in two different places in Table I, one reflecting the fact that it constrains enters (environment-controlled, unshared actions) and one reflecting the fact that it constrains pushes (environment-controlled, shared actions).

G1 是一条陈述式安全性性质，以动作类型 push 与 enter 表达。它表明旋转闸门的构造使得 push 与 enter 严格交替出现，且以一次 push 开始。由于 enter 与 push 都被约束，G1 在表 I 中出现在两个不同位置：一处反映它约束了 enter（环境控制、非共享的动作），另一处反映它约束了 push（环境控制、共享的动作）。

The manufacturers of the turnstile hardware have designed it under the assumption that the controller will perform lock and unlock actions in a strictly alternating sequence. Because of this fault-intolerant design, an improperly controlled turnstile may break, enter an undocumented state, or enter a randomly chosen state. As long as the alternation of locks and unlocks is maintained, after a lock action the turnstile is locked and remains locked until the next unlock action. The turnstile is designed so that whenever it is locked, push actions cannot occur (G4). This is an indicative safety constraint on push actions, and it is the main property that enables the turnstile to do its job of guarding the gate.

The most important requirement on the gate complex is that people are forced to pay for the privilege of entering. Forcing the alternation of enters and pays would be too restrictive, however, because a teacher should be able to pay at once for a whole classful of students and then lead them in without interruption. The correct property is that the accumulated number of enters never exceeds the accumulated number of pays. This is a safety constraint on enter actions (G5).

对闸口系统最重要的需求是：人们必须为进入的特权付费。然而，强制 enter 与 pay 交替出现会限制过严，因为一位老师应当能够一次性为整整一班学生付费，然后不间断地带他们进入。正确的性质是：累计的 enter 次数永远不超过累计的 pay 次数。这是对 enter 动作的一条安全性约束（G5）。

The previous examples should be sufficient to show what phrases such as "do not occur" and "leads to" mean in the brief paraphrases of these properties found in Table I.

电梯余下的性质不值得形式化为 Büchi 自动机，或因为它们过于重复，或因为它们过于复杂。前面的示例应当足以说明"不会发生（do not occur）"与"导致（leads to）"这类措辞，在表 I 所列这些性质的简要改述中意味着什么。

Note from L4 that motor-ons and motor-offs need not alternate—extra commands are simply ignored by the motor. In a totally different situation, a motor-up action could actually be designated as an action of changing the motor direction from down to up (and similarly, from up to down for the motor-down action). In this situation there is an indicative constraint that (L5) motor-ups and motor-downs alternate. L5 is indicative because, given the real-world meanings of motor-up and motor-down, no other behavior is possible—you cannot change a direction from down to up when the direction is already up! Compare this to G3, which is optative because there is nothing in the environment preventing two consecutive lock signals (it is just that we will be sorry if the machine performs two consecutive locks and thus breaks the turnstile).

从 L4 可以看出，motor-on 与 motor-off 不必交替出现——多余的命令会被马达直接忽略。在一种完全不同的情形下，motor-up 动作实际上可以被指称为"把马达方向由向下改为向上"的动作（同理，motor-down 动作为由向上改为向下）。在这种情形下，存在一条陈述式约束：（L5）motor-up 与 motor-down 交替出现。L5 是陈述式的，因为给定 motor-up 与 motor-down 的真实世界含义，其他行为都不可能发生——当方向已经是向上时，你无法把方向由向下改为向上！把这与 G3 作对比：G3 是祈愿式的，因为环境中并没有任何东西阻止两个连续的 lock 信号（只不过，如果机器连续执行两次 lock 从而弄坏旋转闸门，我们会感到懊悔而已）。

One of the simpler lift requirements is that (L6) a request action leads to a corresponding request-light-on action.

电梯较为简单的需求之一是：（L6）一次 request 动作导致一次对应的 request-light-on 动作。

An arrive[floor] action occurs when the lift arrives at the indicated floor (arrival at a floor does not necessarily mean that the lift stops there). Arrive actions are shared with the machine by means of sensors. For efficient lift scheduling, there might be a requirement that (L7) foolish arrive actions do not occur, where "foolish" means that they do not satisfy or move toward satisfying any outstanding request.

当电梯抵达所示楼层时，就发生一次 arrive[floor] 动作（抵达某层并不必然意味着电梯在该层停靠）。arrive 动作通过传感器与机器共享。为实现高效的电梯调度，可能会有这样一条需求：（L7）不发生愚蠢的 arrive 动作；这里"愚蠢"意为它们既不满足、也不趋向于满足任何未完成的请求。

Although we have no examples of these particular constraints and believe they do not occur, the general point of the table is established without them.

闸口系统的更多性质（G6、G7）与电梯环境的更多性质（L8）将在下一节补充。等我们讲完这些示例，表 I 中所有格子都会有示例条目，唯独"对机器控制动作的陈述式活性约束"那一格除外。虽然我们没有这类约束的示例，并且认为它们不会出现，但即便没有它们，该表所要说明的总体要点也已经成立。

Any specification method will provide for optative constraints on machine-controlled actions (the lower-right corner of Table I) because they describe what the machine must do. But optative constraints on environment-controlled actions (the upper-right corner of Table I) are also important because many of the true requirements reside there and are enforced only indirectly by machine actions. Indicative properties (the left half of Table I) are often the intermediaries through which machine actions enforce optative constraints in the environment. 任何规约方法都会支持对机器控制动作的祈愿式约束（表 I 的右下角），因为它们描述机器必须做什么。但对环境控制动作的祈愿式约束（表 I 的右上角）同样重要，因为许多真正的需求就居于其中，并且只能通过机器动作间接地被强制成立。陈述式性质（表 I 的左半部分）往往是机器动作借以在环境中强制祈愿式约束的中介。这些主题将在第 5 节进一步探讨。

4.2 Examples of Control Issues

4.2 控制问题的若干实例

A Z specification [Spivey 1992] is organized in terms of operations that affect the specification state. There is no formal notion of control. Some Z specifications include operations controlled by diverse agents, for example, Spivey's real-time kernel [Spivey 1990]. Since there is no way to say which agent controls which operation, from a formal perspective such specifications are as bizarre as Lamport's example of a queue that spontaneously initiates its own put and get actions [Lamport 1989].

一份 Z 规约 [Spivey 1992] 是围绕影响规约状态的操作来组织的。其中没有形式化的控制概念。有些 Z 规约包含由不同代理控制的操作，例如 Spivey 的实时内核 [Spivey 1990]。由于无法说明哪个代理控制哪个操作，从形式化的角度看，这类规约就像 Lamport 举的那个例子一样古怪：一个自发地发起自己的 put 与 get 动作的队列 [Lamport 1989]。

More commonly, users of Z assume that the specification state is the machine state and that the operations are the interface between the machine and its environment. Operations are always initiated by the environment and have "input" and "output" variables for conveying information across the machine/environment interface.

更常见的是，Z 的使用者假定规约状态就是机器状态，而各项操作就是机器与其环境之间的接口。操作总是由环境发起，并带有"输入"和"输出"变量，用于在机器／环境接口上传递信息。

The computed precondition of a Z operation gives the circumstances under which it can be invoked safely (the operation can be carried out, and all state invariants can be preserved).6 The trouble with the common usage of Z outlined above is that there is no mechanism for recording constraints on what the environment can and cannot do. In particular, there is no way of answering the all-important question: "What properties of the environment justify ignoring the possibility that an operation is initiated when its precondition is not satisfied?"

一个 Z 操作的计算前置条件给出了它可以被安全调用的情形（该操作可以被执行，且所有状态不变式都能被保持）。6 上述 Z 的常见用法的麻烦在于：没有任何机制来记录"环境能做什么、不能做什么"的约束。特别是，无法回答这个至关重要的问题："环境的哪些性质，使我们有理由忽略'某操作在其前置条件不满足时被发起'这种可能性？"

6Again, this is only the most common interpretation, and others are possible [Jackson 1996].
6再次强调，这只是最常见的解读，其他解读也是可能的 [Jackson 1996]。

The Software Cost Reduction (SCR) method [Heitmeyer et al. 1995a; 1995b] emphasizes constraints on the machine and has limited capabilities for constraining the environment.7 These limitations can cause gaps or anomalies.

软件成本削减（Software Cost Reduction, SCR）方法 [Heitmeyer et al. 1995a; 1995b] 侧重于对机器的约束，而在约束环境方面能力有限。7 这些局限可能造成缺口或反常。

7The recent version of the A-7 method [Parnas and Madey 1995; van Schouwen et al. 1992] and the SCR method have common roots in the early version of the A-7 method [Heninger 1980; Parnas and Clements 1986], but the two have now diverged and are quite different with respect to the issues discussed in this article.
7A-7 方法的较晚版本 [Parnas and Madey 1995; van Schouwen et al. 1992] 与 SCR 方法有共同的源头，即 A-7 方法的早期版本 [Heninger 1980; Parnas and Clements 1986]，但二者如今已分道扬镳，在本文所讨论的问题上颇为不同。

The problem is illustrated by an SCR specification of a real-time railroad crossing system [Atlee and Gannon 1993]. The environment is described in terms of boolean variables such as Train (which is true when a train is close to the crossing) and TrainXing (which is true when the train is actually going through the crossing). Within some versions of the SCR method it is possible to say that these two variables cannot be true at the same time, but in no version is it possible to say that when Train becomes true, it stays true for at least 300 seconds.

这一问题可由一份关于实时铁路道口系统的 SCR 规约来说明 [Atlee and Gannon 1993]。该环境用一些布尔变量来描述，例如 Train（当列车接近道口时为真）与 TrainXing（当列车实际正在通过道口时为真）。在 SCR 方法的某些版本中，可以表达这两个变量不能同时为真，但在任何版本中都无法表达：当 Train 变为真之后，它至少保持真 300 秒。

As a result of this deficiency, Atlee and Gannon specify this crucial information as a constraint on the machine. The specified machine is in mode BC when Train is true and in mode Crossing when TrainXing is true. The machine specification states that a mode transition from BC to Crossing occurs if and only if TrainXing becomes true and if the machine has been in mode BC for at least 300 seconds.

由于这一欠缺，Atlee 与 Gannon 把这条关键信息规约为对机器的一条约束。被规约的机器在 Train 为真时处于 BC 模式，在 TrainXing 为真时处于 Crossing 模式。机器规约陈述：当且仅当 TrainXing 变为真、且机器已处于 BC 模式至少 300 秒时，才发生从 BC 到 Crossing 的模式转移。

This is a dangerous specification if taken literally. The BC and Crossing modes provide the gate control algorithm with its information on the location of the train. If a train is faster than expected, then these modes will become decoupled from the train location they are supposed to reflect.

若按字面理解，这是一份危险的规约。BC 与 Crossing 模式向闸门控制算法提供了关于列车位置的信息。如果某列车比预期更快，这些模式就会与它们本应反映的列车位置脱钩。

In CSP [Hoare 1985], the concept of "shared actions" conflates two concepts that we have kept separate: control and joint observability. Section 5 will show that these two concepts play different roles in refinement of requirements, so that there is practical value in distinguishing them.

在 CSP [Hoare 1985] 中，"共享动作"这一概念混同了我们一直分开处理的两个概念：控制与共同可观察性。第 5 节将表明，这两个概念在需求精化中扮演不同角色，因此区分它们具有实际价值。

Suppose that an environment and a machine are represented formally as two concurrent processes in CSP. Consider the problem of recovering from this representation which action types fall into which of the three categories. It is easy enough to recover which actions are jointly observable by the two processes ("shared" in our sense) and which are private to the environment process, because that is a syntactic property.

假设某个环境与某台机器在形式上被表示为 CSP 中的两个并发进程。考虑这样一个问题：从这种表示中恢复出哪些动作类型落入三个类别中的哪一类。要恢复出哪些动作可被两个进程共同观察（即我们所说的"共享"）、哪些为环境进程所私有，是相当容易的，因为这是一个句法性质。

Recovering the control information is harder. For each jointly observable action type and each process, there are exactly two possibilities: the process sometimes refuses to participate in actions of that type, or the process never refuses to participate in actions of that type. If one process sometimes refuses an action type, and the other process never refuses it, then actions of that type are controlled by the sometimes-refusing process. If both processes sometimes refuse an action type, then actions of that type are jointly controlled, which we believe is a misrepresentation in essentially all real situations. If both processes never refuse an action type, then it is impossible to tell which process controls it. (Pay actions in the turnstile system are like this—they are controlled by the environment, but in the environment as described they can occur at any time.)

恢复控制信息则更困难。对每个共同可观察的动作类型和每个进程而言，恰有两种可能：该进程有时拒绝参与该类型的动作，或者该进程从不拒绝参与该类型的动作。如果一个进程有时拒绝某动作类型，而另一个进程从不拒绝它，那么该类型的动作就由那个"有时拒绝"的进程控制。如果两个进程都有时拒绝某动作类型，那么该类型的动作就是共同控制的——我们认为这在基本上一切真实情形中都是一种失真表示。如果两个进程都从不拒绝某动作类型，那就无法判断哪个进程控制它。（旋转闸门系统中的 pay 动作就是这样——它们由环境控制，但在如上所述的环境中，它们可以在任何时候发生。）

Thus CSP is almost expressive enough in terms of control of actions, but it might be confusing. When CSP is used for requirements, engineers would do well to augment it with a more explicit statement of their intentions.

因此，就动作的控制而言，CSP 的表达力几乎是够用的，但它可能带来困惑。当 CSP 被用于需求时，工程师最好用一份更明确的意图陈述来补充它。

Naturally, there are many good examples as well as bad ones. Lamport's method [Lamport 1989] has precise identification and flexible treatment of all shared actions, but no unshared actions. The recent A-7 method uses a state-oriented formalism; it has precise identification and flexible treatment of all shared state components, but no unshared state components. Agent formalisms [Dardenne et al. 1993; Dubois et al. 1993; Feather 1987; Feather et al. 1991; Jeremaes et al. 1986; Johnson 1988] have all the recommended expressive capabilities. Each agent has specific actions that it can perform. Typically some agents represent the environment while some represent the machine, and environment agents are not limited to those that interact directly with the machine. If the agent formalism is also a deontic logic [Jeremaes et al. 1986], then permissions and obligations are like safety and liveness in our example—it must be possible to assert them in both the indicative and optative moods.

自然，好的例子和坏的例子一样多。Lamport 的方法 [Lamport 1989] 对全部共享动作有精确的标识和灵活的处理，但没有非共享动作。较晚的 A-7 方法使用面向状态的形式体系；它对全部共享状态分量有精确的标识和灵活的处理，但没有非共享状态分量。代理形式体系 [Dardenne et al. 1993; Dubois et al. 1993; Feather 1987; Feather et al. 1991; Jeremaes et al. 1986; Johnson 1988] 具备我们所推荐的全部表达能力。每个代理都有它能够执行的特定动作。通常有些代理代表环境，有些代表机器，而环境代理并不局限于那些与机器直接交互的代理。如果该代理形式体系同时还是一种道义逻辑（deontic logic）[Jeremaes et al. 1986]，那么许可（permissions）与义务（obligations）就类似于我们例子中的安全性与活性——必须能够以陈述式和祈愿式两种语气对它们作出断言。

5. THE ROLE OF DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE

5. 领域知识的作用

A requirement is an optative property. Let R be the set of requirements for a software development project, i.e., the set of optative properties whose satisfaction will fully satisfy the customer.

需求是一条祈愿式性质。设 R 为某个软件开发项目的需求集合，即那些一旦被满足就能让客户完全满意的祈愿式性质的集合。

A specification is also an optative property, but one that must be implementable.8 Let S be the set of specifications for a software development project. S can be given to a software development team, which can implement it successfully without recourse to any additional information. S must be such that an implementation of it, connected to the environment, ensures that all the properties of R are satisfied.

规约同样是一条祈愿式性质，但它必须是可实现的。8 设 S 为某个软件开发项目的规约集合。S 可以交给软件开发团队，团队无需借助任何额外信息就能成功地实现它。S 必须满足：对它的实现在连接到环境后，确保 R 中的所有性质都得到满足。

8We are discussing implementability in principle. A specification that is implementable in principle may not be implementable in practice—for instance, because all of the available computers are too slow.
8我们讨论的是原则上的可实现性。一份原则上可实现的规约，在实践中可能并不可实现——例如，因为所有可用的计算机都太慢。

The gap between requirements and specifications has long been recognized. The process of bridging this gap is often referred to as refinement of requirements, by analogy with specification or program refinement. Specification refinement is concerned with (1) removing the features of a specification that are not executable by the target implementation platform and (2) replacing them with features that are executable on that platform. Requirements refinement is concerned with (1) identifying the aspects of a requirement that cannot be guaranteed or effected by a computer alone and (2) augmenting or replacing them until they are fully implementable.

需求与规约之间的鸿沟早已为人所认识。弥合这一鸿沟的过程常被称为需求精化（refinement of requirements），这是类比于规约精化或程序精化的说法。规约精化关心的是：（1）去掉规约中目标实现平台无法执行的特征；（2）用该平台上可执行的特征替换它们。需求精化关心的是：（1）识别出需求中那些仅靠计算机无法保证或无法实现的方面；（2）对它们加以扩充或替换，直到它们完全可实现。

It has also long been recognized that domain knowledge (or domain modeling, assumptions about the environment, etc.) should play an important role in requirements engineering, but there is uncertainty about what it is for. Because there is uncertainty about what it is for, there is also uncertainty about how much of it to gather [Iscoe et al. 1991].

同样早已为人所认识的是，领域知识（或称领域建模、关于环境的假设等）应当在需求工程中扮演重要角色，但人们对它究竟有何用途仍不确定。正因为对它的用途不确定，人们对该收集多少领域知识也不确定 [Iscoe et al. 1991]。

The primary role of domain knowledge is to bridge the gap between requirements and specifications. Requirements that are not specifications are always converted into specifications with the help of domain knowledge. Let K be the relevant domain knowledge, i.e., the set of relevant indicative properties. Then S and K together must be sufficient to guarantee that the requirements are satisfied:

领域知识的首要作用，是弥合需求与规约之间的鸿沟。那些本身不是规约的需求，总是借助领域知识被转换为规约。设 K 为相关的领域知识，即相关陈述式性质的集合。那么 S 与 K 合在一起必须足以保证需求得到满足：

$$S,\; K \;\vdash\; R$$
$$S,\; K \;\vdash\; R$$

It is important to note that a precise characterization of the difference between specifications and requirements depends on precise location of the interface between the machine and environment, i.e., on knowing what the shared actions are. Many papers in requirements engineering are concerned with the flexibility of the machine/environment boundary [Dardenne et al. 1993; Dobson et al. 1994; Feather 1987; 1994; Feather et al. 1991; Mostow 1983; Swartout and Balzer 1982]. Thus we are characterizing the possible results of their explorations, not the explorations themselves.

需要注意的是，要精确刻画规约与需求之间的差别，取决于精确定位机器与环境之间的接口，也即取决于知道哪些动作是共享的。需求工程中的许多论文关注机器／环境边界的灵活性 [Dardenne et al. 1993; Dobson et al. 1994; Feather 1987; 1994; Feather et al. 1991; Mostow 1983; Swartout and Balzer 1982]。因此我们所刻画的是他们那些探索的可能结果，而不是探索过程本身。

Some requirements are already implementable, so they can be copied directly from R to S. Requirements that are not implementable fail to be so for three general reasons. We shall discuss these reasons in the next three subsections, followed by a subsection of comparisons to related ideas.

有些需求本身已经可实现，因此可以直接从 R 复制到 S。不可实现的需求之所以不可实现，一般出于三种原因。我们将在接下来的三个小节中讨论这些原因，随后再用一小节与相关思想作比较。

5.1 Environment Constraint

5.1 环境约束

Some requirements are not directly implementable because the only way to satisfy them is to constrain an action that is controlled by the environment. As an example of a requirement with an environment constraint, there is a requirement on the gate complex that (G5) enters do not outnumber pays. The Buchi automaton in Figure 1 makes it clear that this is a constraint on enter actions, which are controlled by the environment.

有些需求不能直接实现，因为满足它们的唯一方式是去约束一个由环境控制的动作。作为带有环境约束的需求的一个例子，闸口系统有这样一条需求：（G5）enter 的次数不超过 pay 的次数。图 1 中的 Büchi 自动机清楚地表明，这是对 enter 动作的一条约束，而 enter 由环境控制。

To satisfy this requirement, we need several pieces of domain knowledge. It is important that (G1) pushes and enters alternate, so that enters can be prevented by preventing pushes. It is important that (G4) whenever the turnstile is locked, pushes cannot occur. The effects of lock and unlock actions are also important, because they are used to put the turnstile into a locked state. Finally, we also need domain knowledge giving us a lower bound on the elapsed time between two consecutive pushes (because if pushes can follow each other arbitrarily closely, then the machine must be infinitely fast).

为满足这条需求，我们需要若干条领域知识。重要的是（G1）push 与 enter 交替出现，从而可以通过阻止 push 来阻止 enter。重要的是（G4）只要旋转闸门被锁定，push 就不可能发生。lock 与 unlock 动作的效果也很重要，因为它们被用来把旋转闸门置于锁定状态。最后，我们还需要一条领域知识，给出两次连续 push 之间所经历时间的下界（因为如果 push 可以任意紧密地接连发生，那么机器就必须无限快）。

The resultant specification [Jackson and Zave 1995] consists of properties constraining when lock and unlock actions must and must not occur (these properties are summarized as G6 and G7 in Table I). These appropriate actions keep the turnstile locked at the right times, because of the indicative properties, to satisfy G5.

由此得到的规约 [Jackson and Zave 1995] 由若干条性质构成，它们约束 lock 与 unlock 动作必须在何时发生、以及不得在何时发生（这些性质在表 I 中概括为 G6 与 G7）。这些恰当的动作在恰当的时刻使旋转闸门保持锁定，并借助那些陈述式性质，从而满足 G5。

The lift requirement L2 must be refined to several other requirements, including (L8) group waiting at a floor leads to an arrive[floor] action. L8 is not a specification because arrive actions are environment-controlled. Satisfaction of this requirement relies (1) on the domain knowledge that certain motor states will cause the lift to move and (2) on specification of machine actions that control the motor state appropriately.

电梯需求 L2 必须被精化为其他若干条需求，其中包括：（L8）某层有群体在等待，导致一次 arrive[floor] 动作。L8 不是规约，因为 arrive 动作是环境控制的。这条需求的满足依赖于：（1）"某些马达状态会使电梯移动"这一领域知识；（2）对恰当控制马达状态的机器动作所作的规约。

To summarize, requirements that constrain the environment cannot be satisfied by a machine acting alone. Such requirements are always satisfied by coordinating specifications with domain knowledge. A requirement with an environment constraint should always be verified by a demonstration or proof that specified properties, conjoined with domain knowledge, guarantee the satisfaction of the requirement.

概括地说，约束环境的需求无法由机器单独行动来满足。这类需求总是通过把规约与领域知识协同起来才得以满足。带有环境约束的需求，总是应当通过演示或证明"所规约的性质与领域知识合取后保证该需求得到满足"来加以验证。

In this context, Buchi automata are an easy notation to understand because they make it clear which actions are constrained. Thus it is always possible to determine syntactically whether a requirement is a specification or not and to refine it (if the environment allows) to a property that is obviously a specification. For example, in Figure 3 the requirement R constrains both ec (an environment-controlled action type) and mc (a machine-controlled action type). Provided that the domain knowledge K holds, then R can be satisfied by the specification S, which clearly constrains only the machine.

在这个语境下，Büchi 自动机是一种易于理解的记法，因为它们清楚地表明哪些动作被约束。因此，总是可以从句法上判定一条需求是不是规约，并（在环境允许的前提下）把它精化为一条显然是规约的性质。例如，在图 3 中，需求 R 同时约束 ec（一种环境控制的动作类型）与 mc（一种机器控制的动作类型）。只要领域知识 K 成立，那么 R 就可以由规约 S 来满足，而 S 显然只约束机器。

Unfortunately, in other formalisms it may not be so clear what is being constrained by a requirement. In these situations it may be more difficult to determine when the requirements phase is over or to be sure that the specification alone contains everything that the implementors need.

遗憾的是，在其他形式体系中，一条需求究竟约束了什么可能并不那么清楚。在这些情形下，判定需求阶段何时结束、或者确信规约本身已包含实现者所需的一切，都会更加困难。

5.2 Unshared Information

5.2 非共享信息

Some requirements are not directly implementable because they are stated in terms of unshared phenomena. In the banking environment (Section 3.2), for example, there is a requirement (a withdrawal request leads to a withdrawal payout, provided that the requested amount does not exceed the current balance) stated in terms of the account balance. The only shared phenomena in this example are the action types deposit, withdrawal-request, and withdrawal-payout. Since the balance state component (a legal debt) exists in the environment and is not shared with the machine, the machine cannot directly implement this requirement.

有些需求不能直接实现，因为它们是用非共享现象来陈述的。例如在银行环境中（第 3.2 节），有一条用账户余额来陈述的需求（一次取款请求导致一次取款支付，前提是所请求的金额不超过当前余额）。该例中唯一的共享现象是动作类型 deposit、withdrawal-request 与 withdrawal-payout。由于 balance 这一状态分量（一笔法律债务）存在于环境之中且不与机器共享，机器无法直接实现这条需求。

Requirements with unshared information are refined using domain knowledge relating unshared information to shared information. For example, the banking requirement can be refined using the indicative property also given in Section 3.2, which states an equivalence between the balance and an expression on deposit and withdrawal-payout actions. The following is a version of the requirement with the balance replaced by its equivalent action expression. It is implementable (it uses nothing but shared phenomena) and is therefore a specification.

带有非共享信息的需求，要借助"把非共享信息与共享信息关联起来"的领域知识来精化。例如，银行需求可以用第 3.2 节同样给出的那条陈述式性质来精化，该性质陈述了余额与一个关于 deposit 与 withdrawal-payout 动作的表达式之间的等价关系。下面是把余额替换为其等价动作表达式后的需求版本。它是可实现的（除共享现象外它不使用任何东西），因此是一条规约。

$$\begin{aligned}(\forall\, a,\, m,\, p \;\mid\; & \textit{withdrawal-request}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{ends}(a,p) \wedge m \leq \\ & ((+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \mathit{deposit}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m) - \\ & \;\;(+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m)) : \\ & (\exists\, a' \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a',m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,a')))\end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned}(\forall\, a,\, m,\, p \;\mid\; & \textit{withdrawal-request}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{ends}(a,p) \wedge m \leq \\ & ((+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \mathit{deposit}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m) - \\ & \;\;(+m \mid (\exists\, a \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a,m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,p)) : m)) : \\ & (\exists\, a' \mid :\; \textit{withdrawal-payout}(a',m) \wedge \mathit{earlier}(a,a')))\end{aligned}$$

In the presence of the indicative equivalence between the balance and the action expression, it can be proved that the above property satisfies the original requirement.

在余额与该动作表达式之间的陈述式等价成立的前提下，可以证明上述性质满足原始需求。

Another way to achieve the same effect would be to define a new term expected-balance as the sum of the amounts of all previous deposits, minus the sum of the amounts of all previous withdrawal payouts. If a requirement referring to balance is rewritten as referring to expected-balance, then the rewritten form is a specification. In the presence of the definition of expected-balance, it is directly implementable. In the presence of information about balance, it provably satisfies the original requirement.

达到同样效果的另一种方式，是定义一个新术语 expected-balance（预期余额），它等于此前所有存款金额之和减去此前所有取款支付金额之和。如果把一条涉及 balance 的需求改写为涉及 expected-balance，那么改写后的形式就是一条规约。在 expected-balance 的定义成立的前提下，它可直接实现。在关于 balance 的信息成立的前提下，它可证明地满足原始需求。

As another example of unshared information, the requirement (L8) that the lift must arrive at a floor where people are waiting for it is not implementable. How can the machine know that people are waiting? Request and arrive actions are shared with the machine. There is already domain knowledge that (L3) the beginning of group waiting leads to a request action. Other indicative statements must say that people continue to wait until the lift arrives at their floor going in their direction, at which point they enter the lift and end group waiting. From these assumptions about human behavior, and the occurrences of requests and arrivals, the lift controller can compute where people are presumed to be waiting for the lift.

作为非共享信息的另一个例子，需求（L8）"电梯必须抵达有人等候的楼层"是不可实现的。机器如何能知道有人在等候？request 与 arrive 动作与机器共享。已有的领域知识是（L3）群体等待的开始导致一次 request 动作。其他陈述式语句必须说明：人们会持续等待，直到电梯抵达他们所在楼层并沿他们所要的方向运行；届时他们进入电梯并结束群体等待。根据这些关于人类行为的假设，以及 request 与 arrive 的实际发生情况，电梯控制器就能推算出人们被推定正在何处等候电梯。

In the warehouse example (Section 3.3), if requirements are stated in terms of quantity-in-bin, designated as the quantity actually present in the warehouse bin, then refinement is not possible. We know that expected-quantity-in-bin is not the same as quantity-in-bin, and there are no shared phenomena from which the machine can compute the actual quantity-in-bin.

在仓库示例中（第 3.3 节），如果需求以 quantity-in-bin（被指称为货位中实际存在的数量）来陈述，那么精化是不可能的。我们知道 expected-quantity-in-bin 与 quantity-in-bin 并不相同，而且不存在任何共享现象，可供机器据以计算出实际的 quantity-in-bin。

This problem must be solved by changing and elaborating the requirements. Probably the requirements will be stated in terms of expected-quantity-in-bin, which is a defined term representing the best implementable estimate of quantity-in-bin. There will probably be extra requirements for shipping failure (when a bin has unexpectedly run dry) and for periodic stock inventories to reduce the difference between quantity-in-bin and expected-quantity-in-bin. expected-quantity-in-bin will be defined in terms of shared, designated actions including ship, receive, and inventory adjustments.

这个问题必须通过变更并细化需求来解决。多半需求会以 expected-quantity-in-bin 来陈述，它是一个被定义的术语，代表对 quantity-in-bin 的最佳可实现估计。多半还会有额外的需求，用于处理发货失败（当某货位意外见底时），以及用于定期盘点存货以缩小 quantity-in-bin 与 expected-quantity-in-bin 之间的差距。expected-quantity-in-bin 将以共享的、被指称的动作来定义，包括 ship、receive 以及库存调整。

Turski [1988] has pointed out that real time often appears in specifications because of unshared information. If the beginning of a physical process is a shared action, and the ending of the process is not, then the machine can estimate the ending time from a description of the physical process including elapsed time.

Turski [1988] 曾指出，实时之所以经常出现在规约中，是因为非共享信息。如果某个物理过程的开始是一个共享动作，而该过程的结束不是，那么机器就可以根据该物理过程（含所经历时间）的描述来估计其结束时刻。

5.3 Future Reference

5.3 指涉未来

Some requirements are not directly implementable because they are stated in terms of the future. For example, consider the liveness requirement in a switching environment that, whenever the telephone user has dialed the last digit, eventually a connect-to-ringback or connect-to-busytone or connect-to-errortone action occurs. In other words, the tone must be produced when (1) a digit has been dialed and (2) no additional digits will be dialed in the future.

有些需求不能直接实现，因为它们是用未来来陈述的。例如，考虑交换环境中的这条活性需求：每当电话用户拨完最后一位数字后，最终会发生一次 connect-to-ringback、connect-to-busytone 或 connect-to-errortone 动作。换言之，必须在如下条件下产生提示音：（1）已经拨出一位数字；（2）将来不会再拨出更多数字。

Like requirements with unshared information, requirements with future reference are satisfied with the help of domain knowledge, in this case relating the future to the past. The switching requirement can be satisfied using an approximate kind of domain knowledge: if the user is going to dial another digit, he or she will almost always do so within four seconds of the last digit.

与带有非共享信息的需求类似，带有未来指涉的需求也要借助领域知识来满足，只是这里的领域知识把未来与过去关联起来。上述交换需求可以借助一种近似的领域知识来满足：如果用户还要再拨一位数字，他或她几乎总会在拨出上一位数字后的四秒之内这样做。

The resulting specification says that the system must respond after a dialed digit followed by a four-second silence. The silence enables the machine to distinguish the final digit from nonfinal ones and thus satisfy the original requirement.

由此得到的规约表明：系统必须在"拨出一位数字后接着出现四秒静默"之后作出响应。这段静默使机器能够把最后一位数字与非最后一位区分开来，从而满足原始需求。

5.4 Other Views of Requirements Refinement

5.4 关于需求精化的其他观点

Some of these ideas have appeared in the literature before, in a variety of forms. Johnson's refinements called "defining capabilities" and "removing the perfect knowledge assumption" [Johnson 1988] are addressing essentially the same gaps as environment constraint and unshared information, respectively, but they are described in very different terms and without the special emphasis on domain knowledge. Mostow's "operationalization" goals IsAchievable and IsEvaluable are also similar in purpose [Mostow 1983]. But Mostow's work focuses on automated problem solving and thus assumes—if applied to requirements engineering—that the environment is as malleable as the machine.

这些思想中的一部分，此前已以各种形式出现在文献中。Johnson 所称的"定义能力（defining capabilities）"与"去除完美知识假设（removing the perfect knowledge assumption）"这两类精化 [Johnson 1988]，处理的本质上分别与环境约束和非共享信息相同的鸿沟，但它们的表述方式很不一样，也没有特别强调领域知识。Mostow 的"操作化（operationalization）"目标 IsAchievable 与 IsEvaluable 在目的上也相似 [Mostow 1983]。但 Mostow 的工作聚焦于自动问题求解，因而（若应用于需求工程）假定环境与机器一样具有可塑性。

A recent trend in the study of requirements refinement is emphasis on various agents within the environment [Dardenne et al. 1993; Feather et al. 1991]. It is recognized that these agents often cooperate with the machine and with each other to satisfy unrefined requirements.

需求精化研究中的一个近期趋势，是强调环境内部的各种代理 [Dardenne et al. 1993; Feather et al. 1991]。人们认识到，这些代理常常与机器、以及彼此之间相互协作，以满足尚未精化的需求。

Agent-centered refinement is a special case of refinement as we have characterized it. A description of an agent in the environment is indeed domain knowledge, but not all domain knowledge takes the form of agent descriptions. Some domain knowledge simply captures static relationships in the environment.

以代理为中心的精化，是我们所刻画的精化的一个特例。对环境中某个代理的描述确实是领域知识，但并非所有领域知识都采取代理描述的形式。有些领域知识只是刻画环境中的静态关系。

For example, "indirect access" is a requirements refinement in which the machine needs some information it does not have direct access to; an agent in the environment gets it and communicates it to the machine [Feather et al. 1991]. This is the only strategy mentioned by Feather et al. for dealing with unshared information, but there are others. In the lift example, the machine needs to know where people are waiting. It does not obtain this information through the intercession of another agent. Rather, it computes it from a known, fixed relationship between past shared actions (requests and floor arrivals) and the behavior of groups of people waiting for the lift to arrive.

例如，"间接获取（indirect access）"是这样一种需求精化：机器需要某些它无法直接获取的信息，于是由环境中的某个代理取得该信息并传达给机器 [Feather et al. 1991]。这是 Feather 等人提到的唯一一种应对非共享信息的策略，但其实还有别的策略。在电梯示例中，机器需要知道人们在何处等候。它并不是通过另一个代理的居间介入来获得这一信息的。相反，它是根据"过去的共享动作（request 与楼层 arrive）"与"等候电梯的人群行为"之间一种已知的、固定的关系推算出来的。

"Soft" requirements as discussed by Yu et al. [1995] are "soft" for two reasons. They may be vague and imprecise, such as requirements that a system be "secure," "reliable," or "easy to use." Until they are precise enough to be formalized, our description of refinement is not relevant to them. Requirements are also characterized as "soft" if the domain knowledge that supports their satisfaction is not absolutely reliable. For example, the lift requirement that a waiting group is eventually served (L2) is satisfiable partly because (L3) someone in the waiting group pushes the request button. But L3 is a highly probable assumption rather than an unalterable fact. In those rare cases where the assumption is not true, the requirement will not be satisfied, even if the lift controller is perfect.

Yu 等人 [1995] 所讨论的"软（soft）"需求之所以"软"，有两个原因。它们可能含糊而不精确，例如要求系统"安全"、"可靠"或"易用"。在它们精确到足以被形式化之前，我们对精化的描述与它们无关。此外，如果支撑需求得到满足的领域知识并非绝对可靠，这类需求也被称为"软"的。例如，电梯需求"某个等待群体最终会被服务"（L2）之所以可满足，部分是因为（L3）等待群体中有人会按下请求按钮。但 L3 是一条高度可能的假设，而非不可更改的事实。在假设不成立的那些罕见情形中，即便电梯控制器完美无缺，该需求也不会被满足。

In contrast to the requirements refinements discussed above, in which domain knowledge is invoked to bridge the gap from a requirement to a specification, some requirements transformations are equivalence preserving. They restructure the available information, often for the purpose of deriving an acceptable specification, without adding to it. Since the notion of an "acceptable" specification depends on the expressive power of the chosen specification language, categorization of these transformations as requirements refinements or specification refinements is somewhat subjective. For example, unfolding of invariants and finite differencing have been used as requirements refinements by Feather [1994], but they are also used as specification refinements by several authors cited by Feather.

与上面讨论的那些需求精化（其中调用领域知识以弥合从需求到规约的鸿沟）不同，有些需求变换是保持等价的。它们对已有信息进行重构，往往是为了导出一份可接受的规约，而不向其中添加任何东西。由于"可接受"的规约这一概念取决于所选规约语言的表达能力，把这些变换归类为需求精化还是规约精化，多少带有主观性。例如，不变式展开（unfolding of invariants）与有限差分（finite differencing）被 Feather [1994] 用作需求精化，但它们也被 Feather 所引用的若干作者用作规约精化。

6. FOUR CORNERS MARK A FOUNDATION

6. 四角标定一个基础

So far this article has focused on four miscellaneous problems of requirements engineering and has suggested improved ways of understanding or managing them. But the problems are related. Their solutions fit together to define and regulate some of the primary concepts of requirements engineering. The results are summarized in this section.

到目前为止，本文聚焦于需求工程的四个零散问题，并就如何更好地理解或处理它们提出了建议。但这些问题彼此相关。它们的解法相互契合，共同界定并规约了需求工程的若干核心概念。本节对这些结果加以总结。

6.1 Terminology and Applicability

6.1 术语与适用性

Here are brief definitions of the major terms we have used:

以下是我们所使用的主要术语的简要定义：

  • The environment is the portion of the real world relevant to the software development project.
  • The machine is a computer-based machine that will be constructed and connected to the environment, as a result of the software development project.
  • A designation is an informal description of the meaning of an atomic formal term referring to the environment.
  • A definition is a formal description of an atomic term, using other defined or designated terms.
  • A statement (assertion, property) in the indicative mood describes the environment as it would be without or in spite of the machine.
  • A statement (assertion, property) in the optative mood describes the environment as we would like it to be because of the machine.
  • A shared action is an action in the environment in which the machine also participates.
  • An unshared action is an action in the environment in which the machine does not participate.
  • An environment-controlled action is an action in the environment that is controlled, performed, or initiated by the environment.
  • A machine-controlled action is an action in the environment that is not controlled, performed, or initiated by the environment. The intention is that such an action will be controlled by the machine when it is connected to the environment.
  • A requirement is an optative property, intended to express the desires of the customer concerning the software development project.
  • A statement of domain knowledge or domain assumption is an indicative property intended to be relevant to the software development project.
  • A specification is an optative property, intended to be directly implementable and to support satisfaction of the requirements.
  • 环境（environment）是真实世界中与该软件开发项目相关的那一部分。
  • 机器（machine）是一台以计算机为基础的机器，它将作为该软件开发项目的成果被构建出来并连接到环境。
  • 指称（designation）是对某个指向环境的原子形式化术语之含义的非形式化描述。
  • 定义（definition）是对某个原子术语的形式化描述，它使用其他已定义或已指称的术语。
  • 以陈述式（indicative）语气作出的语句（断言、性质）描述环境在没有机器时、或不管机器如何动作时的样子。
  • 以祈愿式（optative）语气作出的语句（断言、性质）描述我们希望环境因为机器而成为的样子。
  • 共享动作（shared action）是环境中的一个动作，机器也参与其中。
  • 非共享动作（unshared action）是环境中的一个动作，机器不参与其中。
  • 环境控制的动作（environment-controlled action）是环境中由环境控制、执行或发起的动作。
  • 机器控制的动作（machine-controlled action）是环境中不由环境控制、执行或发起的动作。其意图是：当机器连接到环境后，这类动作将由机器控制。
  • 需求（requirement）是一条祈愿式性质，用于表达客户对该软件开发项目的期望。
  • 一条领域知识（domain knowledge）或领域假设（domain assumption）的陈述，是一条被认为与该软件开发项目相关的陈述式性质。
  • 规约（specification）是一条祈愿式性质，意在直接可实现，并支撑需求得到满足。

This terminology applies to all software development projects, although projects differ in emphasis, and some projects require imaginative application of the above definitions. The point is that requirements, specifications, and domain knowledge always have the same relationship to each other.

这套术语适用于所有软件开发项目，尽管不同项目侧重点不同，有些项目还需要富有想象力地运用上述定义。要点在于：需求、规约与领域知识彼此之间的关系始终如一。

For one example, if the proposed machine is automating a currently existing manual system, then the focus is on the environment as it will be in the future (when the machine is installed), not as it is now. The indicative properties are those that will be enforced by the environment, including new manual operating procedures. The optative properties are those that will be enforced by the machine; they might be the same as the properties of the replaced manual system, but some changes are likely.

举第一个例子：如果拟建的机器是要把当前既有的人工系统自动化，那么关注点在于环境未来（机器安装之后）的样子，而非它现在的样子。陈述式性质是那些将由环境（包括新的人工操作流程）强制成立的性质。祈愿式性质是那些将由机器强制成立的性质；它们可能与被替换掉的人工系统的性质相同，但多半会有一些变化。

For another example, if the proposed machine is an augmentation of an existing ("legacy") machine, then the environment of this particular development project includes the legacy machine. The indicative properties are the properties that currently hold of the system at large (including both its manual and automated parts), and the optative properties are the desired new properties of the environment.

举第二个例子：如果拟建的机器是对某台既有（"遗留"）机器的增强，那么这个特定开发项目的环境就包含那台遗留机器。陈述式性质是当前对整个大系统（含人工部分与自动部分）成立的性质，而祈愿式性质是所期望的环境新性质。

For a final example, if the proposed machine is a new reusable procedure, then the environment of this particular development project is nothing more than the procedure interface. The indicative properties are the guaranteed constraints on the procedure inputs. The only optative property (a specification as well as a requirement, since it is directly implementable) is the desired function from inputs to outputs.

举最后一个例子：如果拟建的机器是一个新的可复用过程（procedure），那么这个特定开发项目的环境无非就是该过程的接口。陈述式性质是对过程输入的有保证的约束。唯一的祈愿式性质（它既是规约也是需求，因为它直接可实现）是所期望的从输入到输出的函数。

6.2 Notation

6.2 记法

Formal representations used in requirements engineering should obey these four rules. If a requirements language is insufficient in this regard, its users should be able to augment or supplement it as necessary.

需求工程中使用的形式化表示应当遵守以下四条规则。如果某种需求语言在这方面有所不足，其使用者应当能够按需对它加以扩充或补充。

(1) Each atomic term must be designated or defined. This means that everything said is said about the environment, since all atomic terms are grounded in the environment.

(2) Table II characterizes all relevant actions of the environment. Each action type must be identified as belonging to exactly one of the categories in Table II.

(3) Whenever necessary to express relevant properties of the environment, actions in all three categories must be constrained.

(4) Each property or assertion must be identified as a requirement, statement of domain knowledge, or specification.

For clarity and brevity, we have phrased all of our arguments in terms of action-based notations. However, the concepts apply just as well to state-based notations as to action-based notations. When a state-based notation is being used, "action" is just another name for a state change [Abadi and Lamport 1993].

为清晰与简洁起见，我们把全部论证都以基于动作的记法来表述。然而，这些概念对基于状态的记法与对基于动作的记法同样适用。当使用基于状态的记法时，"动作"不过是状态变化的另一个名字 [Abadi and Lamport 1993]。

In a state-based notation, the interface between the machine and environment is represented as shared state components, i.e., components whose values are accessible to both machine and environment. A machine-controlled (shared) component is one whose value is changed only by an action of the machine. An environment-controlled component is one whose value is changed only by an action of the environment. Environment-controlled components can be shared or unshared, i.e., inaccessible to the machine.

在基于状态的记法中，机器与环境之间的接口表示为共享状态分量，即其取值对机器与环境双方都可访问的分量。一个机器控制的（共享）分量，是指其取值仅由机器的动作改变的分量。一个环境控制的分量，是指其取值仅由环境的动作改变的分量。环境控制的分量可以是共享的，也可以是非共享的（即机器不可访问）。

The simplest state-based formulation of Rules 2 and 3 demands that all state components be identified as belonging to one of the three categories and that state components be able to appear equally freely in assertions no matter which category they belong to.

规则 2 与规则 3 最简单的基于状态的表述要求：所有状态分量都必须被标明属于三个类别之一；并且无论属于哪个类别，状态分量都能同等自由地出现在断言之中。

Unfortunately, some state components may be changed by both the machine and the environment. When these "mixed" components are present, it appears necessary to introduce explicit actions, so that the machine-controlled and environment-controlled state changes to the mixed components can be distinguished.

遗憾的是，有些状态分量可能同时被机器与环境改变。当这类"混合"分量存在时，看来就有必要引入显式的动作，以便把对混合分量的机器控制状态变化与环境控制状态变化区分开来。

For clarity and brevity, we have also used examples in which the formal descriptions are collections of distinct, isolated properties. However, the concepts apply just as well to executable notations such as Statecharts [Harel 1987] and RSML [Leveson et al. 1994].

为清晰与简洁起见，我们所用的示例中，形式化描述都是一组彼此分立、互相独立的性质。然而，这些概念对可执行记法（如 Statecharts [Harel 1987] 与 RSML [Leveson et al. 1994]）同样适用。

The reason is that executable notations and property-oriented notations are not as different, fundamentally, as they might seem. Consider a large Buchi automaton—one with many states and transitions. It might be used as an executable specification, just as a Statechart or RSML specification might be. At the same time, the results of Alpern and Schneider [1987] show that it is equivalent to a large number of separate safety ("in state S, actions of type A cannot occur") and liveness ("in state S, an action of type A or B must eventually occur") properties.

原因在于，可执行记法与面向性质的记法在根本上并不像看上去那样不同。设想一个很大的 Büchi 自动机——具有许多状态与转移。它可以被用作可执行规约，正如一份 Statechart 或 RSML 规约那样。与此同时，Alpern 与 Schneider [1987] 的结果表明，它等价于大量彼此分立的安全性性质（"在状态 S 中，类型 A 的动作不能发生"）与活性性质（"在状态 S 中，类型 A 或 B 的动作必须最终发生"）。

There is no reason whatsoever why an executable notation cannot obey the first three rules; it is only necessary to ensure that its states and actions are environment phenomena and that no category of action is discriminated against.

完全没有任何理由说可执行记法不能遵守前三条规则；只需确保它的状态与动作都是环境现象，并且没有任何一类动作受到歧视。

There may be a slight problem with distinguishing indicative and optative properties in executable specifications because, as noted above, many distinct properties are grouped together in a unified whole. This problem can be solved by putting indicative and optative properties in separate modules or by establishing conventions for determining which aspects of a single module are meant to be indicative and which optative.

在可执行规约中区分陈述式性质与祈愿式性质，可能会有一点小麻烦，因为如上所述，许多彼此分立的性质被归并进一个统一的整体。这个问题可以通过把陈述式性质与祈愿式性质放进不同模块来解决，或者通过确立约定来判定单个模块中哪些方面意在陈述式、哪些意在祈愿式。

6.3 Completion

6.3 完成

If the five following criteria are satisfied, then requirements engineering, in the strongest sense, is complete. We are guaranteed that the specification is implementable (at least in principle) without recourse to any additional information. We are also guaranteed that if the specification is implemented as a machine which is subsequently connected to the environment, then the requirements will be satisfied.

如果以下五条判据都得到满足，那么需求工程在最强的意义上就完成了。我们得到保证：规约（至少在原则上）无需借助任何额外信息即可实现。我们还得到保证：如果该规约被实现为一台机器，并随后被连接到环境，那么需求将得到满足。

(1) There is a set R of requirements. Each member of R has been validated (checked informally) as acceptable to the customer, and R as a whole has been validated as expressing all the customer's desires with respect to the software development project.

(2) There is a set K of statements of domain knowledge. Each member of K has been validated (checked informally) as true of the environment.

(3) There is a set S of specifications. The members of S do not constrain the environment; they are not stated in terms of any unshared actions or state components; and they do not refer to the future.

(4) A proof shows that $S,\; K \vdash R$. This proof ensures that an implementation of S will satisfy the requirements.

(5) There is a proof that S and K are consistent. This ensures that the specification is internally consistent and consistent with the environment. Note that the two proofs together imply that S, K, and R are consistent with each other.

（1）存在一个需求集合 R。R 的每个成员都已被验证（非形式化地核查）为客户可接受；并且 R 作为整体已被验证为表达了客户关于该软件开发项目的全部期望。

（2）存在一个领域知识陈述集合 K。K 的每个成员都已被验证（非形式化地核查）为对环境为真。

（3）存在一个规约集合 S。S 的成员不约束环境；它们不以任何非共享动作或状态分量来陈述；并且它们不指涉未来。

（4）有一个证明表明 $S,\; K \vdash R$。该证明确保对 S 的实现将满足需求。

（5）有一个证明表明 S 与 K 是一致的。这确保规约内部一致，且与环境一致。注意，两个证明合在一起蕴涵 S、K 与 R 彼此一致。

The most commonly understood use of verification is to show that an implementation satisfies a specification. The completion rules show that verification also has a role to play in requirements engineering. First, verification is necessary to establish the satisfaction result.

对验证（verification）最常见的理解，是用它表明某个实现满足某份规约。上述完成规则表明，验证在需求工程中同样有其角色。第一，要确立满足性结果，验证是必需的。

Second, some consistency checking is supported by verification. This occurs when there is some redundancy among the various parts to be shown consistent—a natural-enough occurrence in large specifications, with reuse, multiple views, etc. In the presence of redundancy, the statements made during the course of requirements engineering are not all independent, and some are implied by others. A proof that some statements imply others necessarily establishes the consistency of the consequences with the antecedents.

第二，验证还支撑一部分一致性检查。当待证一致的各个部分之间存在某种冗余时就会如此——在大型规约中，由于复用、多视图等原因，这种情况相当自然。存在冗余时，需求工程过程中作出的各条语句并非彼此独立，其中一些被另一些所蕴涵。而"某些语句蕴涵另一些语句"的证明，必然确立了后果与前提之间的一致性。
