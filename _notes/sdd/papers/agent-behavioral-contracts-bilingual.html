Agent Behavioral Contracts（智能体行为契约）：可靠自主 AI 智能体的形式化规约与运行时强制执行

Varun Pratap Bhardwaj · Accenture · arXiv:2602.22302v1 · 2026 年 2 月 25 日 · 原文链接

Abstract

摘要

Traditional software relies on contracts—APIs, type systems, assertions—to specify and enforce correct behavior. AI agents, by contrast, operate on prompts and natural language instructions with no formal behavioral specification. This gap is the root cause of drift, governance failures, and frequent project failures in agentic AI deployments. We introduce Agent Behavioral Contracts (ABC), a formal framework that brings Design-by-Contract principles to autonomous AI agents. An ABC contract $C = (P, I, G, R)$ specifies Preconditions, Invariants, Governance policies, and Recovery mechanisms as first-class, runtime-enforceable components. We define $(p, \delta, k)$-satisfaction—a probabilistic notion of contract compliance that accounts for LLM non-determinism and recovery—and prove a Drift Bounds Theorem showing that contracts with recovery rate $\gamma > \alpha$ (the natural drift rate) bound behavioral drift to $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$ in expectation, with Gaussian concentration in the stochastic setting. We establish sufficient conditions for safe contract composition in multi-agent chains and derive probabilistic degradation bounds. We implement ABC in AgentAssert, a runtime enforcement library, and evaluate on AgentContract-Bench, a benchmark of 200 scenarios across 7 models from 6 vendors. Results across 1,980 sessions show that contracted agents detect 5.2–6.8 soft violations per session that uncontracted baselines miss entirely ($p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $d = 6.7$–$33.8$), achieve 88–100% hard constraint compliance, and bound behavioral drift to $D^* < 0.27$ across extended sessions, with 100% recovery for frontier models and 17–100% across all models, at overhead $< 10$ ms per action.

传统软件依赖契约——API、类型系统、断言——来规约（specification）并强制执行正确行为。而 AI 智能体截然不同：它们依靠提示词（prompt）和自然语言指令运作，没有任何形式化的行为规约。这一鸿沟是 agentic AI 部署中漂移（drift）、治理失效和项目频繁失败的根本原因。我们提出 Agent Behavioral Contracts（ABC，智能体行为契约），这是一个将"按契约设计"（Design-by-Contract）原则引入自主 AI 智能体的形式化框架。一个 ABC 契约 $C = (P, I, G, R)$ 将前置条件（Preconditions）、不变量（Invariants）、治理策略（Governance policies）和恢复机制（Recovery mechanisms）规定为一等的、运行时可强制执行的组件。我们定义了 $(p, \delta, k)$-满足性——一种用于度量契约合规的概率性概念，同时考虑 LLM 的不确定性与恢复能力——并证明了漂移界定定理：对于恢复率 $\gamma > \alpha$（自然漂移率）的契约，行为漂移在期望意义下被界定在 $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$，在随机设定下具有高斯集中性。我们建立了多智能体链中安全契约组合的充分条件，并推导出概率性退化界。我们在 AgentAssert（一个运行时强制执行库）中实现了 ABC，并在 AgentContract-Bench（一个涵盖 6 家厂商 7 个模型、共 200 个场景的基准）上进行了评估。在 1,980 个会话中，结果表明：有契约的智能体每会话检测到 5.2–6.8 个无契约基线完全漏报的软违规（$p < 0.0001$，Cohen's $d = 6.7$–$33.8$），实现 88–100% 的硬约束合规率，在扩展会话中将行为漂移界定在 $D^* < 0.27$，前沿模型恢复率达 100%，全部模型恢复率在 17–100% 之间，每次动作的执行开销 $< 10$ ms。

1 Introduction

1 引言

The deployment of autonomous AI agents in production environments is accelerating at an unprecedented pace. Agents powered by large language models (LLMs) now execute multi-step workflows in financial advisory [1], healthcare triage, customer support [2], code generation [3], and research synthesis [4]. These systems are no longer simple question-answering interfaces: they invoke tools, access databases, make decisions with real-world consequences, and increasingly operate in multi-agent pipelines where outputs of one agent feed directly into another [5, 6]. Yet despite this rapid adoption, agents operate without formal behavioral guarantees. There exists no widely adopted mechanism to specify what an agent should do, verify that it is doing it, or enforce corrective action when it deviates.

自主 AI 智能体在生产环境中的部署正以前所未有的速度加速推进。由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的智能体现已在金融咨询 [1]、医疗分诊、客户支持 [2]、代码生成 [3] 和研究综合 [4] 等领域执行多步骤工作流。这些系统不再是简单的问答接口：它们调用工具、访问数据库、做出具有现实影响的决策，并日益在多智能体流水线中运行——一个智能体的输出直接输入另一个智能体 [5, 6]。然而，尽管采用步伐如此迅猛，智能体的运行依然缺乏形式化的行为保障。目前尚无被广泛采用的机制来规定智能体应该做什么、验证它是否正在执行，或在偏离时强制实施纠正措施。

The Problem

问题所在

Traditional software systems benefit from decades of formal specification tooling: type systems, API contracts, assertions, and interface specifications provide compile-time and runtime guarantees about program behavior [7, 8]. AI agents, by contrast, are governed by prompts—natural language instructions that carry no formal semantics, no verifiable guarantees, and no enforcement mechanisms. This gap between the formality of traditional software contracts and the informality of agent instructions is the root cause of a class of failures unique to agentic AI: behavioral drift, governance violations, and silent degradation.

传统软件系统受益于数十年的形式化规约工具积累：类型系统、API 契约、断言和接口规约提供了关于程序行为的编译期和运行时保证 [7, 8]。而 AI 智能体由提示词驱动——这些自然语言指令不携带形式化语义、不提供可验证保证，也不具备任何执行机制。传统软件契约的形式化程度与智能体指令的非正式性之间的这种鸿沟，是 agentic AI 特有的一类故障的根本原因：行为漂移、治理违规和无声降级。

Behavioral drift manifests when an agent's actions gradually diverge from its intended specification over the course of a multi-turn interaction [9]. An agent tasked with professional customer support may begin with appropriate responses but progressively adopt a more casual tone, hallucinate product features, or volunteer information it was instructed to withhold. A research synthesis agent may start by citing verified sources but drift toward fabricated references as the session extends. These deviations are subtle, incremental, and—critically—undetected until harm has occurred: a customer receives incorrect medical guidance, a financial agent exceeds its trading authority, or a code generation agent introduces a security vulnerability.

行为漂移表现为：在多轮交互中，智能体的行为逐渐偏离其预期规约 [9]。一个被要求提供专业客户支持的智能体，可能一开始表现正常，但逐渐采用更随意的语气，虚构产品功能，或主动披露被要求保密的信息。一个研究综合智能体可能起初引用经验证的来源，但随着会话延长而漂移到捏造的参考文献。这些偏离是微妙的、渐进的，而且——至关重要地——直到危害已经发生才被发现：客户收到错误的医疗指导，金融智能体超出其交易授权，或代码生成智能体引入安全漏洞。

Several important approaches address adjacent aspects of this problem. Constitutional AI [10] embeds behavioral principles during training, producing models that are more aligned at generation time. Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) [11] fine-tunes models toward human preferences. Output guardrails such as NeMo Guardrails [12] filter or redirect agent responses that match prohibited patterns. However, none of these provides formal runtime behavioral contracts with mathematical guarantees. Constitutional AI operates at training time and cannot adapt to deployment-specific constraints. RLHF shapes general tendencies but cannot enforce specific invariants. Guardrails filter outputs but do not specify preconditions, do not monitor invariants over time, and do not compose across multi-agent pipelines. Recent empirical work confirms this gap: Cartagena and Teixeira [13] demonstrate that text-level safety alignment does not transfer to tool-call safety, validating that prompt-level governance contracts are fundamentally insufficient for agents that interact with the world through tools and APIs.

若干重要方法解决了这一问题的相邻方面。Constitutional AI [10] 在训练期间嵌入行为原则，使模型在生成时更加对齐。强化学习人类反馈（RLHF）[11] 根据人类偏好数据微调模型。NeMo Guardrails [12] 等输出护栏对匹配禁止模式的智能体响应进行过滤或重定向。然而，这些方法均未能提供具有数学保证的形式化运行时行为契约。Constitutional AI 在训练时运作，无法适应特定部署的约束。RLHF 塑造一般倾向，但无法强制执行具体不变量。护栏过滤输出，但不规定前置条件、不随时间监控不变量，也不能跨多智能体流水线进行组合。近期实证工作证实了这一鸿沟：Cartagena 和 Teixeira [13] 证明文本级安全对齐不能迁移到工具调用安全，这验证了提示词级治理契约对于通过工具和 API 与世界交互的智能体而言根本不足。

The theoretical case for active enforcement is further strengthened by impossibility results. Wang et al. [14] prove a self-evolution trilemma: in self-evolving AI societies, continuous self-evolution, complete isolation from external correction, and safety invariance cannot coexist. This result implies that passive safety—relying on training-time alignment alone—is provably insufficient for agents that evolve their behavior over extended interactions. Active, runtime enforcement of behavioral specifications is not merely desirable; it is a theoretical necessity.

不可能性结果进一步从理论上强化了主动执行的必要性。Wang 等人 [14] 证明了自我演化三难困境：在自我演化的 AI 社会中，持续自我演化、完全隔绝外部纠正和安全不变性三者不能共存。这一结果意味着，被动安全——仅依赖训练时对齐——对于在长期交互中演化行为的智能体而言被证明是不够的。行为规约的主动运行时强制执行不仅是理想的，更是理论上的必要条件。

Our Contribution

我们的贡献

We introduce Agent Behavioral Contracts (ABC), a formal framework that brings Design-by-Contract [8] principles to autonomous AI agents. Our contributions are:

  1. We define the ABC contract structure $C = (P, I, G, R)$, formalizing agent behavioral expectations as a tuple of Preconditions, Invariants (hard and soft), Governance policies (hard and soft), and Recovery mechanisms (Section 3).
  2. We introduce $(p, \delta, k)$-satisfaction, a probabilistic contract compliance framework that accounts for LLM non-determinism: contracts hold with probability at least $p$, deviations remain within tolerance $\delta$, and recovery occurs within $k$ steps (Section 3).
  3. 2 Background and Related Work

2 背景与相关工作

The ABC framework draws on and extends several established research traditions: Design-by-Contract in software engineering, contract theory for cyber-physical systems, runtime monitoring and verification, and the rapidly evolving landscape of AI agent safety. We survey each in turn, positioning ABC relative to the state of the art.

ABC 框架借鉴并扩展了若干成熟的研究传统：软件工程中的按契约设计、信息物理系统的契约理论、运行时监控与验证，以及 AI 智能体安全这一快速演进的领域。我们逐一综述，并将 ABC 定位于研究前沿。

2.1 Design by Contract

2.1 按契约设计

The Design-by-Contract (DbC) paradigm, introduced by Meyer [8] and elaborated in Meyer [15], formalizes the obligations between software components as preconditions, postconditions, and class invariants. DbC has been operationalized in specification languages such as JML for Java [16] and Spec# for C# [17], enabling static and runtime verification of contractual obligations in traditional software.

按契约设计（DbC）范式由 Meyer [8] 提出并在 Meyer [15] 中详细阐述，将软件组件间的义务形式化为前置条件、后置条件和类不变量。DbC 已在 Java 的 JML [16] 和 C# 的 Spec# [17] 等规约语言中得到实现，使传统软件中契约义务的静态与运行时验证成为可能。

The extension of DbC to neural and neurosymbolic systems is recent. Leoveanu-Condrei [18] propose a neurosymbolic contract layer for trustworthy agent design, defining preconditions and postconditions over individual LLM calls. This work is the closest conceptual predecessor to ABC in the DbC tradition. However, it is limited to single LLM invocations—it does not address multi-turn behavioral drift, multi-agent composition, soft constraint recovery, or runtime governance enforcement over extended sessions. ABC generalizes the DbC paradigm from individual function calls to autonomous agent sessions, introducing invariants that must hold across time, governance constraints over actions, recovery mechanisms for soft violations, and a compositionality theorem for multi-agent chains.

将 DbC 扩展到神经和神经符号系统的研究较为近期。Leoveanu-Condrei [18] 为可信智能体设计提出了一个神经符号契约层，在单个 LLM 调用上定义前置条件和后置条件。这一工作是 DbC 传统中与 ABC 在概念上最接近的前驱。然而，它仅限于单次 LLM 调用——不解决多轮行为漂移、多智能体组合、软约束恢复或扩展会话中的运行时治理强制执行。ABC 将 DbC 范式从单个函数调用推广至自主智能体会话，引入了须随时间保持的不变量、针对行动的治理约束、软违规的恢复机制，以及多智能体链的可组合性定理。

2.2 Contract Theory for Cyber-Physical Systems

2.2 信息物理系统的契约理论

Contract-based design has a rich history in cyber-physical systems (CPS). The meta-theory of Benveniste et al. [19] provides a unifying algebraic framework for assume-guarantee contracts, establishing composition operators, refinement relations, and compatibility conditions across heterogeneous component models. Assume-guarantee reasoning [20] decomposes system-level verification into per-component obligations, a principle that ABC extends to multi-agent AI pipelines through its compositionality theorem (Theorem 4.9).

基于契约的设计在信息物理系统（CPS）领域有着丰富的历史。Benveniste 等人 [19] 的元理论为假设-保证契约提供了统一的代数框架，在异构组件模型之间建立了组合算子、精化关系和兼容性条件。假设-保证推理 [20] 将系统级验证分解为各组件义务，ABC 通过其可组合性定理（定理 4.9）将这一原则扩展到多智能体 AI 流水线。

In the stochastic setting, Li et al. [21] develop stochastic assume-guarantee contracts for CPS under probabilistic requirements, and Hampus and Nyberg [22] extend probabilistic contracts to cyber-physical architectures. These works establish the theoretical foundations for reasoning about contracts in the presence of uncertainty—a necessity shared by AI agents, whose outputs are inherently non-deterministic.

在随机设定下，Li 等人 [21] 为概率需求下的 CPS 开发了随机假设-保证契约，Hampus 和 Nyberg [22] 将概率契约扩展到信息物理架构。这些工作为在不确定性下推理契约奠定了理论基础——这也是 AI 智能体（其输出本质上是不确定性的）的共同需求。

Most recently, Ye and Tan [23] introduce "Agent Contracts" for resource-bounded autonomous AI systems. Their framework formalizes resource governance: multi-dimensional constraints on token consumption, execution time, cost budgets, and delegation hierarchies, with conservation laws ensuring delegated budgets respect parent constraints. The ABC framework is complementary: whereas Ye and Tan [23] govern how much an agent may consume (resource contracts), ABC governs how an agent must behave (behavioral contracts)—specifying preconditions, invariants, drift bounds, and recovery mechanisms over the agent's actions and outputs. The two frameworks address orthogonal concerns and could be composed: resource contracts bounding computation, behavioral contracts bounding behavior.

最近，Ye 和 Tan [23] 为资源受限的自主 AI 系统引入了"智能体契约"。他们的框架形式化了资源治理：对 token 消耗、执行时间、成本预算和委托层级的多维约束，以及确保委托预算不超过父级约束的守恒律。ABC 框架与之互补：Ye 和 Tan [23] 治理智能体可以消耗多少（资源契约），ABC 治理智能体必须如何行为（行为契约）——规约智能体行动和输出的前置条件、不变量、漂移界和恢复机制。两个框架解决正交关切，可以组合：资源契约约束计算，行为契约约束行为。

ABC extends the CPS contract tradition to autonomous AI agents. The key technical differences are: (i) the state space in CPS contracts is typically continuous and governed by physical dynamics, whereas agent state spaces encompass natural language context, tool invocation history, and semantic content; (ii) CPS contracts assume well-characterized noise models (e.g., Gaussian sensor noise), whereas LLM non-determinism arises from discrete token sampling, temperature scaling, and context window effects; and (iii) CPS contracts do not address behavioral drift—a phenomenon specific to autoregressive models operating over extended horizons. The $(p, \delta, k)$-satisfaction framework (Definition 3.7) bridges this gap by defining probabilistic guarantees tailored to the recovery-centric nature of LLM agent behavior.

ABC 将 CPS 契约传统扩展到自主 AI 智能体。主要技术差异在于：（i）CPS 契约中的状态空间通常是连续的、由物理动态支配的，而智能体状态空间涵盖自然语言上下文、工具调用历史和语义内容；（ii）CPS 契约假设有良好刻画的噪声模型（如高斯传感器噪声），而 LLM 的不确定性来自离散 token 采样、温度缩放和上下文窗口效应；（iii）CPS 契约不涉及行为漂移——这是自回归模型在长期任务视野上特有的现象。$(p, \delta, k)$-满足性框架（定义 3.7）通过定义针对 LLM 智能体行为以恢复为中心的概率性保证来弥合这一鸿沟。

2.3 Runtime Monitoring and Verification

2.3 运行时监控与验证

Runtime verification (RV) monitors system executions against formal specifications, typically expressed in temporal logic [24]. Bauer et al. [25] develop efficient online monitoring algorithms for linear temporal logic (LTL) and timed LTL properties, enabling real-time verification of safety and liveness requirements. These techniques provide the theoretical underpinning for ABC's runtime enforcement loop, which evaluates contract predicates at each agent action.

运行时验证（RV）对照通常以时序逻辑表达的形式规约来监控系统执行 [24]。Bauer 等人 [25] 为线性时序逻辑（LTL）和时间 LTL 性质开发了高效的在线监控算法，实现对安全性和活性需求的实时验证。这些技术为 ABC 运行时强制执行循环提供了理论基础，该循环在每个智能体动作处评估契约谓词。

In the reinforcement learning setting, Alshiekh et al. [26] introduce shielding—synthesizing a reactive system (a "shield") from temporal logic specifications that intercepts unsafe actions before they are executed. Shielding provides strong safety guarantees while preserving the convergence properties of the underlying learning algorithm. However, shielding assumes a formal environment model from which the shield can be synthesized, a requirement that is infeasible for LLM agents operating in open-ended natural language environments. ABC achieves analogous runtime enforcement using declarative behavioral contracts evaluated over runtime observations, without requiring a synthesized environment model.

在强化学习设定中，Alshiekh 等人 [26] 引入了屏蔽（shielding）——从时序逻辑规约合成一个响应式系统（"盾牌"），在不安全动作被执行前进行拦截。屏蔽在保留底层学习算法收敛性质的同时提供了强安全保证。然而，屏蔽假设存在可以合成盾牌的形式化环境模型，这对于在开放式自然语言环境中运行的 LLM 智能体来说是不可行的。ABC 使用对运行时观测进行评估的声明式行为契约来实现类似的运行时强制执行，无需合成环境模型。

Two recent systems apply formal verification ideas to LLM agents. VeriGuard [27] combines offline formal verification of a behavioral policy with online monitoring during execution, providing safety guarantees through a dual-stage architecture. StepShield [28] introduces a benchmark for temporal detection of agent violations, measuring not merely whether violations are detected but when—introducing metrics such as Early Intervention Rate and Intervention Gap that quantify the timeliness of enforcement.

两个近期系统将形式化验证思想应用于 LLM 智能体：VeriGuard [27] 将行为策略的离线形式化验证与执行期间的在线监控相结合，通过双阶段架构提供安全保证。StepShield [28] 引入了智能体违规时序检测基准，不仅衡量是否检测到违规，还衡量何时检测到——引入了早期干预率（EIR）和干预间隔（IG）等指标来量化强制执行的及时性。

ABC differs from these approaches in two respects. First, ABC's contract structure is specification-first: contracts are defined declaratively via ContractSpec before deployment, rather than inferred from verification of generated code (VeriGuard) or evaluated post-hoc from execution traces (StepShield). Second, ABC integrates behavioral drift detection as a leading indicator (Remark 3.16), enabling preemptive intervention before constraint violations materialize—a capability absent from both VeriGuard and StepShield.

ABC 在两方面与这些方法有所不同：首先，ABC 的契约结构是规约优先的：契约在部署前通过 ContractSpec 声明式定义，而不是从生成代码的验证（VeriGuard）或执行轨迹的事后评估（StepShield）中推断。其次，ABC 将行为漂移检测作为领先指标整合（注释 3.16），在约束违规实际发生前实现预防性干预——这是 VeriGuard 和 StepShield 都缺乏的能力。

Definition 3.17 (Diagnostic Decomposition Vector). The diagnostic decomposition vector at step $t$ is:

$$\vec{D}(t) = \bigl(D_P(t),\; D_I(t),\; D_G(t),\; D_{\text{distributional}}(t)\bigr),$$
(10)

where $D_P(t)$, $D_I(t)$, and $D_G(t)$ are the compliance gaps restricted to precondition-derived, invariant, and governance constraints, respectively. This vector enables operators to pinpoint whether drift originates from precondition failures, invariant violations, governance breaches, or distributional shift.

定义 3.17（诊断分解向量）。 步骤 $t$ 处的诊断分解向量为：

$$\vec{D}(t) = \bigl(D_P(t),\; D_I(t),\; D_G(t),\; D_{\text{分布}}(t)\bigr),$$
(10)

其中 $D_P(t)$、$D_I(t)$ 和 $D_G(t)$ 分别是受限于前置条件、不变量和治理约束的合规差距。该向量使操作人员能够精确定位漂移源自前置条件失效、不变量违规、治理违规还是分布漂移。

2.4 AI Agent Safety and Governance

2.4 AI 智能体安全与治理

The safety of LLM-based agents has attracted intense research attention, producing a diverse landscape of approaches that we organize by methodology.

基于 LLM 的智能体安全已吸引大量研究关注，形成了多样化的方法格局，我们按方法论进行分类介绍。

Training-time alignment. Constitutional AI [10] trains models to adhere to a set of behavioral principles through self-critique and revision, producing outputs that are more aligned with human values. RLHF [11] fine-tunes models using human preference data to improve instruction-following and reduce harmful outputs. These approaches are complementary to ABC: they improve the baseline behavior of the underlying model, reducing the frequency of contract violations, but they cannot enforce deployment-specific constraints, adapt to novel operational requirements, or provide formal compliance guarantees at runtime.

训练时对齐。Constitutional AI [10] 通过自我批评和修正来训练模型遵守一套行为原则，产生更符合人类价值观的输出。RLHF [11] 使用人类偏好数据微调模型以改善指令遵循并减少有害输出。这些方法与 ABC 互补：它们改善底层模型的基线行为，降低契约违规的频率，但无法强制执行特定部署的约束、适应新的操作需求，或在运行时提供形式化合规保证。

Output filtering and guardrails. NeMo Guardrails [12] provides a programmable framework for constraining LLM application behavior through topical rails, safety rails, and dialog management. Guardrails AI [29] is the most widely deployed open-source LLM output validation library, providing validators for structured output, PII detection, toxicity filtering, and hallucination checks on individual LLM responses. While effective for per-response output filtering, both NeMo Guardrails and Guardrails AI operate on individual responses without maintaining state across turns, do not specify session-level preconditions or invariants, do not detect behavioral drift over multi-turn interactions, and do not provide formal compliance guarantees or recovery mechanisms. ABC operates at a fundamentally different granularity: session-level behavioral contracts rather than per-response output validation.

输出过滤与护栏。NeMo Guardrails [12] 为通过话题轨道、安全轨道和对话管理约束 LLM 应用行为提供了可编程框架。Guardrails AI [29] 是部署最广泛的开源 LLM 输出验证库，为单个 LLM 响应提供结构化输出、PII 检测、毒性过滤和幻觉检查的验证器。尽管对单响应输出过滤有效，NeMo Guardrails 和 Guardrails AI 均在单个响应层面运作，不跨轮次维护状态，不规定会话级前置条件或不变量，不检测多轮交互中的行为漂移，也不提供形式化合规保证或恢复机制。ABC 在根本上不同的粒度运作：会话级行为契约而非每响应输出验证。

Specification-based enforcement. The most directly comparable works to ABC are specification-based systems that define and enforce behavioral rules for agents.

基于规约的强制执行。与 ABC 最直接可比的工作是为智能体定义和强制执行行为规则的基于规约的系统。

AgentSpec [30], accepted at ICSE 2026, introduces a customizable runtime enforcement framework with a rule-based DSL for specifying safety properties of LLM agents. AgentSpec supports both preventive and corrective enforcement modes and evaluates on WebArena and ToolEmu benchmarks. However, AgentSpec does not provide probabilistic compliance guarantees (it treats constraints as deterministic rules), does not model or detect behavioral drift, and does not establish compositionality conditions for multi-agent systems.

AgentSpec [30]（已被 ICSE 2026 录用）引入了一个可定制的运行时强制执行框架，带有用于规约 LLM 智能体安全性质的基于规则 DSL。AgentSpec 支持预防性和纠正性两种强制执行模式。然而，AgentSpec 不提供概率性合规保证（将约束视为确定性规则），不建模或检测行为漂移，也不为多智能体系统建立可组合性条件。

Pro2Guard [31] extends the runtime enforcement paradigm with probabilistic model checking via discrete-time Markov chains (DTMCs). By learning transition probabilities from execution traces, Pro2Guard enables proactive enforcement that anticipates likely violations. This is the closest methodological competitor to ABC: both frameworks reason probabilistically about agent behavior. The key distinction is that Pro2Guard is reactive—it learns its probabilistic model from observed traces and refines enforcement accordingly—whereas ABC is proactive—behavioral expectations are specified as contracts before deployment, with probabilistic guarantees derived from the contract structure itself. Additionally, Pro2Guard does not provide a contract DSL, a compositionality theorem, or a behavioral drift metric.

Pro2Guard [31] 通过离散时间马尔可夫链（DTMC）的概率模型检验扩展了运行时强制执行范式。通过从执行轨迹中学习转移概率，Pro2Guard 实现了预测可能违规的前瞻性强制执行。这是与 ABC 方法论上最接近的竞争者：两个框架都对智能体行为进行概率推理。关键区别在于 Pro2Guard 是响应式的——从观测轨迹中学习概率模型——而 ABC 是前瞻性的——行为期望在部署前作为契约规约，概率保证从契约结构本身推导。此外，Pro2Guard 不提供契约 DSL、可组合性定理或行为漂移指标。

Agent-C [32] defines a DSL for temporal safety constraints and uses SMT solving to enforce compliance during generation. By integrating constraint checking into the decoding process, Agent-C achieves high conformance rates on benchmarks requiring temporal ordering of actions. ABC differs in scope and mechanism: whereas Agent-C focuses on temporal ordering constraints enforced at generation time, ABC specifies behavioral contracts encompassing preconditions, invariants, governance, and recovery, enforced at runtime across entire sessions. Agent-C does not address probabilistic satisfaction, compositionality, or behavioral drift.

Agent-C [32] 为时序安全约束定义了一种 DSL，并使用 SMT 求解在生成过程中强制合规。通过将约束检查集成到解码过程中，Agent-C 在需要动作时序排序的基准上实现了高合规率。ABC 在范围和机制上有所不同：Agent-C 关注在生成时强制执行的时序排序约束，而 ABC 规约涵盖前置条件、不变量、治理和恢复的行为契约，在整个会话的运行时强制执行。Agent-C 不涉及概率满足性、可组合性或行为漂移。

Incident response and governance frameworks. Xiao et al. [33] introduce AIR, a domain-specific language for managing incident response in LLM agents, supporting detection, containment, recovery, and eradication of safety incidents. AIR achieves >90% success rates across its incident lifecycle. The distinction from ABC is one of orientation: AIR is reactive, responding to incidents after they occur; ABC is proactive, specifying contracts that prevent violations or bound their impact a priori. The two approaches are complementary—AIR could serve as the escalation layer when ABC recovery mechanisms are exhausted.

事件响应与治理框架。Xiao 等人 [33] 引入了 AIR，一种用于管理 LLM 智能体中事件响应的领域特定语言，支持检测、遏制、恢复和根除安全事件，在整个事件生命周期中实现 >90% 的成功率。AIR 与 ABC 的区别在于方向：AIR 是被动的，在事件发生后响应；ABC 是主动的，通过规约契约防止违规或先验地限制其影响。两种方法互补——AIR 可以作为 ABC 恢复机制耗尽时的升级层。

AGENTSAFE [34] proposes a unified governance framework spanning design-time, runtime, and audit controls for agentic AI, including anomaly detection and interruptibility mechanisms. POLARIS [1] introduces governed orchestration for enterprise workflows with typed planning and validator-gated execution. Both frameworks operate at a higher level of abstraction than ABC, providing governance architecture rather than formal behavioral contracts with mathematical guarantees.

AGENTSAFE [34] 提出了一个涵盖 agentic AI 设计时、运行时和审计控制的统一治理框架，包括异常检测和可中断性机制。POLARIS [1] 为企业工作流引入了带有类型化规划和验证器门控执行的受治理编排。两个框架都在比 ABC 更高的抽象层次上运作，提供治理架构而非具有数学保证的形式化行为契约。

2.5 Agent Behavioral Drift

2.5 智能体行为漂移

Their work establishes that drift is a real and quantifiable problem; ABC provides the formal machinery to prevent it.

AI 智能体的行为漂移——智能体行为在长期交互中相对于预期规约的渐进性偏离——最近已成为一个被认可的现象。Rath [9] 提供了首个系统性研究，引入了智能体稳定性指数（ASI），并证明多智能体 LLM 系统在长期交互中表现出可量化的行为降级。这一工作确立了漂移是一个真实且可量化的问题；ABC 提供了防止漂移的形式化机制。

The concept of drift in machine learning more broadly is well-studied under the umbrella of concept drift [35], which addresses changes in the underlying data distribution over time. ABC's behavioral drift score $D(t)$ (Definition 3.12) adapts the concept drift framework to the agent setting by combining a compliance-gap component (a lagging indicator of constraint violations) with a Jensen–Shannon divergence component (a leading indicator of distributional shift in the agent's action space).

更广泛的机器学习中的漂移概念在概念漂移 [35] 的框架下已有深入研究，涉及底层数据分布随时间的变化。ABC 的行为漂移评分 $D(t)$（定义 3.12）通过将合规性差距组件（约束违规的滞后指标）与 Jensen–Shannon 散度组件（智能体行动空间中分布偏移的领先指标）相结合，将概念漂移框架适配到智能体设定。

3 The ABC Framework

3 ABC 框架

We now present the formal foundations of Agent Behavioral Contracts (ABC). The framework introduces a contract structure that distinguishes hard constraints (which must never be violated) from soft constraints (which admit transient violations provided recovery occurs within a bounded window). This distinction is motivated by the non-deterministic nature of large language model outputs: demanding perfect compliance at every step is both impractical and unnecessary when effective recovery mechanisms exist.

我们现在介绍 Agent Behavioral Contracts（ABC）的形式化基础。该框架引入了一种契约结构，将硬约束（绝不能违反）与软约束（在有界窗口内允许暂时违反，前提是发生恢复）区分开来。这种区分的动机在于大语言模型输出的不确定性：在有效恢复机制存在的情况下，要求每一步都完全合规既不实际也没有必要。

3.1 Contract Structure

3.1 契约结构

Definition 3.1 (Agent Behavioral Contract). An Agent Behavioral Contract is a tuple $C = (P, I_{hard}, I_{soft}, G_{hard}, G_{soft}, R)$, where:

  1. $P = \{p_1, \ldots, p_m\}$ is a finite set of preconditions: predicates over the initial state $s_0$ that must hold before the agent begins execution.
  2. $I_{hard}$ is a finite set of hard invariants: predicates over states that must hold at every step of execution. Hard invariants encode safety-critical properties such as "no personally identifiable information is emitted" or "data access is restricted to authorized sources." A single violation of any hard invariant constitutes a contract breach.
  3. $I_{soft}$ is a finite set of soft invariants: predicates over states that may be transiently violated provided recovery occurs within a bounded window. Soft invariants encode desirable-but-recoverable properties such as "response maintains professional tone" or "confidence scores exceed threshold $\theta$."
  4. $G_{hard}$ is a finite set of hard governance constraints: predicates over actions that must hold for every action the agent takes. These encode zero-tolerance operational bounds such as spending limits, prohibited tool invocations, or forbidden output categories.
  5. $G_{soft}$ is a finite set of soft governance constraints: predicates over actions that admit transient violations with recovery. Examples include token budget warnings, response latency thresholds, and soft timeout advisories.
  6. $\mathcal{R}: (\mathcal{I}_{soft} \cup \mathcal{G}_{soft}) \times S \rightarrow \mathcal{A}^*$ is a recovery mechanism: a partial mapping from a violated soft constraint and the current state to a finite sequence of corrective actions. When $\mathcal{R}(c, s)$ is defined, its length is at most $k_{max}$. When $\mathcal{R}(c, s)$ is undefined—the monitor emits a RECOVERYFAILED event and defers to external intervention (human operator or orchestrator).

定义 3.1（智能体行为契约）。 一个智能体行为契约是一个元组 $C = (P, I_{hard}, I_{soft}, G_{hard}, G_{soft}, R)$，其中：

  1. $P = \{p_1, \ldots, p_m\}$ 是有限的前置条件集合：关于初始状态 $s_0$ 的谓词，在智能体开始执行前必须成立。
  2. $I_{hard}$ 是有限的硬不变量集合：关于状态的谓词，必须在执行的每一步都成立。硬不变量编码安全关键性质，如"不得泄露个人身份信息"或"数据访问仅限于授权来源"。任何硬不变量的单次违反即构成契约违约。
  3. $I_{soft}$ 是有限的软不变量集合：关于状态的谓词，允许暂时违反，前提是在有界窗口内发生恢复。软不变量编码理想但可恢复的性质，如"响应保持专业语气"或"置信度分数超过阈值 $\theta$"。
  4. $G_{hard}$ 是有限的硬治理约束集合：关于动作的谓词，智能体的每个动作都必须满足。这些约束编码零容忍的操作边界，如支出限额、禁止的工具调用或禁止的输出类别。
  5. $G_{soft}$ 是有限的软治理约束集合：关于动作的谓词，允许暂时违反并进行恢复。示例包括 token 预算警告、响应延迟阈值和软超时建议。
  6. $R: (I_{soft} \cup G_{soft}) \times S \rightarrow A^*$ 是恢复机制：从违反的软约束和当前状态到有限纠正动作序列的偏映射。当 $R(c, s)$ 有定义时，其长度至多为 $k_{max}$。当 $R(c, s)$ 无定义时，监控器发出 RECOVERYFAILED 事件并推迟到外部干预（人工操作员或编排器）。
Remark 3.2 (Safety and Liveness Interpretation). In the taxonomy of temporal properties, hard constraints ($I_{hard}$, $G_{hard}$) are safety properties: they assert that "something bad never happens." Soft constraints ($I_{soft}$, $G_{soft}$) with recovery window $k$ encode bounded liveness: they assert that "something good eventually happens within $k$ steps." The bounded recovery window $k$ distinguishes ABC soft constraints from standard liveness properties, which impose no finite deadline. This bounded-liveness semantics is essential for practical deployment: an unbounded recovery promise is operationally indistinguishable from no recovery promise at all.
注释 3.2（安全性与活性解释）。 在时序性质的分类中，硬约束（$I_{hard}$, $G_{hard}$）是安全性（safety）性质：它们断言"坏事永不发生"。带恢复窗口 $k$ 的软约束（$I_{soft}$, $G_{soft}$）编码有界活性（bounded liveness）：它们断言"好事最终在 $k$ 步内发生"。有界恢复窗口 $k$ 将 ABC 软约束与标准活性性质区分开来，后者不施加有限期限。这种有界活性语义对于实际部署至关重要：无界恢复承诺在操作层面与没有恢复承诺没有区别。
Definition 3.3 (Execution Trace). An execution trace of length $T$ is a finite alternating sequence of states and actions: $\tau = (s_0, a_0, s_1, a_1, \ldots, s_{T-1}, a_{T-1}, s_T)$, where $s_t \in S$ denotes the agent's state at step $t$ and $a_t \in A$ denotes the action taken at step $t$. The state space $S$ encompasses the agent's internal context (e.g., conversation history, accumulated tool outputs, working memory) and the observable environment. The action space $A$ encompasses all outputs the agent may produce (e.g., text responses, tool calls, API invocations).
定义 3.3（执行轨迹）。 长度为 $T$ 的执行轨迹是状态与动作的有限交替序列：$\tau = (s_0, a_0, s_1, a_1, \ldots, s_{T-1}, a_{T-1}, s_T)$，其中 $s_t \in S$ 表示步骤 $t$ 的智能体状态，$a_t \in A$ 表示步骤 $t$ 采取的动作。状态空间 $S$ 涵盖智能体的内部上下文（如对话历史、工具输出积累、工作记忆）和可观测环境。动作空间 $A$ 涵盖智能体可产生的所有输出（如文本响应、工具调用、API 调用）。

3.2 Contract Satisfaction (Deterministic)

3.2 契约满足性（确定性）

Definition 3.4 (Deterministic Contract Satisfaction). An agent $A$ satisfies contract $C = (P, I, G, R)$ over an execution trace $\tau = (s_0, a_0, \ldots, s_T)$ if all of the following conditions hold:

  1. Precondition validity. Every precondition holds at the initial state: $\forall p \in \mathcal{P}: p(s_0) = \text{true}$.
  2. Invariant compliance. Every invariant holds at every state along the trace: $\forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T\}, \forall i \in \mathcal{I}: i(s_t) = \text{true}$.
  3. Governance compliance. Every governance constraint holds for every action: $\forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T-1\}, \forall g \in \mathcal{G}: g(a_t) = \text{true}$.
  4. Recoverability. For every soft constraint violation, the recovery mechanism restores compliance within $k$ steps: $\forall t, \forall c \in \mathcal{I}_{soft} \cup \mathcal{G}_{soft}: \neg c(s_t, a_t) \Rightarrow \exists t' \in \{t,\ldots,\min(t+k,T)\}: c(s_{t'}, a_{t'}) = \text{true}$.

定义 3.4（确定性契约满足性）。 智能体 $A$ 在执行轨迹 $\tau = (s_0, a_0, \ldots, s_T)$ 上满足契约 $C = (P, I, G, R)$，若以下所有条件成立：

  1. 前置条件有效性。 每个前置条件在初始状态处成立：$\forall p \in \mathcal{P}: p(s_0) = \text{true}$。
  2. 不变量合规性。 每个不变量在轨迹的每个状态处都成立：$\forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T\}, \forall i \in \mathcal{I}: i(s_t) = \text{true}$。
  3. 治理合规性。 每个治理约束对每个动作都成立：$\forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T-1\}, \forall g \in \mathcal{G}: g(a_t) = \text{true}$。
  4. 可恢复性。 对于每次软约束违规，恢复机制在 $k$ 步内恢复合规：$\forall t, \forall c \in \mathcal{I}_{soft} \cup \mathcal{G}_{soft}: \neg c(s_t, a_t) \Rightarrow \exists t' \in \{t,\ldots,\min(t+k,T)\}: c(s_{t'}, a_{t'}) = \text{true}$。
Remark 3.5. Deterministic satisfaction is a useful theoretical baseline, but it is too stringent for LLM-based agents. The stochastic nature of token sampling means that even well-aligned agents produce occasional soft violations. The next subsection relaxes this to a probabilistic guarantee.
注释 3.5。 确定性满足性是有用的理论基线，但对于基于 LLM 的智能体而言过于严格。token 采样的随机性意味着即使是对齐良好的智能体也会偶尔产生软违规。下一小节将其放宽为概率性保证。

3.3 Probabilistic $(p, \delta, k)$-Satisfaction

3.3 概率性 $(p, \delta, k)$-满足性

Definition 3.6 (Hard and Soft Compliance Scores). Given contract $C$ and execution trace $\tau$, the hard compliance score and soft compliance score at step $t$ are:

$$C_{hard}(t) = \frac{|\{i \in \mathcal{I}_{hard} : i(s_t) = \text{true}\}|}{|\mathcal{I}_{hard}|},$$
(1)
$$C_{soft}(t) = \frac{|\{i \in \mathcal{I}_{soft} : i(s_t) = \text{true}\}|}{|\mathcal{I}_{soft}|}.$$
(2)

Both scores lie in $[0, 1]$, with 1 indicating full compliance.

定义 3.6（硬/软合规分数）。 给定契约 $C$ 和执行轨迹 $\tau$，步骤 $t$ 处的硬合规分数和软合规分数为：

$$C_{hard}(t) = \frac{|\{i \in \mathcal{I}_{hard} : i(s_t) = \text{true}\}|}{|\mathcal{I}_{hard}|},$$
(1)
$$C_{soft}(t) = \frac{|\{i \in \mathcal{I}_{soft} : i(s_t) = \text{true}\}|}{|\mathcal{I}_{soft}|}.$$
(2)

两个分数均在 $[0, 1]$ 内，1 表示完全合规。

Definition 3.7 ($(p, \delta, k)$-Satisfaction). An agent $A$ $(p, \delta, k)$-satisfies contract $C$ over session length $T$ if both of the following conditions hold:

$$P\!\left[\forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T\}: C_{hard}(t) = 1\right] \geq p,$$
(3)
$$P\!\left[\forall t: C_{soft}(t) < 1{-}\delta \Rightarrow \exists t' \in \{t,\ldots,\min(t{+}k,T)\}: C_{soft}(t') \geq 1{-}\delta\right] \geq p.$$
(4)

The parameters are: $p$ = minimum compliance probability; $\delta$ = tolerable soft deviation; $k$ = recovery window in steps.

定义 3.7（$(p, \delta, k)$-满足性）。 智能体 $A$ 在会话长度 $T$ 上 $(p, \delta, k)$-满足契约 $C$，若以下两个条件均成立：

$$P\!\left[\forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T\}: C_{hard}(t) = 1\right] \geq p,$$
(3)
$$P\!\left[\forall t: C_{soft}(t) < 1{-}\delta \Rightarrow \exists t' \in \{t,\ldots,\min(t{+}k,T)\}: C_{soft}(t') \geq 1{-}\delta\right] \geq p.$$
(4)

参数含义：$p$ = 最低合规概率；$\delta$ = 可容忍软偏差；$k$ = 恢复窗口步数。

Remark 3.8 (Novelty of the Recovery Window Parameter). The recovery window $k$ is, to our knowledge, the first formal inclusion of a bounded recovery horizon as a first-class parameter in a contract satisfaction definition. Prior Design-by-Contract frameworks and runtime verification systems treat violations as binary pass/fail events with no notion of time-bounded recovery. The $k$-parameter bridges formal contracts and practical LLM deployment: it quantifies how much "slack" an agent is allowed before a transient deviation becomes a reportable failure.
注释 3.8（恢复窗口参数的新颖性）。 据我们所知，恢复窗口 $k$ 是首次在契约满足性定义中将有界恢复视野作为一等参数正式纳入。先前的按契约设计框架和运行时验证系统将违规视为二元的通过/失败事件，没有时间有界恢复的概念。$k$ 参数弥合了形式化契约与实际 LLM 部署之间的鸿沟：它量化了智能体在暂时偏差成为可报告失败前被允许的"弹性空间"。
Remark 3.9 (Connection to Probabilistic Computation Tree Logic). The $(p,\delta,k)$-satisfaction conditions have natural counterparts in Probabilistic Computation Tree Logic (PCTL). The hard guarantee corresponds to the PCTL formula $P_{\geq p}[G(C_{hard}(t) = 1)]$, which asserts that with probability at least $p$, the hard compliance score is globally equal to 1. The soft guarantee corresponds to $P_{\geq p}[G(C_{soft}(t) < 1-\delta \Rightarrow F_{\leq k}(C_{soft}(t) \geq 1-\delta))]$. This connection to PCTL enables the use of established model-checking techniques for verification when the agent's transition structure can be approximated as a finite Markov decision process.
注释 3.9（与概率计算树逻辑的联系）。 $(p,\delta,k)$-满足性条件在概率计算树逻辑（PCTL）中有自然对应物。硬保证对应 PCTL 公式 $P_{\geq p}[G(C_{hard}(t) = 1)]$，断言以至少 $p$ 的概率，硬合规分数在全局（每一步）等于 1。软保证对应 $P_{\geq p}[G(C_{soft}(t) < 1-\delta \Rightarrow F_{\leq k}(C_{soft}(t) \geq 1-\delta))]$。这与 PCTL 的联系使得当智能体的转移结构可以近似为有限马尔可夫决策过程时，可以使用成熟的模型检验技术进行验证。

3.4 Recovery Transforms Exponential Decay to Linear Decay

3.4 恢复将指数衰减转变为线性衰减

Lemma 3.10 (Recovery Linearizes Compliance Decay). Let $q \in (0,1)$ denote the per-step compliance probability and $r \in [0,1]$ the recovery effectiveness. Then:

  1. Without recovery, the probability of sustained compliance over $T$ steps decays exponentially:
  2. $$P[\text{compliance over }T\text{ steps}] = q^T.$$
    (5)
  3. With recovery, the probability of recoverable compliance satisfies:
  4. $$P[\text{recoverable compliance over }T\text{ steps}] \geq 1 - T(1-q)(1-r).$$
    (6)

引理 3.10（恢复使合规衰减线性化）。 设 $q \in (0,1)$ 为每步合规概率，$r \in [0,1]$ 为恢复有效性。则：

  1. 无恢复时，在 $T$ 步上持续合规的概率指数衰减：
  2. $$P[\text{T 步上的合规}] = q^T.$$
    (5)
  3. 有恢复时，可恢复合规的概率满足：
  4. $$P[\text{可恢复合规}] \geq 1 - T(1-q)(1-r).$$
    (6)

Proof. The first claim follows directly from the independence assumption: compliance at each step occurs with probability $q$, so compliance at all $T$ steps occurs with probability $q^T$.

证明。 第一个结论由独立性假设直接得出：每步以概率 $q$ 合规，故 $T$ 步全部合规的概率为 $q^T$。

For the second claim, we apply a union bound. A failure of recoverable compliance occurs when some step $t$ has a violation that is not recovered within $k$ steps. By the independence assumption, the probability of a violation at step $t$ is $(1-q)$. Given a violation, the probability that recovery fails within the window is $(1-r)$. By a union bound over all $T$ steps, the probability of at least one unrecovered violation is at most $T(1-q)(1-r)$. Therefore the probability of recoverable compliance is at least $1-T(1-q)(1-r)$. $\square$

对于第二个结论，应用联合界。可恢复合规失败当且仅当某步 $t$ 发生违规且未在 $k$ 步内恢复。由独立性假设，步骤 $t$ 发生违规的概率为 $(1-q)$。给定违规，恢复在窗口内失败的概率为 $(1-r)$。对所有 $T$ 步取联合界，至少一次未恢复违规的概率至多为 $T(1-q)(1-r)$。故可恢复合规的概率至少为 $1-T(1-q)(1-r)$。$\square$

Example 3.11. Consider an agent with per-step compliance $q = 0.99$ over a session of $T = 100$ steps. Without recovery, the probability of sustained compliance is $0.99^{100} \approx 0.366$—a coin flip is more reliable. With a recovery mechanism of effectiveness $r = 0.95$, the bound becomes $1 - 100 \cdot 0.01 \cdot 0.05 = 0.95$. Recovery transforms a 36.6% compliance probability into a 95% guarantee: a qualitative shift from unreliable to deployable.
例 3.11。 考虑一个在 $T = 100$ 步会话中每步合规概率 $q = 0.99$ 的智能体。无恢复时，持续合规的概率为 $0.99^{100} \approx 0.366$——比抛硬币还不可靠。有效性 $r = 0.95$ 的恢复机制将界变为 $1 - 100 \cdot 0.01 \cdot 0.05 = 0.95$。恢复将 36.6% 的合规概率转变为 95% 的保证：从不可靠到可部署的质变。

3.5 Behavioral Drift Score

3.5 行为漂移评分

Compliance scores detect violations after they occur. We introduce the behavioral drift score $D(t)$ as a composite metric that combines a reactive compliance component with a predictive distributional component, enabling early detection of emerging misalignment.

合规评分在违规发生后才能检测到。我们引入行为漂移评分 $D(t)$ 作为综合指标，将响应性合规组件与预测性分布组件相结合，从而能够早期检测到正在出现的不对齐。

Definition 3.12 (Behavioral Drift Score). The behavioral drift score at step $t$ is:

$$D(t) = w_c \cdot D_{compliance}(t) + w_d \cdot D_{distributional}(t),$$
(7)

where $w_c, w_d \geq 0$ with $w_c + w_d = 1$, and the components are:

定义 3.12（行为漂移分数）。 步骤 $t$ 处的行为漂移分数定义为：

$$D(t) = w_c \cdot D_{合规}(t) + w_d \cdot D_{分布}(t),$$
(7)

其中 $w_c, w_d \geq 0$ 且 $w_c + w_d = 1$，各分量为：

Compliance drift — the weighted compliance gap at step $t$:

$$D_{compliance}(t) = 1 - C(t) = \frac{\sum_i w_i(1 - \sigma_i(t))}{\sum_i w_i},$$
(8)

where $\sigma_i(t) \in \{0,1\}$ indicates whether constraint $i$ is satisfied at step $t$, and $w_i > 0$ is its weight.

合规漂移 — 步骤 $t$ 处的加权合规差距：

$$D_{合规}(t) = 1 - C(t) = \frac{\sum_i w_i(1 - \sigma_i(t))}{\sum_i w_i},$$
(8)

其中 $\sigma_i(t) \in \{0,1\}$ 表示约束 $i$ 在步骤 $t$ 是否满足，$w_i > 0$ 为其权重。

Distributional drift — the Jensen–Shannon divergence between the observed and reference action distributions:

$$D_{distributional}(t) = \text{JSD}(P_{observed}(t) \| P_{reference}).$$
(9)

分布漂移 — 观测动作分布与参考动作分布之间的 Jensen–Shannon 散度：

$$D_{分布}(t) = \text{JSD}(P_{观测}(t) \| P_{参考}).$$
(9)
Remark 3.13 (Interpretability of $D(t)$ Values). The drift score $D(t) \in [0,1]$ admits the following operational interpretation: $D(t) = 0$ indicates perfect compliance and distributional alignment; $D(t) \in (0, \theta_1]$ indicates negligible drift (no intervention required); $D(t) \in (\theta_1, \theta_2]$ indicates mild drift (monitoring should increase in frequency); $D(t) > \theta_2$ indicates significant drift (active intervention is recommended). Typical enterprise deployments use $\theta_1 = 0.10$ and $\theta_2 = 0.25$.
注释 3.13（$D(t)$ 值的可解释性）。 漂移分数 $D(t) \in [0,1]$ 具有以下操作解释：$D(t) = 0$ 表示完全合规且分布对齐；$D(t) \in (0, \theta_1]$ 表示可忽略漂移（无需干预）；$D(t) \in (\theta_1, \theta_2]$ 表示轻度漂移（应增加监控频率）；$D(t) > \theta_2$ 表示显著漂移（建议主动干预）。典型企业部署使用 $\theta_1 = 0.10$，$\theta_2 = 0.25$。
Proposition 3.14 (Properties of the Drift Score). The behavioral drift score $D(t)$ satisfies: (1) Boundedness: $D(t) \in [0,1]$ for all $t$. (2) Minimality: $D(t) = 0$ if and only if full compliance holds and the observed action distribution is identical to the reference distribution. (3) Incremental computability: $D(t)$ can be updated incrementally with complexity linear in the number of constraints and the action vocabulary size. (4) Metric structure: $\sqrt{\text{JSD}}$ is a metric on probability distributions and satisfies the triangle inequality.
命题 3.14（漂移分数的性质）。 行为漂移分数 $D(t)$ 满足：（1）有界性：对所有 $t$，$D(t) \in [0,1]$。（2）极小性：$D(t) = 0$ 当且仅当完全合规且观测动作分布与参考分布相同。（3）增量可计算性：$D(t)$ 可增量更新，复杂度关于约束数量和动作词汇表大小线性。（4）度量结构：$\sqrt{\text{JSD}}$ 是概率分布上的度量且满足三角不等式。

Proof. (1) Both $D_{compliance}(t) \in [0,1]$ (since $C(t) \in [0,1]$) and $D_{distributional}(t) \in [0,1]$ (JSD with base-2 logarithm is bounded by 1). Since $w_c + w_d = 1$ with $w_c, w_d \geq 0$, the convex combination lies in $[0,1]$. (2) $D(t) = 0$ requires $w_c \cdot D_{compliance}(t) = 0$ and $w_d \cdot D_{distributional}(t) = 0$. Since $w_c, w_d > 0$, this forces $C(t) = 1$ and $\text{JSD}(P_{obs} \| P_{ref}) = 0$. (3) The compliance component costs $O(k)$; the distributional component maintains a histogram over a sliding window, with $O(|A|)$ cost per update. (4) Proven by Endres and Schindelin [2003]. $\square$

证明。 (1) $D_{合规}(t) \in [0,1]$（因 $C(t) \in [0,1]$），$D_{分布}(t) \in [0,1]$（以 2 为底的 JSD 有界为 1）。由 $w_c + w_d = 1$，$w_c, w_d \geq 0$，凸组合在 $[0,1]$ 内。(2) $D(t) = 0$ 要求 $w_c \cdot D_{合规}(t) = 0$ 且 $w_d \cdot D_{分布}(t) = 0$。由 $w_c, w_d > 0$，迫使 $C(t) = 1$ 且 $\text{JSD}(P_{观测} \| P_{参考}) = 0$。(3) 合规分量开销 $O(k)$；分布分量在滑动窗口上维护直方图，每次更新开销 $O(|A|)$。(4) 由 Endres and Schindelin [2003] 证明。$\square$

Remark 3.15 (Meaningfulness of the Distributional Component). The JSD distributional component of $D(t)$ requires a sufficiently rich action vocabulary to produce informative signals. When the action space is insufficiently diverse, the empirical action distribution may be sparse and distributional measures exhibit high variance. In such cases, practitioners should either increase the observation window to smooth the estimate, or adjust the component weights to emphasize constraint-based compliance over distributional alignment. For typical enterprise deployments with diverse tool invocations, text categories, and API call types, the action vocabulary is easily sufficient.
注释 3.15（分布分量的有意义性）。 $D(t)$ 的 JSD 分布分量要求足够丰富的动作词汇表才能产生信息性信号。当动作空间多样性不足时，经验动作分布可能稀疏，分布度量呈现高方差。在这种情况下，从业者应当增大观测窗口以平滑估计值，或调整分量权重以更强调基于约束的合规性而非分布对齐。对于具有多样工具调用、文本类别和 API 调用类型的典型企业部署，动作词汇表通常已足够丰富。
Remark 3.16 (Leading vs. Lagging Indicators). The two components serve complementary diagnostic roles. The compliance drift is a lagging indicator: it registers non-zero values only after a constraint violation has already occurred. The distributional drift is a leading indicator: it can detect shifts in the agent's action distribution before these shifts manifest as explicit constraint violations. This early-warning capability is critical for preemptive intervention.
注释 3.16（领先指标与滞后指标）。 两个组件发挥互补的诊断作用。合规漂移是滞后指标：仅在约束违规已经发生后才记录非零值。分布漂移是领先指标：它能在这些偏移表现为显式约束违规之前，检测到智能体动作分布的偏移——如对冲性语言的增加使用、非典型工具调用模式或话题焦点的漂移。这种预警能力对于预防性干预至关重要。

3.6 Additional Operational Metrics

3.6 附加操作性指标

Definition 3.18 (Recovery Effectiveness). The recovery effectiveness for a violation event at step $t$ is:

$$E(t) = \frac{\Delta t_{recovery}}{\nu(t)},$$
(11)

where $\Delta t_{recovery}$ is the number of steps required to restore compliance and $\nu(t) \in (0,1]$ is the severity of the violation. Lower values of $E$ indicate more effective recovery. The session-level recovery effectiveness is $\bar{E} = \frac{1}{|V|}\sum_{t \in V} E(t)$, where $V$ is the set of violation events.

定义 3.18（恢复有效性）。 步骤 $t$ 处违规事件的恢复有效性为：

$$E(t) = \frac{\Delta t_{恢复}}{\nu(t)},$$
(11)

其中 $\Delta t_{恢复}$ 是恢复合规所需步数，$\nu(t) \in (0,1]$ 是违规严重程度。$E$ 值越低表示恢复越有效。会话级恢复有效性为 $\bar{E} = \frac{1}{|V|}\sum_{t \in V} E(t)$，其中 $V$ 为违规事件集合。

Definition 3.19 (Stress Resilience Index). The stress resilience index measures compliance degradation under adversarial or high-load conditions:

$$S = \frac{\mathbb{E}[C(t) \mid \text{stressed}]}{\mathbb{E}[C(t) \mid \text{baseline}]},$$
(12)

where the expectations are taken over steps within stressed and baseline sessions. $S = 1$ indicates no degradation; $S < 1$ quantifies the compliance penalty under adversarial conditions.

定义 3.19（压力韧性指数）。 压力韧性指数衡量对抗性或高负载条件下的合规性退化：

$$S = \frac{\mathbb{E}[C(t) \mid \text{压力}]}{\mathbb{E}[C(t) \mid \text{基线}]},$$
(12)

其中期望在压力和基线会话的各步骤上取得。$S = 1$ 表示无退化；$S < 1$ 量化对抗性条件下的合规惩罚。

Definition 3.20 (Agent Reliability Index). The agent reliability index is a weighted composite that summarizes an agent's overall contractual fitness:

$$\Theta = \alpha_1 \cdot C(t) + \alpha_2 \cdot (1 - D(t)) + \alpha_3 \cdot \frac{1}{1+E} + \alpha_4 \cdot S,$$
(13)

where $\sum_{i=1}^4 \alpha_i = 1$. The index $\Theta \in [0, 1]$ provides a single scalar summary suitable for comparing agents, tracking reliability over time, and establishing deployment thresholds.

定义 3.20（智能体可靠性指数）。 智能体可靠性指数是汇总智能体整体契约适应性的加权综合指标：

$$\Theta = \alpha_1 \cdot C(t) + \alpha_2 \cdot (1 - D(t)) + \alpha_3 \cdot \frac{1}{1+E} + \alpha_4 \cdot S,$$
(13)

其中 $\sum_{i=1}^4 \alpha_i = 1$。指数 $\Theta \in [0, 1]$ 提供单一标量汇总，适用于比较智能体、跟踪长期可靠性和设定部署阈值。

4 Drift Prevention via Contracts

4 通过契约防止漂移

This section provides the theoretical backbone of the ABC framework. We model behavioral drift as a continuous-time stochastic process, derive tight probabilistic bounds on drift under contract enforcement, establish sufficient conditions for safe contract composition in multi-agent chains, and analyze the runtime cost of contract checking.

本节提供 ABC 框架的理论支撑。我们将行为漂移建模为连续时间随机过程，在契约强制执行下推导漂移的紧概率界，为多智能体链中的安全契约组合建立充分条件，并分析契约检查的运行时代价。

4.1 Drift Dynamics Model

4.1 漂移动态模型

Definition 4.1 (Drift Dynamics). Let $D(t) \geq 0$ denote the behavioral drift of an agent at time $t \geq 0$. The drift evolves according to the stochastic differential equation:

$$dD(t) = (\alpha - \gamma D(t))\,dt + \sigma\,dW(t),$$
(14)

where $\alpha > 0$ is the natural drift rate, $\gamma > 0$ is the contract recovery rate, $\sigma > 0$ is the volatility, and $W(t)$ is a standard Wiener process.

定义 4.1（漂移动态）。 设 $D(t) \geq 0$ 表示智能体在时刻 $t \geq 0$ 的行为漂移。漂移按随机微分方程演化：

$$dD(t) = (\alpha - \gamma D(t))\,dt + \sigma\,dW(t),$$
(14)

其中 $\alpha > 0$ 为自然漂移率，$\gamma > 0$ 为契约恢复率，$\sigma > 0$ 为波动率，$W(t)$ 为标准维纳过程。

The three terms admit clear interpretations: (i) Baseline drift ($\alpha\,dt$): in the absence of enforcement, the agent's behavior naturally diverges from the contracted specification at rate $\alpha$. (ii) Contract recovery ($-\gamma D(t)\,dt$): the enforcement mechanism exerts a restorative force proportional to current drift. (iii) Stochastic perturbation ($\sigma\,dW(t)$): LLM outputs are inherently non-deterministic due to token sampling.

三项具有清晰的解释：（i）基础漂移（$\alpha\,dt$）：在无强制执行的情况下，智能体行为以速率 $\alpha$ 自然偏离契约规约。（ii）契约恢复（$-\gamma D(t)\,dt$）：强制执行机制施加与当前漂移成比例的恢复力。（iii）随机扰动（$\sigma\,dW(t)$）：LLM 输出因 token 采样而本质上具有不确定性。

Remark 4.2. Equation is an instance of the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck (OU) process with mean-reversion level $\mu^* = \alpha/\gamma$. The OU process admits a closed-form transition density, a Gaussian stationary distribution, and exponential ergodicity bounds.
注释 4.2。 该方程是均值回归水平为 $\mu^* = \alpha/\gamma$ 的 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck（OU）过程的实例。OU 过程具有封闭形式的转移密度、高斯平稳分布和指数遍历性界——本节将充分利用这些性质。

4.2 Drift Bounds Theorem

4.2 漂移界定定理

Theorem 4.3 (Stochastic Drift Bound). Let $D(t)$ evolve according to the drift dynamics (14) with initial condition $D(0) = D_0 \geq 0$. Then:

  • Stationary distribution. There exists a unique stationary distribution:
  • $$\pi_D = \mathcal{N}\!\left(\frac{\alpha}{\gamma},\; \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}\right).$$
    (15)
  • Mean drift bound. Under the stationary distribution:
  • $$\mathbb{E}_\pi[D(t)] = \frac{\alpha}{\gamma}.$$
    (16)
  • In particular, if $\gamma > \alpha$ then $\mathbb{E}_\pi[D(t)] < 1$.
  • Variance bound. Under the stationary distribution:
  • $$\text{Var}_\pi(D(t)) = \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}.$$
    (17)
  • Higher contract recovery rate $\gamma$ quadratically reduces the spread of drift fluctuations relative to the noise level $\sigma$.
  • High-probability bound. For any $\eta > 0$:
  • $$P_\pi\!\left(D(t) > \frac{\alpha}{\gamma} + \eta\right) \leq \exp\!\left(-\frac{\gamma\eta^2}{\sigma^2}\right).$$
    (18)
  • Exponential convergence. For all $t \geq 0$:
  • $$\mathbb{E}\!\left[(D(t) - \alpha/\gamma)^2\right] = \left(D_0 - \frac{\alpha}{\gamma}\right)^2 e^{-2\gamma t} + \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}\left(1 - e^{-2\gamma t}\right).$$
    (19)
  • Contract design criterion. To ensure $D(t) < D_{max}$ with probability at least $1-\varepsilon$, choose $\gamma$ as the larger root of:
  • $$D_{max}^2 \gamma^2 - \left(2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon)\right)\gamma + \alpha^2 = 0,$$
    (20)
  • i.e.:
  • $$\gamma \geq \frac{2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon) + \sqrt{\left(2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon)\right)^2 - 4\alpha^2 D_{max}^2}}{2 D_{max}^2}.$$
    (21)
  • When $\sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon) \ll 2\alpha D_{max}$, this simplifies to the approximate criterion $\gamma \gtrsim \alpha/D_{max} + \sigma\sqrt{2\ln(1/\varepsilon)}/(2D_{max})$.

定理 4.3（随机漂移界）。 设 $D(t)$ 在初始条件 $D(0) = D_0 \geq 0$ 下按漂移动态 (14) 演化。则：

  • 平稳分布。 存在唯一平稳分布：
  • $$\pi_D = \mathcal{N}\!\left(\frac{\alpha}{\gamma},\; \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}\right).$$
    (15)
  • 均值漂移界。 在平稳分布下：
  • $$\mathbb{E}_\pi[D(t)] = \frac{\alpha}{\gamma}.$$
    (16)
  • 特别地，若 $\gamma > \alpha$ 则 $\mathbb{E}_\pi[D(t)] < 1$。
  • 方差界。 在平稳分布下：
  • $$\text{Var}_\pi(D(t)) = \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}.$$
    (17)
  • 较高的契约恢复率 $\gamma$ 二次减小了相对于噪声水平 $\sigma$ 的漂移波动范围。
  • 高概率界。 对任意 $\eta > 0$：
  • $$P_\pi\!\left(D(t) > \frac{\alpha}{\gamma} + \eta\right) \leq \exp\!\left(-\frac{\gamma\eta^2}{\sigma^2}\right).$$
    (18)
  • 指数收敛。 对所有 $t \geq 0$：
  • $$\mathbb{E}\!\left[(D(t) - \alpha/\gamma)^2\right] = \left(D_0 - \frac{\alpha}{\gamma}\right)^2 e^{-2\gamma t} + \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}\left(1 - e^{-2\gamma t}\right).$$
    (19)
  • 契约设计准则。 为确保以至少 $1-\varepsilon$ 的概率 $D(t) < D_{max}$，选择 $\gamma$ 为以下二次方程的较大根：
  • $$D_{max}^2 \gamma^2 - \left(2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon)\right)\gamma + \alpha^2 = 0,$$
    (20)
  • 即：
  • $$\gamma \geq \frac{2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon) + \sqrt{\left(2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon)\right)^2 - 4\alpha^2 D_{max}^2}}{2 D_{max}^2}.$$
    (21)
  • 当 $\sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon) \ll 2\alpha D_{max}$ 时，这简化为近似准则 $\gamma \gtrsim \alpha/D_{max} + \sigma\sqrt{2\ln(1/\varepsilon)}/(2D_{max})$。

Proof sketch. Define the centered error process $e(t) = D(t) - \alpha/\gamma$. Substituting into (14) yields the centered OU equation:

$$de(t) = -\gamma e(t)\,dt + \sigma\,dW(t),$$
(22)

which has zero mean-reversion level and rate $\gamma$.

证明概要。 定义中心化误差过程 $e(t) = D(t) - \alpha/\gamma$。代入 (14) 得到中心化 OU 方程：

$$de(t) = -\gamma e(t)\,dt + \sigma\,dW(t),$$
(22)

该方程的均值回归水平为零，回归速率为 $\gamma$。

To establish convergence, define the Lyapunov function $V(e) = e^2$ and apply Itô's formula: $dV = (-2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2)\,dt + 2\sigma e\,dW(t)$. Taking expectations eliminates the martingale term, yielding the deterministic ODE $\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}[V(t)] = -2\gamma\mathbb{E}[V(t)] + \sigma^2$. This ODE has solution $\mathbb{E}[V(t)] = V(0)e^{-2\gamma t} + \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}(1-e^{-2\gamma t})$, establishing parts (iii) and (v). The stationary distribution (i) follows from standard Ornstein–Uhlenbeck theory: the unique invariant measure is Gaussian with mean $\alpha/\gamma$ and variance $\sigma^2/(2\gamma)$, from which part (ii) is immediate.

为建立收敛性，定义 Lyapunov 函数 $V(e) = e^2$ 并应用 Itô 公式：$dV = (-2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2)\,dt + 2\sigma e\,dW(t)$。取期望消去鞅项，得确定性 ODE $\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}[V(t)] = -2\gamma\mathbb{E}[V(t)] + \sigma^2$。该 ODE 的解为 $\mathbb{E}[V(t)] = V(0)e^{-2\gamma t} + \frac{\sigma^2}{2\gamma}(1-e^{-2\gamma t})$，证明了（iii）和（v）。平稳分布（i）由标准 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 理论得出：唯一不变测度是均值为 $\alpha/\gamma$、方差为 $\sigma^2/(2\gamma)$ 的高斯分布，（ii）由此直接得出。

The tail bound (iv) applies Gaussian concentration to the stationary distribution: for $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma_s^2)$ with $\sigma_s^2 = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$, $\mathbb{P}(X > \mu + \eta) \leq \exp(-\eta^2/(2\sigma_s^2)) = \exp(-\gamma\eta^2/\sigma^2)$. The design criterion (vi) follows by setting the right-hand side of (18) to $\varepsilon$ with $\eta = D_{max} - \alpha/\gamma$ and solving for $\gamma$. The full details are in Section A. $\square$

尾部界（iv）对平稳分布应用高斯集中性：对 $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma_s^2)$，$\sigma_s^2 = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$，有 $\mathbb{P}(X > \mu + \eta) \leq \exp(-\eta^2/(2\sigma_s^2)) = \exp(-\gamma\eta^2/\sigma^2)$。设计准则（vi）通过令 (18) 右端等于 $\varepsilon$，$\eta = D_{max} - \alpha/\gamma$，解出 $\gamma$ 得到。完整细节见附录 A。$\square$

Remark 4.4. Theorem 4.3 has direct engineering implications. Part (vi) provides an exact design rule: given a maximum tolerable drift $D_{max}$ and reliability requirement $1-\varepsilon$, the contract designer solves the quadratic to obtain the minimum recovery rate $\gamma$ needed to meet the specification.
注释 4.4。 定理 4.3 具有直接的工程意义。第（vi）部分提供了精确的设计规则：给定应用的最大可容忍漂移 $D_{max}$ 和可靠性需求 $1-\varepsilon$，契约设计者求解该二次方程即可得到满足规约所需的最低恢复率 $\gamma$。

4.3 Contract Composition

4.3 契约组合

Enterprise agentic systems rarely consist of a single agent. A typical deployment chains multiple specialized agents into a sequential pipeline. We now establish conditions under which individual contract guarantees compose into end-to-end guarantees for the chain.

企业 agentic 系统很少由单个智能体组成。典型部署将多个专用智能体——规划者、检索者、编码者、审查者——链接成顺序流水线。我们现在建立个体契约保证组合为链的端到端保证的条件。

4.3.1 Serial Composition

4.3.1 串行组合

Consider a serial chain $A \to B$ where agent $A$ produces output consumed by agent $B$.

考虑串行链 $A \to B$，其中智能体 $A$ 的输出由智能体 $B$ 消费。

Definition 4.5 (Composed Contract). Given contracts $C_A = (P_A, I_A, G_A, R_A)$ and $C_B = (P_B, I_B, G_B, R_B)$ for agents $A$ and $B$ respectively, the composed contract for the serial chain $A \to B$ is

$$C_{A\oplus B} = (P_{A\oplus B},\; I_{A\oplus B},\; G_{A\oplus B},\; R_{A\oplus B})$$
(23)

定义 4.5（组合契约）。 给定智能体 $A$ 和 $B$ 的契约 $C_A = (P_A, I_A, G_A, R_A)$ 和 $C_B = (P_B, I_B, G_B, R_B)$，串行链 $A \to B$ 的组合契约为

$$C_{A\oplus B} = (P_{A\oplus B},\; I_{A\oplus B},\; G_{A\oplus B},\; R_{A\oplus B})$$
(23)

where:

$$P_{A\oplus B} = P_A,$$
(24)
$$I_{A\oplus B} = I_A \wedge I_B \wedge I_{\text{handoff}},$$
(25)
$$G_{A\oplus B} = G_A \cup G_B \quad (\text{assuming no conflicts}),$$
(26)
$$R_{A\oplus B} = \text{compose}(R_A, R_B, R_{\text{cascade}}),$$
(27)

where $I_{\text{handoff}}$ is a handoff invariant ensuring safe state transfer between $A$ and $B$, and $R_{\text{cascade}}$ is a cascade recovery policy that coordinates individual recovery actions across the chain boundary.

其中：

$$P_{A\oplus B} = P_A,$$
(24)
$$I_{A\oplus B} = I_A \wedge I_B \wedge I_{\text{交接}},$$
(25)
$$G_{A\oplus B} = G_A \cup G_B \quad (\text{假设无冲突}),$$
(26)
$$R_{A\oplus B} = \text{compose}(R_A, R_B, R_{\text{级联}}),$$
(27)

其中 $I_{\text{交接}}$ 是确保 $A$ 和 $B$ 之间安全状态转移的交接不变量，$R_{\text{级联}}$ 是协调链边界各恢复动作的级联恢复策略。

Definition 4.6 (Postcondition). The postcondition of agent $A$ under contract $C_A$, denoted $\text{PostCond}_A$, is the set of states reachable at termination of $A$ that satisfy all of $A$'s invariants: $\text{PostCond}_A = \{s \in S : \forall i \in \mathcal{I}_A,\, i(s) = \text{true}\}$.
定义 4.6（后置条件）。 智能体 $A$ 在契约 $C_A$ 下的后置条件，记为 $\text{PostCond}_A$，是 $A$ 终止时可达且满足 $A$ 所有不变量的状态集合：$\text{PostCond}_A = \{s \in S : \forall i \in \mathcal{I}_A,\, i(s) = \text{true}\}$。

Safe composition requires four sufficient conditions:

安全组合需要四个充分条件：

Definition 4.7 (Composition Conditions). A serial chain $A \to B$ satisfies the composition conditions if:

  • (C1) Interface Compatibility: the output type of $A$ is a subtype of the input type expected by $B$.
  • (C2) Assumption Discharge: the postconditions of $A$ together with handoff invariants logically imply the preconditions of $B$.
  • (C3) Governance Consistency: every action permitted by $A$'s governance policies is not prohibited by $B$'s governance policies.
  • (C4) Recovery Independence: after $A$'s recovery mechanism triggers, the resulting state still satisfies $B$'s preconditions.

定义 4.7（组合条件）。 串行链 $A \to B$ 满足组合条件，若：

  • (C1) 接口兼容性： $A$ 的输出类型是 $B$ 期望的输入类型的子类型。
  • (C2) 假设释放： $A$ 的后置条件连同交接不变量逻辑蕴含 $B$ 的前置条件。
  • (C3) 治理一致性： $A$ 的治理策略允许的动作均不被 $B$ 的治理策略禁止。
  • (C4) 恢复独立性： $A$ 的恢复机制触发后，结果状态仍满足 $B$ 的前置条件。
Remark 4.8 (Standard vs. Novel Composition Conditions). Conditions (C1) and (C2) (interface compatibility and assumption discharge) are standard in Design-by-Contract composition and assume-guarantee reasoning. Conditions (C3) and (C4) (governance consistency and recovery independence) are novel contributions of ABC, motivated by the unique operational requirements of multi-agent LLM pipelines: governance constraints span organizational policies (not just type systems), and recovery mechanisms can have cross-agent side effects that invalidate downstream preconditions.
注释 4.8（标准条件与新颖条件）。 条件（C1）和（C2）（接口兼容性和假设释放）是按契约设计组合和假设-保证推理中的标准条件。条件（C3）和（C4）（治理一致性和恢复独立性）是 ABC 框架的新颖贡献，源于多智能体 LLM 流水线的独特操作需求：治理约束跨越组织策略（而不仅仅是类型系统），且恢复机制可能产生跨智能体副作用，从而使下游前置条件失效。
Theorem 4.9 (Compositionality). Let agents $A$ and $B$ satisfy their respective contracts. If conditions (C1)–(C4) hold, then $\text{Chain}(A, B) \models C_{A\oplus B}$.
定理 4.9（可组合性）。 设智能体 $A$ 和 $B$ 分别满足其契约。若条件（C1）–（C4）成立，则 $\text{Chain}(A, B) \models C_{A\oplus B}$。

Proof sketch. We verify each component of $C_{A\oplus B}$. The chain's precondition $P_{A\oplus B} = P_A$ holds by assumption. Since $A \models C_A$, agent $A$ terminates in a state satisfying $\text{PostCond}_A$. Condition (C2) then guarantees $P_B$ holds at the handoff point, so $B$ begins execution with a valid precondition. The composed invariant $I_A \wedge I_B \wedge I_{\text{handoff}}$ is maintained: $I_A$ holds during $A$'s execution by $A \models C_A$, $I_B$ holds during $B$'s execution by $B \models C_B$, and $I_{\text{handoff}}$ holds at the transition by construction. Condition (C3) ensures the union $G_A \cup G_B$ is conflict-free. Condition (C4) ensures that if $A$'s recovery fires, the post-recovery state remains a valid input for $B$. The full proof is in Section A. $\square$

证明概要。 验证 $C_{A\oplus B}$ 的每个分量。链的前置条件 $P_{A\oplus B} = P_A$ 由假设成立。由于 $A \models C_A$，智能体 $A$ 终止于满足 $\text{PostCond}_A$ 的状态。条件（C2）保证 $P_B$ 在交接点成立，故 $B$ 以有效前置条件开始执行。组合不变量 $I_A \wedge I_B \wedge I_{\text{交接}}$ 得到维持：$I_A$ 在 $A$ 执行期间由 $A \models C_A$ 保持，$I_B$ 在 $B$ 执行期间由 $B \models C_B$ 保持，$I_{\text{交接}}$ 在转换处由构造保持。条件（C3）确保 $G_A \cup G_B$ 无冲突。条件（C4）确保 $A$ 的恢复机制触发后，恢复后状态仍是 $B$ 的有效输入。完整证明见附录 A。$\square$

Remark 4.10 (Recovery Window Composition). When composing contracts with recovery windows $k_A$ and $k_B$, the composed recovery window is $k_{A\oplus B} = \max(k_A, k_B)$. The maximum (rather than the sum) is the correct composition rule because recovery windows operate concurrently within each agent's execution phase: a soft violation in $A$ must be recovered within $k_A$ steps of $A$'s local trace, not within a global budget shared with $B$. The composed window therefore reflects the more demanding of the two per-agent requirements.
注释 4.10（恢复窗口组合）。 当组合具有恢复窗口 $k_A$ 和 $k_B$ 的契约时，组合恢复窗口为 $k_{A\oplus B} = \max(k_A, k_B)$。最大值（而非求和）是正确的组合规则，因为恢复窗口在每个智能体的执行阶段内并发运行：$A$ 中的软违规必须在 $A$ 的局部轨迹的 $k_A$ 步内恢复，而非在与 $B$ 共享的全局预算内。因此，组合窗口反映了两个单智能体需求中更严格的那个。

4.3.2 Probabilistic Composition

4.3.2 概率性组合

In practice, contract satisfaction is probabilistic (cf. Definition 3.7). We now characterize how probabilistic guarantees degrade under composition.

在实践中，契约满足性是概率性的（参见定义 3.7）。我们现在刻画概率保证在组合下如何退化。

Theorem 4.11 (Probabilistic Compositionality). Suppose agent $A$ $(p_A, \delta_A)$-satisfies $C_A$ and agent $B$ $(p_B, \delta_B)$-satisfies $C_B$. Let $p_h$ denote the handoff success probability and $\delta_h$ the handoff-induced drift. Assuming condition (C5) (Conditional Independence), the composed chain $(p_{A\oplus B}, \delta_{A\oplus B})$-satisfies $C_{A\oplus B}$ with:

$$p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h,$$
(28)
$$\delta_{A\oplus B} \leq \delta_A + \delta_B + \delta_h.$$
(29)

定理 4.11（概率可组合性）。 设智能体 $A$ $(p_A, \delta_A)$-满足 $C_A$，智能体 $B$ $(p_B, \delta_B)$-满足 $C_B$。设 $p_h$ 为交接成功概率，$\delta_h$ 为交接引入的漂移。在条件（C5）（条件独立性）下，组合链 $(p_{A\oplus B}, \delta_{A\oplus B})$-满足 $C_{A\oplus B}$，其中：

$$p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h,$$
(28)
$$\delta_{A\oplus B} \leq \delta_A + \delta_B + \delta_h.$$
(29)

Proof sketch. The chain satisfies $C_{A\oplus B}$ only if all three events occur: $A$ satisfies $C_A$, the handoff succeeds, and $B$ satisfies $C_B$. By condition (C5) (conditional independence of agent-level failures given contract-compliant inputs), the joint probability is at least $p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h$. The drift bound follows from sub-additivity: the maximum end-to-end deviation is bounded by the sum of per-stage deviations plus the handoff-induced deviation. See Section A for the formal argument. $\square$

证明概要。 链满足 $C_{A\oplus B}$ 当且仅当三个事件同时发生：$A$ 满足 $C_A$、交接成功、$B$ 满足 $C_B$。由条件（C5）（在给定契约合规输入下，智能体级失败的条件独立性），联合概率至少为 $p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h$。漂移界由次可加性得出：端到端最大偏差以各阶段偏差之和加上交接引入的偏差为上界。正式论证见附录 A。$\square$

Remark 4.12 (Conditional Independence and Correlated LLM Failures). Condition (C5) is satisfied when agents $A$ and $B$ operate on separate LLM instances or use distinct model providers. When both agents share the same underlying LLM, correlated failure modes (e.g., systematic prompt sensitivity, shared training biases) may violate conditional independence. In such settings, the probability bound $p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h$ becomes optimistic; practitioners should apply a correlation penalty or use the tighter Fréchet–Hoeffding lower bound $p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A + p_B \cdot p_h - 1$ as a conservative alternative.
注释 4.12（条件独立性与相关 LLM 失效）。 当智能体 $A$ 和 $B$ 在独立 LLM 实例上运行或使用不同模型提供商时，条件（C5）满足。当两个智能体共享同一底层 LLM 时，相关失效模式（如系统性提示敏感性、共享训练偏差）可能违反条件独立性。在这种情况下，概率界 $p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h$ 变得过于乐观；从业者应施加相关性惩罚，或使用更保守的 Fréchet–Hoeffding 下界 $p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A + p_B \cdot p_h - 1$ 作为替代。

Corollary 4.13 (N-Agent Chain). For a serial chain of $N$ agents where each agent $A_i$ $(p_i, \delta_i)$-satisfies $C_i$ and each handoff has reliability $p_{h_i}$ and drift $\delta_{h_i}$:

$$p_{chain} \geq \prod_{i=1}^N p_i \cdot \prod_{i=1}^{N-1} p_{h_i},$$
(30)
$$\delta_{chain} \leq \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_i + \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} \delta_{h_i}.$$
(31)

推论 4.13（N 智能体链）。 对于 $N$ 个智能体的串行链，其中每个智能体 $A_i$ $(p_i, \delta_i)$-满足 $C_i$，每个交接可靠性为 $p_{h_i}$、漂移为 $\delta_{h_i}$：

$$p_{链} \geq \prod_{i=1}^N p_i \cdot \prod_{i=1}^{N-1} p_{h_i},$$
(30)
$$\delta_{链} \leq \sum_{i=1}^N \delta_i + \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} \delta_{h_i}.$$
(31)

Proof. Follows by inductive application of Theorem 4.11 along the chain. $\square$

证明。 由沿链对定理 4.11 进行归纳应用得出。$\square$

Remark 4.14 (The Broken Telephone Effect). Corollary 4.13 formalizes the "broken telephone" effect in multi-agent systems: reliability degrades multiplicatively while drift accumulates additively. Example: a 5-agent chain where each agent satisfies its contract with probability $p_i = 0.95$ and each handoff succeeds with probability $p_{h_i} = 0.98$ yields $p_{chain} \geq 0.95^5 \cdot 0.98^4 \approx 0.714$. A chain that appears reliable at the individual level (95% per agent) delivers only ~71.4% end-to-end reliability.
注释 4.14（"传话游戏"效应）。 推论 4.13 将多智能体系统中的"传话游戏"效应形式化：可靠性乘积式退化，而漂移加性累积。示例：5 个智能体的链，每个智能体合规概率 $p_i = 0.95$，每个交接成功概率 $p_{h_i} = 0.98$，则 $p_{链} \geq 0.95^5 \cdot 0.98^4 \approx 0.714$。在个体层面看起来可靠（每个智能体 95%）的链，端到端可靠性仅约 71.4%。这量化了为何多智能体流水线需要在每个阶段而非仅在端点显式进行契约强制执行。

4.4 Complexity Analysis

4.4 复杂度分析

Proposition 4.15 (Runtime Contract Checking). Let $k$ denote the number of constraints and $|A|$ the size of the agent's action vocabulary. The per-action cost of runtime contract checking is $O(k + |A|)$.
命题 4.15（运行时契约检查）。 设 $k$ 为约束数量，$|A|$ 为智能体动作词汇表的大小。运行时契约检查的每动作代价为 $O(k + |A|)$。

Proof. The enforcement loop performs three operations per agent action: (1) Constraint evaluation: each of the $k$ constraints is evaluated as a Boolean predicate — each is $O(1)$, so evaluating all $k$ costs $O(k)$. (2) Behavioral drift update: the JSD is maintained incrementally via a sliding-window histogram over the action vocabulary; updating and recomputing costs $O(|A|)$. (3) Weighted aggregation: the overall compliance score is a weighted sum, computed in $O(1)$. Combining: $O(k) + O(|A|) + O(1) = O(k + |A|)$. $\square$

证明。 执行循环每次智能体动作执行三个操作：(1) 约束评估：每个 $k$ 个约束评估为布尔谓词——每个 $O(1)$，故评估全部 $k$ 个开销 $O(k)$。(2) 行为漂移更新：通过滑动窗口直方图增量维护 JSD，更新和重新计算开销 $O(|A|)$。(3) 加权聚合：整体合规分数为加权求和，$O(1)$ 计算。综合：$O(k) + O(|A|) + O(1) = O(k + |A|)$。$\square$

Remark 4.16 (Practical Overhead). For typical enterprise contracts with $k < 100$ constraints and action vocabularies $|A| < 50$, the measured wall-clock overhead of contract checking in AgentAssert is consistently below 10 ms per action—approximately 0.5–5% of LLM inference latency.
注释 4.16（实际开销）。 对于 $k < 100$ 个约束和 $|A| < 50$ 的动作词汇表的典型企业契约，AgentAssert 契约检查的实测挂钟开销始终低于每动作 10ms——约为 LLM 推断延迟的 0.5–5%。

5 ContractSpec 与 AgentAssert

5.1 ContractSpec: A Domain-Specific Language for Agent Contracts

5.1 ContractSpec：智能体契约的领域特定语言

ContractSpec is a YAML-based DSL that translates the mathematical contract tuple into a human-readable, machine-validatable specification. The design reflects three principles:

  1. Declarative over imperative — contract authors specify what must hold, not how to check it.
  2. Hybrid syntax — constraints may be expressed via structured operators or via expressive predicates.
  3. File-reference composition — pipeline contracts reference per-agent contracts by name or path, enabling compositional specification without duplication.

ContractSpec 是一种基于 YAML 的 DSL，将数学契约元组转化为人类可读、机器可验证的规约。设计遵循三个原则：

  1. 声明式而非命令式 — 契约作者规约什么必须成立，而非如何检查。
  2. 混合语法 — 约束可以通过结构化算子或表达性谓词表达。
  3. 文件引用组合 — 流水线契约通过名称或路径引用各智能体契约，无需重复，直接支持定义 4.7 的组合条件。

5.2 AgentAssert Architecture

5.2 AgentAssert 架构

AgentAssert implements the ABC framework as a modular Python library with distinct functional concerns:

  • Parsing and validation: Loads contract specifications, validates structure and semantics, produces typed contract objects.
  • Constraint evaluation: Evaluates preconditions, invariants, and governance constraints against observed agent state, computes compliance scores.
  • Metric tracking: Maintains time-series data for compliance, drift, and recovery effectiveness, computes the reliability index.
  • Runtime orchestration: Coordinates per-turn enforcement, recovery execution, and event notification.
  • Integration: Provides framework-agnostic hooks and framework-specific adapters for agent platforms.
  • Benchmarking: Evaluates contracts against the AgentContract-Bench suite.

AgentAssert 将 ABC 框架实现为具有清晰功能分层的模块化 Python 库：

  • 解析与验证：加载契约规约，验证结构和语义，生成有类型的契约对象。
  • 约束评估：对观测智能体状态评估前置条件、不变量和治理约束，计算合规分数。
  • 指标跟踪：维护合规、漂移和恢复有效性的时间序列数据，计算可靠性指数。
  • 运行时编排：协调每轮强制执行、恢复执行和事件通知。
  • 集成：提供框架无关的钩子和针对特定智能体平台的适配器。
  • 基准测试：对 AgentContract-Bench 套件评估契约。

5.3 Per-Turn Enforcement

5.3 每轮强制执行

At each agent execution step, the monitor:

  1. evaluates all contract constraints against the current observed state;
  2. updates compliance and drift metrics;
  3. emits notification events for violations and drift alerts;
  4. attempts recovery for soft constraint violations within the bounded recovery window;
  5. resets recovery state for constraints that return to satisfaction.

在每个智能体执行步骤，监控器：

  1. 对当前观测状态评估所有契约约束；
  2. 根据评估结果更新合规和漂移指标；
  3. 为违规和漂移警报发出通知事件；
  4. 在有界恢复窗口内对软约束违规尝试恢复；
  5. 为重新满足的约束重置恢复状态。

5.4 Recovery Mechanisms

5.4 恢复机制

Recovery is the operational realization of the mapping $R: (I_{soft} \cup G_{soft}) \times S \to A^*$. ContractSpec defines a taxonomy of recovery types organized by escalation severity, ranging from lightweight prompt modifications through autonomy reduction to human escalation and session termination. Each recovery strategy may specify a fallback strategy and an attempt limit. When a primary strategy is exhausted, the executor follows the fallback chain, ensuring graceful degradation from automated correction to human intervention or session termination.

恢复是映射 $R: (I_{soft} \cup G_{soft}) \times S \to A^*$ 的操作实现。ContractSpec 定义了按升级严重程度组织的恢复类型分类体系，从轻量级提示修改到降低自主性、人工升级和会话终止。每个恢复策略可以指定一个后备策略和尝试次数上限。当主策略耗尽时，执行器遵循后备链，确保从自动纠正到人工干预或会话终止的优雅降级。

7 实验

  3. E3: Governance Under Stress (Section 7.5) — adversarial prompt injection to test contract resilience under active attack.

7.1 Evaluation Methodology

7.1 评估方法

Before presenting individual experiments, we describe the evaluation methodology that governs all four studies. The methodology addresses five concerns: fair experimental controls (Section 7.1.1), principled evaluation methods (Section 7.1.2), rigorous statistical analysis (Section 7.1.3), multi-vendor model coverage (Section 7.1.4), and full reproducibility (Section 7.1.5). We also document an empirical finding regarding platform-level guardrail interference that influenced our experimental configuration (Section 7.1.6).

在介绍各实验之前，我们描述所有四项研究共同遵循的评估方法。该方法涉及五个方面：公平的实验对照（第 7.1.1 节）、有原则的评估方法（第 7.1.2 节）、严格的统计分析（第 7.1.3 节）、多厂商模型覆盖（第 7.1.4 节）和完整的可复现性（第 7.1.5 节）。我们还记录了关于平台级护栏干扰的实证发现，该发现影响了我们的实验配置（第 7.1.6 节）。

7.1.1 Experimental Controls

7.1.1 实验对照设计

We impose four experimental controls (F1–F4) to ensure observed differences are attributable to contract enforcement. F1: Fair comparison—contracted agents see every constraint injected into the system prompt; uncontracted agents receive only domain context. F2: Real recovery—soft violations trigger genuine LLM re-prompting, not post-hoc metric manipulation. F3: Same evaluator—both conditions use the identical constraint evaluator. F4: True ablation—the ablation study uses structurally different contracts, not post-hoc metric masking.

我们施加四个实验控制（F1–F4）以确保观测到的差异归因于契约强制执行。F1：公平比较——有契约的智能体在系统提示中看到每一条约束；无契约的智能体只收到领域上下文。F2：真实恢复——软违规触发真实的 LLM 重新提示，而非事后指标操控。F3：相同评估器——两种条件使用相同的约束评估器实例。F4：真实消融——消融研究使用结构不同的契约，而非事后指标掩盖。

7.1.2 Evaluation Methods

7.1.2 评估方法

We employ a three-tiered evaluation strategy: an LLM-based judge as the primary evaluator, heuristic extraction as a secondary evaluator for ablation purposes, and human annotation as ground truth for judge calibration.

我们采用三层评估策略：基于 LLM 的评判模型作为主评估器，启发式提取作为消融目的的辅助评估器，人工标注作为评判校准的真值。

Primary: LLM-as-Judge. All constraint evaluations in E1–E4 are performed by a GPT-4o-mini judge model using structured JSON output. For each conversational turn, the judge receives the agent's response and the full set of 12 constraints, and returns a per-constraint structured evaluation: {"constraint_id": "...", "satisfied": true|false, "confidence": 0.0–1.0, "evidence": "...", "reasoning": "..."}. All 12 constraints are assessed in a single judge call per turn (batch evaluation).

主评估器：LLM 即评判。 E1–E4 中所有约束评估均由 GPT-4o-mini 评判模型使用结构化 JSON 输出执行。对于每个对话轮次，评判接收智能体响应和全部 12 个约束，并返回每个约束的结构化评估：{"constraint_id": "...", "satisfied": true|false, "confidence": 0.0–1.0, "evidence": "...", "reasoning": "..."}。所有 12 个约束在每轮次的单次评判调用中批量评估。

Ground truth: Human annotation. To calibrate judge reliability, we conduct a human annotation study on a stratified sample of 100 sessions (25 per model, drawn from 4 of the 7 models in E1), yielding 7,200 constraint-level judgments. Three annotators evaluate each turn independently:

  1. A human domain expert with financial regulatory knowledge.
  2. The GPT-4o-mini judge model (the primary evaluator used in E1–E4).
  3. A Claude Haiku judge model (an independent second LLM judge for cross-model agreement).

真值：人工标注。 为校准评判可靠性，我们对 100 个会话进行人工标注研究，产生 7,200 个约束级判断。三位标注者独立评估每个轮次：

  1. 具有金融法规知识的人类领域专家。
  2. GPT-4o-mini 评判模型（E1–E4 的主评估器）。
  3. Claude Haiku 评判模型（跨模型一致性的独立第二 LLM 评判）。

7.1.3 Statistical Methodology

7.1.3 统计方法

Primary evaluation uses a GPT-4o-mini judge with structured JSON output. Human annotation on a stratified sample of 100 sessions confirms judge reliability (Cohen's $\kappa \geq 0.75$ target). All statistical comparisons use Welch's t-test with Bonferroni correction ($\alpha_{adj} = 0.01$). Cohen's $d$ effect sizes accompany all pairwise comparisons.

主要评估使用带有结构化 JSON 输出的 GPT-4o-mini 裁判。对 100 个会话的分层样本进行人工标注，确认裁判可靠性（目标 Cohen's $\kappa \geq 0.75$）。所有统计比较使用带 Bonferroni 校正（$\alpha_{adj} = 0.01$）的 Welch t 检验。所有两两比较均附有 Cohen's $d$ 效应量。

7.1.6 Platform Guardrail Interference

7.1.6 平台护栏干扰

During execution, we discovered that platform-level content safety guardrails interact non-trivially with application-level behavioral contracts. 7.3.1 The Transparency Effect

7.3.1 透明性效应

The most striking result is what we term the transparency effect: contracted agents exhibit lower soft compliance ($C^C_{soft} = 0.829$ mean) than uncontracted agents ($C^U_{soft} = 0.982$ mean). This is not regression—it is the central finding. Uncontracted agents have no soft constraints to violate: without a contract, there is no specification against which soft behavior can be measured. Contracted agents make violations visible: the runtime monitor surfaces an average of 6.15 soft violations per session that would otherwise go undetected. The value of ABC contracts is not that they eliminate violations, but that they make violations measurable.

表 9 中最引人注目的结果乍看之下似乎自相矛盾：有契约的智能体展现出更低的软合规率（$C^C_{soft}$ 均值 0.829）vs. 无契约智能体（$C^U_{soft}$ 均值 0.982）。我们将此称为透明性效应，这是本工作的核心发现。无契约的智能体没有软约束可违反：没有契约就没有度量软行为的规约。有契约的智能体使违规可见：运行时监控器平均每会话检测到 6.15 个否则不会被发现的软违规。ABC 契约的价值不在于消除违规，而在于使违规可量化。

7.3.2 Hard Compliance

7.3.2 硬合规性

Hard compliance is uniformly high across all models and both conditions. Five of seven models achieve $C_{hard} \geq 0.989$ in contracted mode; GPT-5.2 and GPT-4o-mini achieve perfect hard compliance ($C_{hard} = 1.000$) in both conditions. This suggests that frontier LLMs already internalize basic hard safety constraints from alignment training.

硬合规率在所有模型和两种条件下均保持一致的高水平。7 个模型中有 5 个在有契约模式下实现 $C_{hard} \geq 0.989$；GPT-5.2 和 GPT-4o-mini 在两种条件下均实现完美硬合规（$C_{hard} = 1.000$）。这表明前沿 LLM 已通过对齐训练内化了基本硬安全约束。

The primary exception is Mistral Large 3, which exhibits the lowest hard compliance in both contracted ($C^C_{hard} = 0.882$) and uncontracted ($C^U_{hard} = 0.838$) modes. The +4.5 percentage point improvement under contract enforcement is statistically significant ($p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $d = 1.69$), demonstrating that even for safety-critical hard constraints, runtime enforcement provides measurable benefit for models with weaker alignment.

主要例外是 Mistral Large 3，在有契约（$C^C_{hard} = 0.882$）和无契约（$C^U_{hard} = 0.838$）模式下均表现出最低的硬合规率。契约强制执行下 +4.5 个百分点的改善具有统计显著性（$p < 0.0001$，Cohen's $d = 1.69$），表明即使对于安全关键的硬约束，运行时强制执行也能为对齐较弱的模型提供可测量的收益。

7.6.3 Interpreting the Θ Paradox

7.6.3 解读 Θ 悖论

The most important interpretive caveat in Table 13 is that the Hard Only and Drift Only conditions report higher $\Theta$ than Full ABC. This is not a deficiency of the full framework; it is a direct consequence of how $\Theta$ is defined.

表 13 中最重要的解释注意事项是，仅硬约束和仅漂移条件报告的 $\Theta$ 高于完整 ABC。这不是完整框架的缺陷；这是 $\Theta$ 定义方式的直接后果。

When soft constraints are removed from the contract, there are no soft constraints to violate, so $C_{soft} = 1.0$ vacuously, inflating $\Theta$ by eliminating the penalty from soft non-compliance. The same logic applies to the Drift Only condition.

当软约束从契约中移除时，没有软约束可以违反，因此 $C_{soft} = 1.0$ 空洞成立，通过消除软不合规惩罚使 $\Theta$ 虚高。相同逻辑适用于仅漂移条件。

The ablation does not show that removing soft constraints improves reliability. It shows that removing the measurement of soft compliance produces a higher score by eliminating the metric that detects violations. This is precisely analogous to the E1 transparency effect: less monitoring produces better-looking numbers, not better behavior.

消融不表明移除软约束提高了可靠性。它表明移除软合规的测量通过消除检测违规的指标产生了更高分数。这与 E1 透明性效应完全类似：更少的监控产生看起来更好的数字，而非更好的行为。

The meaningful comparisons are therefore those where removing a component produces $\Theta$ degradation: the Soft Only and No Recovery conditions.

因此有意义的比较是那些移除组件产生 $\Theta$ 退化的条件：仅软约束和无恢复条件。

The Hard Only and Drift Only conditions report higher $\Theta$ than Full ABC because removing soft constraints causes $C_{soft} = 1.0$ vacuously, inflating $\Theta$. The ablation does not show that removing soft constraints improves reliability—it shows that removing the measurement of soft compliance produces a better-looking number. Recovery and soft constraints are the dominant contributors to $\Theta$: removing recovery (No Recovery) or monitoring soft constraints without recovery (Soft Only) produces mean $\Delta\Theta \approx -0.20$, consistent across all models. Detection without correction is equivalent to not monitoring soft behavior at all.

仅硬约束和仅漂移条件报告的 $\Theta$ 高于完整 ABC，这是因为移除软约束导致 $C_{soft} = 1.0$（因无软约束可违反而为 1），从而虚增了 $\Theta$。消融不是表明移除软约束改善了可靠性，而是表明移除软合规的度量产生了更好看的数字。恢复和软约束是 $\Theta$ 的主导贡献者：移除恢复（无恢复）或在无恢复的情况下监控软约束（仅软约束）产生均值 $\Delta\Theta \approx -0.20$，在所有模型中保持一致。没有纠正的检测等同于根本不监控软行为。

7.6.4 Key Findings

7.6.4 关键发现

Finding 1: Recovery and soft constraints are the dominant contributors to $\Theta$. Across all 4 models, removing recovery mechanisms (No Recovery) or soft constraint monitoring (Soft Only) produces the largest $\Theta$ drops. The mean $\Theta$ drop when recovery is disabled is $-0.209$ (±0.007); the mean drop in the Soft Only condition is $-0.200$ (±0.006). A $\Theta$ reduction of ∼0.20 represents a shift from "reliably governed" ($\Theta > 0.90$) to "partially governed" ($\Theta \approx 0.72$).

发现1：恢复和软约束是 $\Theta$ 的主要贡献者。 在所有 4 个模型中，移除恢复机制（无恢复）或软约束监控（仅软约束）产生最大的 $\Theta$ 下降。禁用恢复时的平均 $\Theta$ 下降为 $-0.209$（±0.007）；仅软约束条件下的平均下降为 $-0.200$（±0.006）。约 0.20 的 $\Theta$ 降低代表从"可靠治理"（$\Theta > 0.90$）到"部分治理"（$\Theta \approx 0.72$）的转变。

Finding 2: The $\Theta$ drop is remarkably consistent across models. The cross-model standard deviation of $\Delta\Theta$ for both degrading conditions is $< 0.01$:

  • Soft Only: $\Delta\Theta$ ranges from $-0.192$ (Mistral Large 3) to $-0.208$ (GPT-5.2).
  • No Recovery: $\Delta\Theta$ ranges from $-0.199$ (Llama 3.3 70B) to $-0.215$ (Mistral Large 3).

发现2：$\Theta$ 下降在各模型间具有显著一致性。 两种退化条件的跨模型 $\Delta\Theta$ 标准差均 $< 0.01$：

  • 仅软约束：$\Delta\Theta$ 从 $-0.192$（Mistral Large 3）到 $-0.208$（GPT-5.2）。
  • 无恢复：$\Delta\Theta$ 从 $-0.199$（Llama 3.3 70B）到 $-0.215$（Mistral Large 3）。

Finding 3: Recovery contributes the largest marginal improvement. The No Recovery condition produces the largest $\Theta$ degradation for 3 of 4 models. Mistral Large 3 — the model with the weakest baseline alignment — shows the largest recovery contribution ($\Delta\Theta = -0.215$), consistent with the theoretical prediction that recovery has the greatest marginal impact on high-drift agents (Theorem 4.3).

发现3：恢复贡献最大的边际改善。 无恢复条件在 4 个模型中的 3 个产生最大的 $\Theta$ 退化。Mistral Large 3——基线对齐最弱的模型——表现出最大的恢复贡献（$\Delta\Theta = -0.215$），与定理 4.3 预测恢复对高漂移智能体具有最大边际影响的理论预测一致。

Finding 4: Hard constraints maintain safety independently. In the Hard Only condition, all models retain their $C_{hard}$ scores from Full ABC (within ±0.001), confirming that hard constraint enforcement does not depend on the presence of soft constraints or recovery mechanisms.

发现4：硬约束独立维持安全性。 在仅硬约束条件下，所有模型保留了完整 ABC 中的 $C_{hard}$ 分数（误差 ±0.001），证实硬约束强制执行不依赖于软约束或恢复机制的存在。

Finding 5: Drift monitoring operates independently of constraints. The Drift Only condition produces $\bar{D}$ values identical to all other conditions for each model (GPT-5.2: 0.084; Claude Opus 4.6: 0.121; Llama 3.3 70B: 0.056; Mistral Large 3: 0.153). This confirms that the JSD-based drift computation operates on the raw action distribution regardless of whether constraints are enforced.

发现5：漂移监控独立于约束运行。 仅漂移条件对每个模型产生与所有其他条件相同的 $\bar{D}$ 值（GPT-5.2: 0.084；Claude Opus 4.6: 0.121；Llama 3.3 70B: 0.056；Mistral Large 3: 0.153）。这证实基于 JSD 的漂移计算在原始动作分布上运行，无论约束是否被强制执行。

7.6.5 Component Interaction Analysis

7.6.5 组件交互分析

The ablation results reveal a critical architectural property of ABC: the components interact multiplicatively, not additively. Consider the two degrading conditions:

  • Soft Only (removes hard constraints + recovery): soft violations are detected (∼6 per session) but never corrected. $\Theta$ drops by ∼0.20.
  • No Recovery (removes recovery only): both hard and soft violations are detected ($C_{hard}$ and $C_{soft}$ remain measurable) but no corrective action is taken. $\Theta$ drops by ∼0.21.

消融结果揭示了 ABC 的关键架构属性：组件以乘积方式而非加法方式交互。考虑两种退化条件：

  • 仅软约束（移除硬约束+恢复）：软违规被检测（每会话约 6 次）但从不纠正。$\Theta$ 下降约 0.20。
  • 无恢复（仅移除恢复）：硬和软违规均被检测（$C_{hard}$ 和 $C_{soft}$ 保持可测量），但不采取纠正动作。$\Theta$ 下降约 0.21。

The practical implication: ABC contracts should always include recovery strategies alongside soft constraints. Detection without correction leaves $\Theta$ at the same level as not monitoring soft behavior at all.

实践含义：ABC 契约应始终在软约束旁边包含恢复策略。检测而不纠正使 $\Theta$ 与根本不监控软行为处于相同水平。

7.6.6 Statistical Considerations

7.6.6 统计注意事项

All E4 comparisons use independent sessions (30 per condition per model) with structurally different contracts. The within-condition variance is low: $\Theta$ standard deviations range from 0.002 (GPT-5.2, Full ABC) to 0.046 (Llama 70B, Soft Only). The $\Delta\Theta$ values of ∼0.20 far exceed within-condition variability, producing large effect sizes (Cohen's $d > 10$ for all degrading comparisons).

所有 E4 比较使用独立会话（每个条件每个模型 30 个），采用结构上不同的契约。条件内方差较低：$\Theta$ 标准差从 0.002（GPT-5.2，完整 ABC）到 0.046（Llama 70B，仅软约束）。约 0.20 的 $\Delta\Theta$ 值远超条件内变异性，产生大效应量（所有退化比较的 Cohen's $d > 10$）。

Because the ablation conditions are not pairwise-independent (they share the same task set and model), we do not report Bonferroni-corrected $p$-values for the ablation comparisons. Instead, we emphasize practical significance: a $\Theta$ drop of 0.20 is an order of magnitude larger than the measurement noise ($\sigma_{\Theta} < 0.02$), and is consistent across all 4 models.

由于消融条件并非成对独立（它们共享相同的任务集和模型），我们不报告消融比较的 Bonferroni 校正 $p$ 值。相反，我们强调实践显著性：0.20 的 $\Theta$ 下降比测量噪声（$\sigma_{\Theta} < 0.02$）大一个数量级，且在所有 4 个模型中保持一致。

8 Discussion

8 讨论

8.1 Interpretation of Key Findings

8.1 关键发现的解读

The transparency effect is the most striking empirical result: contracts surface approximately 5.2–6.8 soft constraint violations per session that would otherwise pass unnoticed. This finding has a direct analogy in software engineering: introducing a test suite does not cause bugs—it reveals bugs that already existed. Similarly, introducing behavioral contracts does not degrade agent performance; it reveals performance gaps that were always present but previously unobservable. You cannot govern what you cannot measure, and contracts provide the measurement apparatus.

透明性效应是最引人注目的实证结果：来自 6 家厂商的全部 7 个模型中，有契约的智能体每会话检测到约 5.2–6.8 个无契约智能体完全漏报的软约束违规。这一发现在软件工程中有直接类比：引入测试套件不会导致 bug，它揭示了本已存在的 bug。类似地，引入行为契约不会降低智能体性能；它揭示了本已存在但先前不可观测的性能缺口。你无法治理你无法量化的东西，契约提供了量化的装置。

Hard constraint compliance was high across all models, with contracted agents achieving $C_{hard} \geq 0.88$. This suggests that frontier LLMs have internalized many safety-critical behaviors through training-time alignment. However, the small but nonzero hard violation rate in weaker models indicates that training-time alignment alone is insufficient for zero-tolerance deployment scenarios. The ABC framework provides the additional enforcement layer needed to close this gap.

所有模型的硬约束合规率均保持较高水平，有契约的智能体实现 $C_{hard} \geq 0.88$。这表明前沿 LLM 已通过训练时对齐内化了许多安全关键行为。然而，较弱模型中小但非零的硬违规率表明，对于要求零容忍安全约束的部署场景，仅靠训练时对齐是不够的。ABC 框架提供了填补这一空缺所需的额外强制执行层。

The $(p, \delta, k)$-satisfaction framework translates the transparency effect into an operationally useful governance primitive. An enterprise deploying a financial advisory agent can now specify testable requirements—e.g., $p \geq 0.99$, $\delta = 0.10$, $k = 3$—and the contract design criterion (21) tells the deployer exactly what recovery rate $\gamma$ is needed. This closes the loop between governance requirements and engineering implementation.

$(p, \delta, k)$-满足性框架将透明性效应转化为操作上有用的治理原语。企业现在可以规定可测试的需求——如 $p \geq 0.99$、$\delta = 0.10$、$k = 3$——契约设计准则告诉部署者实现规约所需的精确恢复率 $\gamma$。这闭合了治理需求与工程实现之间的环路。

8.2 Limitations

8.2 局限性

We identify six limitations: (L1) State dictionary assumption—the evaluator requires pre-computed feature fields in a structured state dictionary. (L2) Reference distribution calibration—the distributional drift component requires dedicated calibration runs before deployment, with no automated tooling for recalibration. (L3) Recovery is monitoring by default—out-of-the-box, AgentAssert detects violations but does not correct them without custom recovery handler registration. (L4) $k$-window stationarity assumption—the drift bounds theorem relies on the stationary distribution assumption, which may not hold for short sessions. (L5) Compositionality under correlated failures—the probability bound becomes optimistic when agents share the same underlying LLM. (L6) Benchmark circularity—AgentContract-Bench evaluates engine consistency rather than behavioral detection accuracy on live agents.

我们识别出六个局限性：（L1）状态字典假设——评估器需要结构化状态字典中预计算的特征字段。（L2）参考分布标定——分布漂移组件需要在部署前进行专门的标定运行，没有自动化的重标定工具。（L3）恢复默认为监控——开箱即用时，AgentAssert 检测违规但不纠正，除非注册自定义恢复处理器。（L4）$k$-窗口平稳性假设——漂移界定定理依赖平稳分布假设，对短会话可能不成立。（L5）相关失败下的可组合性——当智能体共享同一 LLM 时，概率界变得乐观。（L6）基准循环性——AgentContract-Bench 评估引擎一致性而非对真实智能体行为的检测准确率。

8.3 Threats to Validity

8.3 效度威胁

Internal validity: LLM API responses are non-deterministic; we mitigate this with 30 sessions per condition and Welch's t-tests. Temperature effects and prompt sensitivity remain concerns. External validity: experiments use 10 financial advisory tasks over 6-turn sessions; generalizability to other domains is not fully established empirically. Construct validity: metrics are contract-relative—they measure compliance with specified constraints, not absolute agent quality.

内部效度：LLM API 响应是不确定性的；我们通过每条件 30 个会话和 Welch t 检验来缓解这一问题。温度效应和提示敏感性仍是隐患。外部效度：实验使用 10 个金融咨询任务的 6 轮会话；对其他领域的泛化能力尚未充分实证建立。构念效度：指标是相对于契约的——它们衡量对规约约束的合规性，而非绝对的智能体质量。

8.4 Broader Impact

8.4 更广泛的影响

Quantifiable AI governance: ABC enables continuous compliance monitoring for regulated industries, creating a formal link between regulatory intent and technical implementation. Relationship to training-time alignment: ABC is complementary to Constitutional AI and RLHF. Training-time alignment reduces drift rate $\alpha$; runtime contracts increase recovery rate $\gamma$; the equilibrium drift $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$ benefits from both. Potential misuse: contracts carry a risk of creating a false sense of security—a contract with few shallow constraints may show high compliance while leaving many behaviors unmonitored. Relationship to AI safety: runtime enforcement addresses the question of ensuring that agents do behave well—regardless of whether their internal representations are aligned or interpretable.

可量化的 AI 治理：ABC 为受监管行业提供持续合规监控，在监管意图与技术实现之间建立形式化联系。与训练时对齐的关系：ABC 与 Constitutional AI 和 RLHF 互补。训练时对齐降低漂移率 $\alpha$；运行时契约提高恢复率 $\gamma$；均衡漂移 $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$ 受益于两者。潜在滥用：契约存在产生虚假安全感的风险——约束少且浅薄的契约可能展现高合规率，而许多行为仍未被监控。与 AI 安全社区的关系：运行时强制执行解决了确保智能体确实表现良好的问题——无论其内部表征是否对齐或可解释。

9 Conclusion

9 结论

We have presented Agent Behavioral Contracts (ABC), a formal framework that brings Design-by-Contract principles to autonomous AI agents. The contract tuple $C = (P, I_{hard}, I_{soft}, G_{hard}, G_{soft}, R)$ distinguishes hard constraints from soft constraints, paired with a recovery mechanism that transforms exponential compliance decay into linear decay. The $(p, \delta, k)$-satisfaction definition provides a probabilistic notion of contract compliance connecting agent behavioral specification to established PCTL model-checking semantics.

我们提出了 Agent Behavioral Contracts（ABC），一个将按契约设计原则引入自主 AI 智能体的形式化框架。契约元组 $C = (P, I_{hard}, I_{soft}, G_{hard}, G_{soft}, R)$ 将硬约束与软约束区分开来，配合将指数合规衰减转变为线性衰减的恢复机制。$(p, \delta, k)$-满足性定义将智能体行为规约与已建立的 PCTL 模型检验语义相连接。

Summary of contributions:

  1. Hard/soft constraint separation enabling nuanced governance.
  2. Behavioral drift detection via the composite drift score $D(t)$ grounded in an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck model, with drift bounded to $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$ by the Stochastic Drift Bound Theorem.
  3. Real recovery via LLM re-prompting achieving measurable compliance restoration.
  4. Compositionality theorems establishing end-to-end guarantees for multi-agent chains with quantified reliability degradation bounds.
  5. SPRT certification providing statistically principled agent compliance decisions.
  6. ContractSpec and AgentAssert providing a declarative DSL and production-grade enforcement runtime with sub-10ms overhead.

贡献摘要：

  2. 行为漂移检测，通过基于 Ornstein–Uhlenbeck 模型的综合漂移评分 $D(t)$ 实现，随机漂移界定定理证明漂移被界定在 $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$。
  3. 真实恢复，通过 LLM 重新提示实现可量化的合规恢复。
  4. 可组合性定理，为多智能体链建立端到端保证，附量化可靠性退化界。
  5. SPRT 认证，为智能体合规决策提供统计上严格的停止规则。

The core thesis of this paper is that autonomous AI agents require the same principled behavioral specification and runtime enforcement that traditional software has relied on for decades. Prompts are not contracts. Trust is not governance. Agent Behavioral Contracts close this gap: they make agent behavior formally specifiable, continuously measurable, and provably bounded—turning the current practice of "deploy and hope" into the engineering discipline of "specify, monitor, and enforce."

A.1 Full Proof of the Stochastic Drift Bounds Theorem

A.1 随机漂移界定理的完整证明

We prove each part of the Stochastic Drift Bounds Theorem through a sequence of increasingly general arguments: a deterministic warm-up via Lyapunov theory, the stochastic extension via Itô calculus, ergodicity via the Foster–Lyapunov criterion, and finally the contract design criterion.

我们通过一系列递进的论证来证明随机漂移界定理的每个部分：通过 Lyapunov 理论的确定性热身、通过 Itô 微积分的随机扩展、通过 Foster–Lyapunov 准则的遍历性，以及最后的契约设计准则。

A.1.1 Deterministic Case (Warm-up)

A.1.1 确定性情形（热身）

Consider the deterministic drift dynamics:

$$\frac{dD}{dt} = \alpha - \gamma D(t), \quad D(0) = D_0 \geq 0,$$
(32)

where $\alpha > 0$ is the drift injection rate and $\gamma > 0$ is the mean-reversion strength.

考虑确定性漂移动态：

$$\frac{dD}{dt} = \alpha - \gamma D(t), \quad D(0) = D_0 \geq 0$$
(32)

其中 $\alpha > 0$ 为漂移注入率，$\gamma > 0$ 为均值回归强度。

Lemma A.1 (Deterministic Stability). The equilibrium $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$ of (32) is globally asymptotically stable, with explicit convergence $|D(t) - \alpha/\gamma| = |D_0 - \alpha/\gamma| e^{-\gamma t}$.
引理 A.1（确定性稳定性）。 均衡点 $D^* = \alpha/\gamma$ 全局渐近稳定，显式收敛：$|D(t) - \alpha/\gamma| = |D_0 - \alpha/\gamma| e^{-\gamma t}$。

Proof. Define the error variable $e(t) := D(t) - D^* = D(t) - \alpha/\gamma$. Substituting into (32): $\frac{de}{dt} = \alpha - \gamma(e + D^*) = -\gamma e$. The Lyapunov function $V(e) = e^2$ satisfies $V(0) = 0$, $V(e) > 0$ for $e \neq 0$, and $\frac{dV}{dt} = 2e(-\gamma e) = -2\gamma e^2 = -2\gamma V(e) \leq 0$. Global asymptotic stability follows by the Lyapunov stability theorem. $\square$

证明。 定义误差 $e(t) := D(t) - D^*$，则 $\frac{de}{dt} = -\gamma e$。Lyapunov 函数 $V(e) = e^2$ 满足 $\frac{dV}{dt} = -2\gamma V(e) \leq 0$，全局渐近稳定性成立。$\square$

A.1.2 Stochastic Extension via Itô Calculus

A.1.2 通过 Itô 微积分的随机扩展

Theorem A.2 (Stochastic Drift Bounds — Mean-Square Convergence). Consider the stochastic drift dynamics $dD = (\alpha - \gamma D)\,dt + \sigma\,dW(t)$ (equation 34). Then: (i) the mean-square error satisfies equation (35); (ii) as $t \to \infty$, $\mathbb{E}[(D(t) - \alpha/\gamma)^2] \to \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$; (iii) the convergence rate to the stationary variance is $2\gamma$.
定理 A.2（随机漂移界——均方收敛）。 对随机漂移动态 $dD = (\alpha - \gamma D)\,dt + \sigma\,dW(t)$（方程 34）：(i) 均方误差满足方程 (35)；(ii) $t \to \infty$ 时收敛到 $\sigma^2/(2\gamma)$；(iii) 收敛速率为 $2\gamma$。

Proof. Define $e(t) := D(t) - \alpha/\gamma$, so $de = -\gamma e\,dt + \sigma\,dW$. Apply Itô's formula to $V(e) = e^2$:

$$dV = 2e(-\gamma e\,dt + \sigma\,dW) + \sigma^2\,dt = (-2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2)\,dt + 2\sigma e\,dW.$$
(37)

证明。 定义误差过程 $e(t) := D(t) - \alpha/\gamma$，则 $de = -\gamma e\,dt + \sigma\,dW$。对 $V(e) = e^2$ 应用 Itô 公式：

$$dV = 2e(-\gamma e\,dt + \sigma\,dW) + \sigma^2\,dt = (-2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2)\,dt + 2\sigma e\,dW.$$
(37)

The infinitesimal generator is $\mathcal{L}V(e) = -2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2$. Taking expectations (the stochastic integral vanishes as a martingale):

$$\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}[V(t)] = -2\gamma\mathbb{E}[V(t)] + \sigma^2.$$
(39)

无穷小生成元为 $\mathcal{L}V(e) = -2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2$。取期望（随机积分作为鞅其期望为零）：

$$\frac{d}{dt}\mathbb{E}[V(t)] = -2\gamma\mathbb{E}[V(t)] + \sigma^2.$$
(39)

Solving via the integrating factor $e^{2\gamma t}$: $\frac{d}{dt}[e^{2\gamma t}\mathbb{E}[V(t)]] = \sigma^2 e^{2\gamma t}$. Integrating and solving for $\mathbb{E}[V(t)]$ yields (35). As $t \to \infty$, $\mathbb{E}[V(\infty)] = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$, establishing parts (ii), (iii), and (v) of the main theorem. $\square$

利用积分因子 $e^{2\gamma t}$：$\frac{d}{dt}[e^{2\gamma t}\mathbb{E}[V(t)]] = \sigma^2 e^{2\gamma t}$。积分并求解 $\mathbb{E}[V(t)]$ 得方程 (35)。当 $t \to \infty$ 时，$\mathbb{E}[V(\infty)] = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$，证明了主定理的 (ii)、(iii) 和 (v) 部分。$\square$

A.1.3 Ergodicity via the Foster–Lyapunov Criterion

A.1.3 通过 Foster–Lyapunov 准则的遍历性

Theorem A.3 (Foster–Lyapunov Criterion). Let $\{X(t)\}_{t \geq 0}$ be a continuous-time Markov process on $\mathbb{R}^d$ with infinitesimal generator $\mathcal{L}$. Suppose there exist $V: \mathbb{R}^d \to [1,\infty)$, constants $\lambda > 0$, $b \geq 0$, and a compact set $C$ such that:

$$\mathcal{L}V(x) \leq -\lambda V(x) + b \quad \text{for all } x \in \mathbb{R}^d.$$
(40)

Then $\{X(t)\}$ possesses a unique stationary distribution $\pi$.

定理 A.3（Foster–Lyapunov 准则）。 若存在 $V \geq 1$ 使得 $\mathcal{L}V(x) \leq -\lambda V(x) + b$（方程 40），则马尔可夫过程具有唯一平稳分布 $\pi$。
Proposition A.4 (Ergodicity of the Drift Process). The stochastic drift process (34) admits a unique stationary distribution $\pi$ satisfying $\mathbb{E}_\pi[e^2] \leq \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$.
命题 A.4（漂移过程的遍历性）。 随机漂移过程 (34) 具有满足 $\mathbb{E}_\pi[e^2] \leq \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$ 的唯一平稳分布 $\pi$。

Proof. Define $\tilde{V}(e) = e^2 + 1$. Then $\mathcal{L}\tilde{V}(e) = -2\gamma e^2 + \sigma^2 = -2\gamma(\tilde{V}(e) - 1) + \sigma^2 = -2\gamma\tilde{V}(e) + (2\gamma + \sigma^2)$. This satisfies (40) globally with $\lambda = 2\gamma$, $b = 2\gamma + \sigma^2$. By Theorem A.3, a unique stationary distribution $\pi$ exists. The tighter bound $\mathbb{E}_\pi[e^2] = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$ follows directly from Theorem A.2. $\square$

证明。 令 $\tilde{V}(e) = e^2 + 1$，则 $\mathcal{L}\tilde{V}(e) = -2\gamma\tilde{V}(e) + (2\gamma + \sigma^2)$，满足 $\lambda = 2\gamma$，$b = 2\gamma + \sigma^2$ 的 Foster–Lyapunov 条件，唯一平稳分布存在。$\square$

A.1.4 Gaussian Tail Bound

A.1.4 高斯尾部界

Proposition A.5 (Stationary Tail Probability). Under the stationary distribution, the drift exceeds threshold $\alpha/\gamma + \eta$ with probability:

$$P_\pi(D > \alpha/\gamma + \eta) \leq \exp\!\left(-\frac{\gamma\eta^2}{\sigma^2}\right).$$
(41)
命题 A.5（平稳尾部概率）。 在平稳分布下：$P_\pi(D > \alpha/\gamma + \eta) \leq \exp(-\gamma\eta^2/\sigma^2)$（方程 41）。

Proof. The stationary distribution is $\pi_D = \mathcal{N}(\alpha/\gamma, \sigma^2/(2\gamma))$. For $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, s^2)$: $P(X > \mu + \eta) \leq \exp(-\eta^2/(2s^2))$. Substituting $s^2 = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$ yields the bound. $\square$

证明。 平稳分布 $\pi_D = \mathcal{N}(\alpha/\gamma, \sigma^2/(2\gamma))$，代入高斯尾部界 $P(X > \mu+\eta) \leq \exp(-\eta^2/(2s^2))$，令 $s^2 = \sigma^2/(2\gamma)$ 即得。$\square$

A.1.5 Contract Design Criterion

A.1.5 契约设计准则

Proposition A.6 (Minimum Correction Strength). To guarantee $P_\pi(D > D_{max}) \leq \varepsilon$, it suffices that:

$$\gamma \geq \frac{\alpha}{D_{max}} + \sigma\sqrt{\frac{2\ln(1/\varepsilon)}{(2D_{max})^2}}.$$
(44)
命题 A.6（最小纠正强度）。 为保证 $P_\pi(D > D_{max}) \leq \varepsilon$，$\gamma$ 需满足方程 (44)。

Proof. From Proposition A.5 with $\eta = D_{max} - \alpha/\gamma$, setting $\exp(-\gamma\eta^2/\sigma^2) \leq \varepsilon$ and solving: $\gamma(D_{max} - \alpha/\gamma)^2 \geq \sigma^2 \ln(1/\varepsilon)$. Expanding and multiplying through by $\gamma > 0$ yields the quadratic $D_{max}^2\gamma^2 - (2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2\ln(1/\varepsilon))\gamma + \alpha^2 = 0$, from which the larger root is equation (21). $\square$

证明。 由命题 A.5 令 $\eta = D_{max} - \alpha/\gamma$，解 $\exp(-\gamma\eta^2/\sigma^2) \leq \varepsilon$ 展开得二次方程 $D_{max}^2\gamma^2 - (2\alpha D_{max} + \sigma^2\ln(1/\varepsilon))\gamma + \alpha^2 = 0$，取较大根得方程 (21)。$\square$

A.2 Proof of the Recovery Lemma

A.2 恢复引理的证明

Lemma A.7 (Recovery-Augmented Compliance). Let $q \in (0,1)$ denote the per-step compliance probability and $r \in [0,1]$ the recovery effectiveness. Then: (i) Without recovery: $P[\text{compliance over }T\text{ steps}] = q^T$. (ii) With recovery: $P[\text{recoverable compliance}] \geq 1 - T(1-q)(1-r)$.
引理 A.7（恢复增强合规）。 设 $q \in (0,1)$ 为每步合规概率，$r \in [0,1]$ 为恢复有效性。则：(i) 无恢复：$P[T \text{ 步合规}] = q^T$；(ii) 有恢复：$P[\text{可恢复合规}] \geq 1 - T(1-q)(1-r)$。

Proof. At each step $t$, define $V_t := \{C_{soft}(t) < 1 - \delta\}$ (violation) and $F_t := V_t \cap \{\text{recovery fails within }k\text{ steps}\}$ (unrecoverable failure). Part (i): Without recovery, compliance at all $T$ steps requires $V_t^c$ at every step, and by independence $P[\bigcap_t V_t^c] = q^T$. Part (ii): $P(F_t) = P(V_t) \cdot P(\text{recovery fails} | V_t) = (1-q)(1-r)$. By the union bound: $P(\exists t: F_t) \leq \sum_t P(F_t) = T(1-q)(1-r)$. Taking the complement gives $P(\text{soft guarantee holds}) \geq 1 - T(1-q)(1-r)$. $\square$

证明。 定义 $V_t$（违规事件）和 $F_t$（不可恢复失败）。第 (i) 部分：由独立性 $P[\bigcap_t V_t^c] = q^T$。第 (ii) 部分：$P(F_t) = (1-q)(1-r)$，联合界得 $P(\exists t: F_t) \leq T(1-q)(1-r)$，取补集得证。$\square$

Remark A.8 (Tightness of the Union Bound). The union bound is conservative because successful recovery at step $t$ increases compliance probability at step $t+1$ (negative autocorrelation). A tighter renewal-theoretic bound yields an expected violation fraction of $(1-q)\cdot\mathbb{E}[\tau_{recovery}] / (\mathbb{E}[\tau_{recovery}] + \mathbb{E}[\tau_{inter-violation}])$, which is tight as $T \to \infty$.
注释 A.8（联合界的紧密性）。 联合界保守，因成功恢复在步骤 $t$ 产生负自相关。更紧的更新理论界为 $(1-q)\cdot\mathbb{E}[\tau_{\text{恢复}}] / (\mathbb{E}[\tau_{\text{恢复}}] + \mathbb{E}[\tau_{\text{违规间隔}}])$，在 $T \to \infty$ 时紧。

A.3 Proof of the Compositionality Theorem

A.3 可组合性定理的证明

Theorem A.9 (Deterministic Contract Composition). Let $A \models C_A$ and $B \models C_B$. Under conditions (C1)–(C4), $\text{Chain}(A, B) \models C_{A\oplus B}$.
定理 A.9（确定性契约组合）。 设 $A \models C_A$，$B \models C_B$，在条件 (C1)–(C4) 下，$\text{Chain}(A,B) \models C_{A\oplus B}$。

Proof. We verify each component of $C_{A\oplus B}$:

  • Preconditions. $P_{A\oplus B} = P_A$ holds by assumption.
  • Pre/postcondition chaining. Since $A \models C_A$, $\text{PostCond}_A$ holds upon A's completion. By (C1), $I_{\text{handoff}}$ holds at the boundary. By (C2), $\text{PostCond}_A \wedge I_{\text{handoff}} \Rightarrow P_B$. Therefore $P_B$ holds and $B$ executes within $C_B$.
  • Invariant preservation. The composed invariant $I_A \wedge I_B \wedge I_{\text{handoff}}$: $I_A$ holds during A's execution by $A \models C_A$; $I_B$ holds during B's execution by $B \models C_B$; $I_{\text{handoff}}$ holds by (C1).
  • Governance respect. $G_{A\oplus B} = G_A \cup G_B$ is conflict-free by (C3). Both $G_A$ and $G_B$ are respected by the respective agents' contract satisfaction.
  • Recovery composition. By (C4), $R_A$ does not violate $P_B$ and $R_B$ does not violate $I_A$. The cascade recovery $R_{\text{cascade}}$ handles cross-boundary effects by construction.

证明。 逐一验证 $C_{A\oplus B}$ 的五个分量：

  • 前置条件。 $P_{A\oplus B} = P_A$ 由假设成立。
  • 前/后置条件链接。 由 (C1) $I_{\text{交接}}$ 在边界成立，由 (C2) $\text{PostCond}_A \wedge I_{\text{交接}} \Rightarrow P_B$，故 $B$ 以有效前置条件执行。
  • 不变量保持。 $I_A$、$I_B$、$I_{\text{交接}}$ 分别由 $A \models C_A$、$B \models C_B$、(C1) 保持。
  • 治理遵守。 $G_{A\oplus B} = G_A \cup G_B$ 由 (C3) 无冲突。
  • 恢复组合。 由 (C4) 级联恢复处理跨边界效应，$R_A$ 不违反 $P_B$，$R_B$ 不违反 $I_A$。

A.4 Proof of Probabilistic Compositionality

A.4 概率可组合性的证明

Theorem A.11 (Probabilistic Contract Composition). Suppose $A$ $(p_A, \delta_A)$-satisfies $C_A$, $B$ $(p_B, \delta_B)$-satisfies $C_B$, and the handoff succeeds with probability $p_h$ introducing deviation $\delta_h$. Then $\text{Chain}(A,B)$ $(p_{A\oplus B}, \delta_{A\oplus B})$-satisfies $C_{A\oplus B}$ with $p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h$ and $\delta_{A\oplus B} \leq \delta_A + \delta_B + \delta_h$.
定理 A.11（概率契约组合）。 设 $A$ $(p_A, \delta_A)$-满足 $C_A$，$B$ $(p_B, \delta_B)$-满足 $C_B$，交接成功概率 $p_h$，引入偏差 $\delta_h$。则链满足 $p_{A\oplus B} \geq p_A \cdot p_B \cdot p_h$，$\delta_{A\oplus B} \leq \delta_A + \delta_B + \delta_h$。

Proof. Define $E_A := \{A \text{ satisfies } C_A\}$, $E_B := \{B \text{ satisfies } C_B\}$, $E_h := \{\text{handoff succeeds}\}$. The chain succeeds iff $E_A \cap E_h \cap E_B$. By the chain rule and the conditional independence assumption: $P(E_A \cap E_h \cap E_B) = P(E_A) \cdot P(E_h | E_A) \cdot P(E_B | E_A \cap E_h) \geq p_A \cdot p_h \cdot p_B$. The deviation bound follows from sub-additivity of per-stage deviations. By induction, this extends to N-agent chains, yielding equations (47)–(48). $\square$

证明。 定义 $E_A := \{A \text{ 满足 } C_A\}$，$E_B := \{B \text{ 满足 } C_B\}$，$E_h := \{\text{交接成功}\}$。由链式法则和条件独立性：$P(E_A \cap E_h \cap E_B) \geq p_A \cdot p_h \cdot p_B$。偏差界由次可加性得出。对 N 个智能体的链归纳应用得方程 (47)–(48)。$\square$

Remark A.12 (Tightness and Practical Implications). The probability bound (45) is tight when events are independent, but conservative under positive correlation. The N-agent extension (47) reveals that reliability degrades multiplicatively with chain length, motivating checkpointing and recovery mechanisms at intermediate handoff points.
注释 A.12（紧密性与实践含义）。 概率界 (45) 在独立时是紧的，正相关下保守。N 智能体扩展 (47) 揭示可靠性随链长度乘积退化，激励在中间交接点使用检查点和恢复机制。

A.5 Sample Complexity for $(p, \delta, k)$-Satisfaction Certification

A.5 $(p, \delta, k)$-满足性认证的样本复杂度

Proposition A.13 (Hoeffding Baseline). To estimate compliance probability $p$ within additive error $\varepsilon$ with confidence $1 - \alpha$, the required sample size is:

$$n \geq \frac{1}{2\varepsilon^2} \ln\frac{2}{\alpha}.$$
(49)

For $\varepsilon = 0.01$ and $\alpha = 0.05$: $n \geq 18{,}445$ sessions.

命题 A.13（Hoeffding 基线）。 以误差 $\varepsilon$ 和置信度 $1-\alpha$ 估计合规概率 $p$，所需样本量为 $n \geq \frac{1}{2\varepsilon^2}\ln\frac{2}{\alpha}$（方程 49）。对 $\varepsilon = 0.01$，$\alpha = 0.05$：$n \geq 18{,}445$。

Proof. By Hoeffding's inequality on i.i.d. Bernoulli$(p)$ observations: $P(|\hat{p}_n - p| \geq \varepsilon) \leq 2\exp(-2n\varepsilon^2)$. Setting equal to $\alpha$ and solving for $n$ yields (49). $\square$

证明。 由 Hoeffding 不等式：$P(|\hat{p}_n - p| \geq \varepsilon) \leq 2\exp(-2n\varepsilon^2)$，令右端等于 $\alpha$ 解得方程 (49)。$\square$

Proposition A.14 (SPRT Improvement). Wald's Sequential Probability Ratio Test for $H_0: p \leq 0.90$ vs. $H_1: p \geq 0.95$ with Type I/II error rates $\alpha = \beta = 0.05$ requires approximately 150–300 sessions (expected), representing a $60\times$–$120\times$ reduction over the Hoeffding baseline.
命题 A.14（SPRT 改进）。 Wald SPRT 对 $H_0: p \leq 0.90$ vs $H_1: p \geq 0.95$（$\alpha = \beta = 0.05$）预期样本量约 150–300，比 Hoeffding 基线减少 $60\times$–$120\times$。

Proof sketch. The SPRT maintains log-likelihood ratio $\Lambda_n = \sum_{i=1}^n [X_i \ln(p_1/p_0) + (1-X_i)\ln((1-p_1)/(1-p_0))]$ and terminates when $\Lambda_n$ exits the continuation region $(\ln(\beta/(1-\alpha)), \ln((1-\beta)/\alpha))$. Under $H_1$, the expected increment per observation equals $\text{KL}(p_1 \| p_0) = 0.95\ln(0.95/0.90) + 0.05\ln(0.05/0.10) \approx 0.0167$ nats. Wald's approximation gives $\mathbb{E}_{p_1}[N] \approx 159$. The range 150–300 accounts for boundary overshoot and discrete-sample effects. The optimality of SPRT follows from the Wald–Wolfowitz theorem: no sequential test achieves lower expected sample size under both $H_0$ and $H_1$. $\square$

证明概要。 KL 散度 $\text{KL}(0.95 \| 0.90) \approx 0.0167$ nats，Wald 近似给出 $\mathbb{E}_{p_1}[N] \approx 159$。最优性由 Wald–Wolfowitz 定理保证。$\square$

Remark A.15 (Practical Certification Protocol). The SPRT reduction from $\sim$18,445 to $\sim$150–300 sessions makes runtime certification practical. In deployment, each agent interaction constitutes one Bernoulli trial, and $\Lambda_n$ is updated incrementally. When $\Lambda_n$ crosses the upper boundary, the agent is certified; crossing the lower boundary flags it for remediation.
注释 A.15（实用认证协议）。 从约 18,445 到约 150–300 个会话的减少使运行时认证实际可行。每次智能体交互构成一个 Bernoulli 试验，$\Lambda_n$ 增量更新；越过上界则认证通过，越过下界则标记需整改。
