Applying "Design by Contract"

应用"按契约设计"

Reliability is even more important in object-oriented programming than elsewhere. This article shows how to reduce bugs by building software components on the basis of carefully designed contracts.
可靠性在面向对象编程中比在其他场合更为重要。本文展示如何通过在精心设计的契约基础上构造软件部件来减少 bug。

As object-oriented techniques steadily gain ground in the world of software development, users and prospective users of these techniques are clamoring more and more loudly for a "methodology" of object-oriented software construction — or at least for some methodological guidelines. This article presents such guidelines, whose main goal is to help improve the reliability of software systems. Reliability is here defined as the combination of correctness and robustness or, more prosaically, as the absence of bugs.

随着面向对象技术在软件开发领域稳步占据阵地,这些技术的使用者和潜在使用者对面向对象软件构造的"方法论"—— 或至少是某种方法论指引 —— 的呼声越来越高。本文即提供这样的指引,其主要目标是帮助改善软件系统的可靠性。此处可靠性(Reliability)定义为正确性(correctness)与健壮性(robustness)之合,或者更通俗一点,就是没有 bug。

Everyone developing software systems, or just using them, knows how pressing this question of reliability is in the current state of software engineering. Yet the rapidly growing literature on object-oriented analysis, design, and programming includes remarkably few contributions on how to make object-oriented software more reliable. This is surprising and regrettable, since at least three reasons justify devoting particular attention to reliability in the context of object-oriented development:

凡是开发软件系统或仅仅是使用软件系统的人,都清楚可靠性这个问题在当下软件工程中有多迫切。然而,关于面向对象分析、设计和编程的文献虽然迅速增长,其中论及如何使面向对象软件更可靠的却少得令人意外 —— 也令人遗憾,因为至少有三个理由,使得我们应当在面向对象开发的语境下特别关注可靠性:

(1) The cornerstone of object-oriented technology is reuse. For reusable components, which may be used in thousands of different applications, the potential consequences of incorrect behavior are even more serious than for application-specific developments. (2) Proponents of object-oriented methods make strong claims about their beneficial effect on software quality. Reliability is certainly a central component of any reasonable definition of quality as applied to software. (3) The object-oriented approach, based on the theory of abstract data types, provides a particularly appropriate framework for discussing and enforcing reliability.

(1) 面向对象技术的基石是复用。对于可能被成千上万个不同应用使用的可复用部件来说,错误行为的潜在后果比应用专属开发严重得多。(2) 面向对象方法的提倡者对该方法在软件质量上的正面效应作出很强的主张。在任何对软件"质量"所下的合理定义中,可靠性都必定是核心组成部分。(3) 面向对象方法建立在抽象数据类型(abstract data types)理论之上,为讨论并强制可靠性提供了特别合适的框架。

The pragmatic techniques presented in this article, while certainly not providing infallible ways to guarantee reliability, may help considerably toward this goal. They rely on the theory of design by contract, which underlies the design of the Eiffel analysis, design, and programming language [1] and of the supporting libraries, from which a number of examples will be drawn.

本文给出的这些实用技术当然不是保证可靠性的万灵药,但朝这个目标可以有相当的帮助。它们依赖于按契约设计(design by contract)理论,这一理论也是 Eiffel 分析/设计/编程语言 [1] 及其配套库的设计基础,本文的诸多例子就取自这些库。

The contributions of the work reported below include (1) a coherent set of methodological principles helping to produce correct and robust software; (2) a systematic approach to the delicate problem of how to deal with abnormal cases, leading to a simple and powerful exception-handling mechanism; and (3) a better understanding of inheritance and of the associated techniques (redeclaration, polymorphism, and dynamic binding) through the notion of subcontract, allowing a systematic approach to using these powerful but sometimes dangerous mechanisms.

本文所述工作的贡献包括:(1) 一组有助于产出正确、健壮软件的、连贯的方法论原则(methodological principles);(2) 对如何处理异常情形这一棘手问题的系统化方法,由此得出一套简单而强大的异常处理(exception-handling)机制;(3) 通过转包(subcontract)这一概念,对继承(inheritance)及其相关技术(重新声明、多态、动态绑定)的更好理解,使这些强大但有时危险的机制能够被系统化地使用。

Most of the concepts presented here have appeared elsewhere. They were previewed in the book Object-Oriented Software Construction [2]; and a more complete exposition was presented in a recent book chapter [3], from which this article has been adapted. More profoundly, this work finds its root in earlier work on systematic program development [4],[5] and abstract data types [6]-[8]. This article focuses on the central ideas, introducing them concisely for direct application by developers.

本文呈现的大部分概念此前已在别处出现过。它们曾在《Object-Oriented Software Construction》[2] 一书中有预告;更完整的阐述收在近期的一篇专著章节 [3] 中,本文即由该章改编。更追本溯源地说,这项工作植根于早期在系统化程序开发 [4],[5] 和抽象数据类型 [6]-[8] 方面的工作。本文聚焦于其中的核心思想,以简洁的方式介绍出来,便于开发者直接应用。

Defensive programming revisited

重访防御式编程

Software engineering and programming methodology textbooks that discuss reliability often emphasize the technique known as defensive programming, which directs developers to protect every software module against the slings and arrows of outrageous fortune. In particular, this encourages programmers to include as many checks as possible, even if they are redundant with checks made by callers. Include them anyway, the advice goes; if they do not help, at least they will not harm.

讨论可靠性的软件工程与编程方法论教材往往会强调一种被称为防御式编程(defensive programming)的技术,它要求开发者保护每一个软件模块免受飞来横祸。尤其是,它鼓励程序员尽可能多地加入检查,哪怕与调用方所做的检查重复也要加 —— 反正加上去,就算没帮助也不会有害,建议是这么说的。

This approach suggests that routines should be as general as possible. A partial routine (one that works only if the caller ensures certain restrictive conditions at the time of the call) is considered dangerous because it might produce unwanted consequences if a caller does not abide by the rules.

这种做法暗含的是:例程应当尽可能通用。一个"部分例程"(partial routine,即只有在调用方于调用时满足某些限制性条件才能工作的例程)被视作危险的,因为一旦调用方不守规矩,它可能会产生意料之外的后果。

This technique, however, often defeats its own purposes. Adding possibly redundant code "just in case" only contributes to the software's complexity — the single worst obstacle to software quality in general, and to reliability in particular. The result of such blind checking is simply to introduce more software, hence more sources of things that could go wrong at execution time, hence the need for more checks, and so on ad infinitum. Such blind and often redundant checking causes much of the complexity and unwieldiness that often characterizes software.

然而,这种技术常常适得其反。"以防万一"地加入可能冗余的代码,只是给软件的复杂性火上浇油 —— 而复杂性是软件质量总体上、尤其是可靠性方面的头号障碍。这种盲目检查的结果,就只是引入更多代码,因而引入更多运行时出错源头,因而需要更多检查,如此无穷无尽。许多软件之所以复杂而笨重,很大程度上就是这种盲目而又往往冗余的检查造成的。

Obtaining and guaranteeing reliability requires a more systematic approach. In particular, software elements should be considered as implementations meant to satisfy well-understood specifications, not as arbitrary executable texts. This is where the contract theory comes in.

要获得并保障可靠性,需要一种更系统化的方法。特别是,应当把软件元素视作旨在满足明确理解的规约的实现,而不是任意的可执行文本。这就是契约理论登场的地方。

The notion of contract

契约的概念

Assume you are writing some program unit implementing a task to be performed at runtime. Unless the task is trivial, it involves a number of subtasks. For example, it might appear as

设想你正在编写某个程序单元,它要在运行时执行某项任务。除非任务十分平凡,否则它会涉及若干子任务。比如它可能长这样:

my_task is

        subtask_1 ;
        subtask_2 ;

        subtask_n ;
    end

    do
        subtask_1 ;
        subtask_2 ;
        ...
        subtask_n ;

a form that suffices for this discussion, although in many cases the control structure linking the various subtasks is less simple than the mere sequencing shown here.

对本文的讨论而言,这种形式就够了 —— 虽然很多情况下,串起各子任务的控制结构并不像这里单纯的顺序这么简单。

For each of these subtasks, you may either write the corresponding solution in line as part of the body of _my_task_, or rely on a call to another unit. The decision is a typical design trade-off: Too much calling causes fragmentation of the software text; too little results in overcomplex individual units.

对这些子任务中的每一个,你要么把对应的解答作为 _my_task_ 主体的一部分内联写出,要么依赖对另一个单元的调用。这个取舍是典型的设计抉择:调用太多会把软件文本切得七零八落,调用太少则会让单个单元过于臃肿。

Assume you decide to use a routine call for one of the subtasks. This is similar to the situation encountered in everyday life when you decide to contract out for a certain (human) task rather than doing it yourself. For example, if you are in Paris and want an urgent letter or package delivered to another Paris address, you may decide to deliver it yourself, or you may contract out the task to a courier service.

假设你决定用例程调用来处理其中一个子任务。这类似日常生活中的情形:你决定把某项(人类的)任务转包出去,而不是亲自去做。举例来说,你在巴黎,要把一封加急信件或一个包裹送到巴黎另一个地址,你可以自己送,也可以把这项任务转包给一家快递公司。

Two major properties characterize human contracts involving two parties: (1) Each party expects some benefits from the contract and is prepared to incur some obligations to obtain them. (2) These benefits and obligations are documented in a contract document.

涉及两方的人类契约具有两个主要特征:(1) 每一方都期望从契约中获得某些利益,并准备为此承担某些义务。(2) 这些利益和义务都被记录在一份契约文件里。

A contract document protects both sides: (1) It protects the client by specifying how much should be done: The client is entitled to receive a certain result. (2) It protects the contractor by specifying how little is acceptable: The contractor must not be liable for failing to carry out tasks outside of the specified scope.

一份契约文件对双方都有保护作用:(1) 它通过明确规定应做多少来保护客户方:客户方有权获得特定的结果。(2) 它通过明确规定最低限度可接受到什么程度来保护承包方:承包方不应为完成规定范围之外的任务而担责。

As evidenced by this example, what is an obligation for one party is usually a benefit for the other.

这个例子也印证了:一方的义务通常就是另一方的利益。

This example also suggests a somewhat more subtle observation, which is important in the following discussion (and in studying the application of these ideas to concurrent computation). If the contract is exhaustive, every "obligation" entry also in a certain sense describes a "benefit" by stating that the constraints given are the only relevant ones. For example, the obligation entry for the client indicates that a client who satisfies all the constraints listed is entitled to the benefits shown in the next entry. This is the No Hidden Clauses rule: With a fully spelled out contract between honest parties, no requirement other than the contract's official obligations may be imposed on the client as a condition for obtaining the contract's official benefits.

这个例子还暗含一个更微妙的观察,它对后续讨论(以及研究这些思想在并发计算中的应用)很重要。如果契约是穷尽的,那么每一条"义务"条目在某种意义上也描述了一种"利益",因为它声明给出的约束是唯一相关的约束。例如,客户方的义务条目意味着:一个满足所列全部约束的客户方有权获得下一条条目所列的利益。这就是 No Hidden Clauses(无隐藏条款)规则:在诚实双方之间一份充分写明的契约中,除了契约正式列出的义务之外,不得对客户方施加任何其他要求来作为获取契约正式利益的条件。

The No Hidden Clauses principle does not prevent us from including references, implicit or explicit, to rules not physically part of the contract. For example, general rules such as the relevant laws and common business practices are implicitly considered to be part of every contract of a certain kind, even if not explicitly repeated in the text of each contract. They apply to both client and supplier and will lead below to the notion of class invariant.

No Hidden Clauses 原则并不妨碍我们在契约中隐式或显式地援引物理上不在该契约之内的规则。例如,诸如相关法律和通行商业惯例这类通用规则,即使没有在每份契约的文本中明确重复,也会被隐式视为这一类契约的组成部分。它们对客户方和供应方同样适用,并将引出后文的类不变量(class invariant)概念。

Assertions: Contracting for software

断言:为软件拟定契约

It is not difficult to see how the preceding ideas apply to software construction. If the execution of a certain task relies on a routine call to handle one of its subtasks, it is necessary to specify the relationship between the client (the caller) and the supplier (the called routine) as precisely as possible. The mechanisms for expressing such conditions are called assertions. Some assertions, called preconditions and postconditions, apply to individual routines. Others, the class invariants, constrain all the routines of a given class and will be discussed later.

不难看出前述思想如何适用于软件构造。如果某项任务的执行依赖于一个例程调用来处理它的某个子任务,那就有必要尽可能精确地规定客户方(caller,调用方)与供应方(called routine,被调用例程)之间的关系。用于表达这类条件的机制被称为断言(assertions)。其中一些断言 —— 称为前置条件(preconditions)和后置条件(postconditions)—— 作用于单个例程;另一些 —— 类不变量(class invariants)—— 则约束给定类的所有例程,后文再讨论。

It is important to include the preconditions and postconditions as part of routine declarations (see Figure 1).

把前置条件和后置条件作为例程声明的一部分写出来,这一点很重要(见图 1)。

In this Eiffel notation, the Require and Ensure clauses (as well as the header comment) are optional. They introduce assertions — respectively the precondition and the postcondition. Each assertion is a list of Boolean expressions, separated by semicolons; here a semicolon is equivalent to a Boolean "and" but allows individual identification of the assertion clauses.

在这种 Eiffel 记法中,Require 和 Ensure 子句(连同头部注释)都是可选的。它们引入的是断言 —— 分别是前置条件和后置条件。每个断言是一个由分号分隔的布尔表达式列表;这里的分号等价于布尔"与",但允许对各断言子句作单独标识。

The precondition expresses requirements that any call must satisfy if it is to be correct; the postcondition expresses properties that are ensured in return by the execution of the call.

前置条件表达任何调用若要正确,就必须满足的要求;后置条件表达的则是调用执行作为回报所保证的性质。

A missing precondition clause is equivalent to the clause require True, and a missing postcondition to the clause ensure True. The assertion True is the least committing of all possible assertions. Any possible state of the computation will satisfy it.

缺省的前置条件子句等价于 require True,缺省的后置条件等价于 ensure True。断言 True 是所有可能断言中承诺最少的:计算过程中任何可能的状态都满足它。

例如,考虑一个描述树节点的类 TREE,其中有一个例程 put_child,用于为树节点 Current 添加一个新子节点。子节点通过一个引用访问,该引用必须附着于一个已存在的节点对象。表 2 以非形式化方式表达了这一契约。

这就是 put_child 对任何潜在调用方强制施加的契约。它包含了关于该例程所能给出的最重要的信息:契约中每一方为一次正确调用必须保证什么,作为回报每一方有权获得什么。正因为这些信息对使用该例程构造可靠系统极为关键,它们应当成为例程文本的正式组成部分(见图 2)。

A few more details about the rules of object-oriented programming as embodied in Eiffel should help make this example completely clear:

关于 Eiffel 中所体现的面向对象编程规则,再补充几点细节,这个例子就完全清楚了:

  • A reference such as new is either void (not attached to any object) or attached to an object. In the first case, it equals the value Void. Here the precondition expresses that the reference new must not be void, as stated informally by the corresponding entry in Table 2.
  • 像 new 这样的引用要么是void(未附着于任何对象),要么附着于某个对象。前一种情况下它等于值 Void。这里的前置条件表达的就是:引用 new 必须不为 void,正如表 2 的相应条目非形式化所述。
  • In accordance with Eiffel's object-oriented principles, the routine will appear in the text of a class describing trees, or tree nodes. This is why it does not need an argument representing the node to which the routine will add the reference new as a child; all routines of the class are relative to a typical tree node, the "current instance" of the class. In a specific call such as some_node.put_child (x), the value before the period, here some_node, serves as the current instance.
  • 按 Eiffel 的面向对象原则,该例程出现在描述树或树节点的类的文本中。正因如此,它不需要一个额外的参数来表示"将向其添加 new 作为子节点的那个节点";类中所有例程都是相对于一个典型的树节点 —— 即类的"当前实例"(current instance)—— 而言的。在形如 some_node.put_child (x) 的具体调用中,句点之前的值(这里是 some_node)就是当前实例。
  • In the text of the class, the predefined name Current serves, if necessary, to refer to the current instance. Here it is used in the postcondition.
  • 在类文本中,预定义名 Current 在必要时可用于指代当前实例。这里在后置条件中使用了它。
  • The notation old child_count, appearing in the postcondition of put_child, denotes the value of child_count as captured on entry to a particular call. In other words, the second clause of the postcondition expresses that the routine must increase child_count by one. The construct old may appear only in a routine postcondition.
  • put_child 后置条件中出现的记法 old child_count,表示 child_count 在某次调用进入时所捕获的值。换言之,后置条件的第二个子句表达的是:例程必须使 child_count 增加一。构造 old 只可出现在例程的后置条件中。
Further sources

One of the two primary sources of inspiration for this work is the research on program proving and systematic program construction pioneered by Floyd [1], Hoare [2], and Dijkstra [3]. Other well-known work on the application of proof methods to software construction includes contributions by Gries [4] and Mills [5]. The other major influence is the theory of abstract data types (see references in the body of the article).

The use of assertions in an object-oriented language and the approach to inheritance presented here (based on the notion of subcontracting) appear original to Eiffel. The exception-handling model and its implementation are also among Eiffel's contributions. These mechanisms, and the reasoning that led to them, are discussed in detail in references 1 and 2 of the main bibliography at the end of the article.

The notion of class invariant comes directly from Hoare's data invariants [6]. Invariants, as well as other assertions, also play an important role in the VDM software specification method, as described by Jones [7]. The transposition of data invariants to object-oriented software development, in the form of class invariants, appears to be new with Eiffel.

Nonobject-oriented research languages that support assertions have included Euclid [8] and Alphard [9]; see also the Ada-based specification language Anna [10]. CLU, cited in the text, includes nonformal assertions.

The view of programs as mechanisms to compute partial functions is central in the mentioned VDM method.

Another view of exceptions can be found in Cristian [11]. Eiffel's notion of a rescue clause bears some resemblance to Randell's recovery blocks [12], but the spirit and aims are different. Recovery blocks as defined by Randell are alternate implementations of the original goal of a routine, to be used when the initial implementation fails to achieve this goal. In contrast, a rescue clause does not attempt to carry on the routine's official business; it simply patches things up by bringing the object to a stable state. Any retry attempt uses the original implementation again. Also, recovery blocks require that the initial system state be restored before an alternate implementation is tried after a failure. This appears impossible to implement in any practical environment for which efficiency is of any concern. Eiffel's rescue clauses do not require any such preservation of the state; the only rule is that the rescue clause must restore the class invariant and, if resumption is attempted, the routine precondition.

The rescue clause notion was actually derived from a corresponding formal notion of surrogate function, also called doppelgänger, which appeared in the specification method and language M [13], a direct successor to Abrial's original Z language [14]. Like Z and unlike Eiffel, M was a formal specification language, not an executable language. Functions in an M specification may be partial. A surrogate is associated with a partial function and serves as a backup for arguments that do not belong to that function's domain.

The role of assertions

断言的作用

You may well be wondering what happens if one of these conditions fails to be satisfied during execution. This question will be answered by whether assertions are monitored at runtime, depending on programmer wishes. But this is not a crucial question at this point. The prime goal of this discussion is to find ways of writing reliable software systems that work. The question of what happens when they do not work, although practically significant, comes only after achieving that more fundamental goal.

你可能正想问:如果这些条件中有某一条在执行时不被满足,会发生什么?这个问题的答案取决于是否在运行时对断言进行监控 —— 这依程序员的意愿而定。但在这里,这个问题并不是关键。本讨论的首要目标,是找到编写能正确运行的可靠软件系统的方法。软件不正确运行时会发生什么,虽然在实践中也很重要,但得在实现那个更根本的目标之后再谈。

Another way of expressing this observation is to notice that assertions do not describe special but expected cases that call for special treatment. In other words, the above assertions are not a way to describe (for example) the handling of void arguments to put_child. If we wanted to treat void arguments as an acceptable (although special) case, we would handle it not through assertions but through standard conditional control structures (see Figure 3).

换一种方式表述这个观察:断言不是用来描述那些需要特殊处置的、特殊但可预期的情形。换言之,上述的断言不是用来描述(比如)对 put_child 传入 void 实参的处理。如果我们想把 void 实参视作一种可接受的(虽然是特殊的)情形,我们不会通过断言,而是通过标准的条件控制结构来处理(见图 3)。

    ... Take care of special case ...
else
    ... Take care of standard case ...
end

Assertions (here the precondition) are something else: ways to describe the conditions on which software elements will work, and the conditions they will achieve in return. By putting the condition new /= Void in the precondition, we make it part of the routine's specification; the last form shown (with the If) would mean that we have changed that specification, broadening it to include the special case new = Void as acceptable.

断言(这里是前置条件)是另一回事:它们描述软件元素将会工作所依据的条件,以及作为回报将会达成的条件。把条件 new /= Void 放进前置条件,我们就使它成为例程规约的一部分;而上面最后那种形式(用 If)的含义是,我们改变了那个规约,扩大到把 new = Void 这种特殊情形也算作可接受的。

As a consequence, any runtime violation of an assertion is not a special case but always the manifestation of a software bug. To be precise:

其结果是,运行时任何断言违反都不是某种特殊情形,而永远是软件 bug 的表现。确切地说:

  • A precondition violation indicates a bug in the client (caller). The caller did not observe the conditions imposed on correct calls.
  • 前置条件违反(precondition violation)表示客户方(调用方)有 bug。调用方没有遵守施加于正确调用之上的条件。
  • A postcondition violation is a bug in the supplier (routine). The routine failed to deliver on its promises.
  • 后置条件违反(postcondition violation)是供应方(例程)的 bug。例程未能兑现其承诺。

Using such a construction would defeat the purpose of having a precondition (Require clause). This is an absolute rule: Either you have the condition in the Require, or you have it in an If instruction in the body of the routine, but never in both.

用这种构造,就会抵消设立前置条件(Require 子句)的意义。这是一条绝对规则:要么条件写在 Require 里,要么写在例程主体的 If 指令里,绝不能两处都写。

This principle is the exact opposite of the idea of defensive programming, since it directs programmers to avoid redundant tests. Such an approach is possible and fruitful because the use of assertions encourages writing software to spell out the consistency conditions that could go wrong at runtime. Then instead of checking blindly, as with defensive programming, you can use clearly defined contracts that assign the responsibility for each consistency condition to one of the parties. If the contract is precise and explicit, there is no need for redundant checks.

这条原则正是防御式编程理念的反面,因为它引导程序员避免冗余测试。这种做法之所以可行且有效,正是因为断言的使用鼓励开发者在编写软件时明确地把运行时可能出错的一致性条件写出来。然后,不必像防御式编程那样盲目检查,而是可以使用清晰定义的契约,把每一条一致性条件的责任分派给某一方。契约若精确而显式,冗余检查就没有必要。

The stronger the precondition, the heavier the burden on the client, and the easier for the supplier. The matter of who should deal with abnormal values is essentially a pragmatic decision about division of labor: The best solution is the one that achieves the simplest architecture. If every routine and caller checked for every possible call error, routines would never perform any useful work.

前置条件越强,客户方的负担越重,供应方越轻松。谁来处理异常值,本质上是关于分工的实用决策:最佳解就是能得到最简架构的那个解。如果每个例程和每个调用方都去检查每一种可能的调用错误,例程就永远干不了什么有用的活。

In many existing programs, one can hardly find the islands of useful processing in oceans of error-checking code. In the absence of assertions, defensive programming may be the only reasonable approach. But with techniques for defining precisely each party's responsibility, as provided by assertions, such redundancy (so harmful to the consistency and simplicity of the structure) is not needed.

在许多现有程序里,很难在错误检查代码的汪洋大海中找到那几座真正有用处理的孤岛。没有断言机制的话,防御式编程也许是唯一合理的选择。但有了断言所提供的、用于精确定义各方职责的手段,这种(对结构的一致性和简洁性极为有害的)冗余就不再需要了。

Who should check?

该由谁检查?

The rejection of defensive programming means that the client and supplier are not both held responsible for a consistency condition. Either the condition is part of the precondition and must be guaranteed by the client, or it is not stated in the precondition and must be handled by the supplier.

拒绝防御式编程意味着:对于一个一致性条件,不会让客户方和供应方双方都担责。这个条件要么是前置条件的一部分、由客户方保证,要么不在前置条件中、由供应方处理。

Which of these two solutions should be chosen? There is no absolute rule; several styles of writing routines are possible, ranging from "demanding" ones where the precondition is strong (putting the responsibility on clients) to "tolerant" ones where it is weak (increasing the routine's burden). Choosing between them is to a certain extent a matter of personal preference; again, the key criterion is to maximize the overall simplicity of the architecture.

两种做法该选哪一种?没有绝对的规则;编写例程的风格有很多种,从前置条件很强的"苛刻型"(把责任压在客户方身上)到前置条件很弱的"宽容型"(加重例程负担)。选择在一定程度上是个人偏好;同样,关键判据是让架构的整体简洁性最大化。

The experience with Eiffel, in particular the design of the libraries, suggests that the systematic use of a demanding style can be quite successful. In this approach, every routine concentrates on doing a well-defined job so as to do it well, rather than attempting to handle every imaginable case. Client programmers do not expect miracles. As long as the conditions on the use of a routine make sense, and the routine's documentation states these conditions (the contract) explicitly, the programmers will be able to use the routine properly by observing their part of the deal.

Eiffel 的经验 —— 特别是库的设计 —— 表明,系统化地采用苛刻风格可以相当成功。在这种做法下,每个例程都专注于把一件定义良好的事情做好,而不是试图应对所有能想到的情形。客户方程序员并不指望奇迹。只要例程的使用条件说得通,并且例程的文档把这些条件(契约)明确陈述出来,程序员就能通过遵守自己那一份协议来正确地使用该例程。

One objection to this style is that it seems to force every client to make the same checks, corresponding to the precondition, and thus results in unnecessary and damaging repetitions. But this argument is not justified:

对这种风格有一种反对意见是:它似乎迫使每个客户方都做相同的、对应于前置条件的检查,从而造成不必要且有害的重复。但这个论点站不住脚:

  • The presence of a precondition p in a routine r does not necessarily mean that every call must test for p, as in
  • 一个例程 r 中存在前置条件 p,并不必然意味着每次调用都必须测试 p,像这样:
if x.p then
    x.r
else
    ... Special Treatment ...
end
if x.p then
    x.r
else
    ... Special Treatment ...
end

What the precondition means is that the client must guarantee property p; this is not the same as testing for this condition before each call. If the context of the call implies p, then there is no need for such a test. A typical scheme is

前置条件的含义是客户方必须保证性质 p;这与每次调用前测试这个条件不是一回事。如果调用的上下文已经蕴含 p,那就没必要测试。一种典型的模式是

x.s; x.r

where the postcondition of s implies p.

其中 s 的后置条件蕴含 p。

  • Assume that many clients will indeed need to check for the precondition. Then what matters is the "Special Treatment." It is either the same for all calls or specific to each call. If it is the same, causing undue repetition in various clients, this is simply the sign of a poor class interface design, using an overly demanding contract for r. The contract should be renegotiated and made broader (more tolerant) to include the standard Special Treatment as part of the routine's specification.
  • 假设许多客户方确实需要检查前置条件。那么关键在于"Special Treatment"(特殊处理)。它要么对所有调用都一样,要么因调用而异。如果一样 —— 从而在各个客户方那里造成多余重复 —— 这仅仅说明类的接口设计差,对 r 采用了过于苛刻的契约。契约应当被重新协商,放宽成更宽容的版本,把标准的特殊处理纳入例程规约的一部分。

Class invariants

类不变量

Routine preconditions and postconditions may be used in non-object-oriented approaches, although they fit particularly well with the object-oriented method. Invariants, the next major use of assertions, are inconceivable outside of the object-oriented approach.

例程的前置条件和后置条件在非面向对象的方法中也可以使用,只不过它们与面向对象方法尤为契合。而不变量 —— 断言的下一个主要用途 —— 在面向对象方法之外是不可想象的。

A class invariant is a property that applies to all instances of the class, transcending particular routines. For example, the invariant of a class describing nodes of a binary tree could be of the form shown in Figure 4, stating that the parent of both the left and right children of a node, if these children exist, is the node itself. (The Implies operator denotes implication. Eiffel operator precedence rules make the parentheses unnecessary here; they have been added for clarity.)

类不变量是适用于该类所有实例的性质,超越于个别例程之上。例如,描述二叉树节点的类的不变量可以写成图 4 所示的形式,表达的是:一个节点的左、右子节点(如果存在)的父节点就是这个节点本身。(Implies 运算符表示蕴含。Eiffel 的运算符优先级规则使这里的括号本不必要,加上只为清晰起见。)

    left /= Void implies (left.parent = Current);
    right /= Void implies (right.parent = Current)

    right /= Void implies (right.parent = Current)

    ... As shown above ...

Two properties characterize a class invariant:

类不变量具有两个特征:

  • The invariant must be satisfied after the creation of every instance of the class (every binary tree in this example). This means that every creation procedure of the class must yield an object satisfying the invariant. (A class may have one or more creation procedures, which serve to initialize objects. The creation procedure to be called in any given case is specified in the creation instruction.)
  • 不变量必须在该类每个实例(本例中每棵二叉树)被创建之后成立。这意味着该类每个创建过程(creation procedure)都必须产出满足不变量的对象。(一个类可以有一个或多个创建过程,用于初始化对象。具体某次调用哪个创建过程,由创建指令指定。)
  • The invariant must be preserved by every exported routine of the class (that is to say, every routine available to clients). Any such routine must guarantee that the invariant is satisfied on exit if it was satisfied on entry.
  • 不变量必须被该类每个导出的(即可供客户方使用的)例程所保持。任何这样的例程都必须保证:若不变量在入口处成立,则在出口处也成立。

In effect, then, the invariant is added to the precondition and postcondition of every exported routine of the class. But the invariant characterizes the class as a whole rather than its individual routines.

实际上,这就等于把不变量附加到了该类每个导出例程的前置条件和后置条件上。但不变量刻画的是类作为一个整体,而不是其单个例程。

On the assertion language

This article includes many examples of typical assertions. But what exactly is permissible in an assertion?

Eiffel assertions are Boolean expressions, with a few extensions such as the old notation. Since the whole power of Boolean expressions is available, they may include function calls. Because the full power of the language is available to write these functions, the conditions they express can be quite sophisticated. For example, the invariant of a class ACYCLIC_GRAPH may contain a clause of the form not cyclic, where cyclic is a Boolean-valued function that determines whether a graph contains any cycles by using the appropriate graph algorithm.

In some cases, one might want to use quantified expressions of the form "For all x of type T, p (x) holds" or "There exists x of type T, such that p (x) holds," where p is a certain Boolean property. Such expressions are not available in Eiffel. It is possible, however, to express the corresponding properties by using the same technique: calls to functions that rely on loops to emulate the quantifiers.

Although some thought has been given to extend the language to include a full-fledged formal specification language, with first-order predicate calculus, the need for such an extension does not seem crucial. In Eiffel, intended as a vehicle for industrial software development rather than just for research, the use of function calls in assertions seems to provide an acceptable trade-off between different design goals: reliability, the ability to generate efficient code, and overall simplicity of the language.

In fact, first-order predicate calculus would not necessarily be sufficient. Many practically important properties, such as the requirement that a graph be noncyclic, would require higher order calculus.

The use of functions — that is to say, computations — is not without its dangers. In software, a function is a case of a routine; it prescribes certain actions. This makes software functions imperative, whereas mathematical functions are said to be applicative. The major difference is that software functions can produce side effects (change the state of the computation). Introducing functions into assertions lets the imperative fox back into the applicative chicken coop.

In practice, this means that any function used in assertions must be of unimpeachable quality, avoiding any change to the current state and any operation that could result in abnormal situations.

关于断言语言

本文给了许多典型断言的例子。但断言里究竟允许出现什么?

Eiffel 的断言是布尔表达式,附带若干扩展,比如 old 记法。既然可以使用布尔表达式的全部力量,它们就可以包含函数调用。又因为写这些函数时可以使用语言的全部能力,它们所能表达的条件可以相当复杂。比如,类 ACYCLIC_GRAPH 的不变量中可能包含形如 not cyclic 的子句,其中 cyclic 是一个布尔值函数,用相应的图算法判断一个图是否含有任何环路。

某些情况下,可能想用"对所有类型 T 的 x,p (x) 成立"或"存在类型 T 的 x,使得 p (x) 成立"这种带量词的表达式,其中 p 是某个布尔性质。这类表达式在 Eiffel 中不可用。但可以用同样的技术表达相应的性质:调用依赖循环来模拟量词的函数。

虽然曾考虑过将语言扩展为包含一阶谓词演算的完整形式化规约语言,但这样的扩展看起来并不是关键的。Eiffel 被定位为工业软件开发的载体,而不仅是研究工具,在断言中使用函数调用看来在多个设计目标(可靠性、能生成高效代码、语言整体简洁性)之间提供了可接受的折中。

事实上一阶谓词演算未必够用。许多在实践中重要的性质,比如要求图无环,都需要高阶演算。

使用函数 —— 也就是计算 —— 并非没有风险。在软件中,函数是例程的一种;它规定了某些动作。这使得软件函数是命令式(imperative)的,而数学函数则是应用式(applicative)的。主要差异在于:软件函数可能产生副作用(改变计算状态)。把函数引入断言,就等于让命令式的狐狸溜回了应用式的鸡窝。

在实践中,这意味着断言中使用的任何函数都必须质量过硬:不改变当前状态,不做任何可能导致异常情形的操作。

Monitoring assertions

监控断言

What happens if, during execution, a system violates one of its own assertions?

如果在执行过程中系统违反了自己的某条断言,会发生什么?

In the development environment, the answer depends on a compilation option. For each class, you may choose from various levels of assertion monitoring: no assertion checking, preconditions only (the default), preconditions and postconditions, all of the above plus class invariants, or all assertions. (The difference between the last two follows from the existence of other assertions, such as loop invariants, not covered in the present discussion.)

在开发环境里,答案取决于一个编译选项。对每个类,你可以在若干个断言监控级别中选择:不做断言检查;仅前置条件(默认);前置加后置条件;上述加类不变量;或所有断言。(最后两档之间的差别源于还存在其他断言,比如循环不变量 —— 本文不涉及。)

For a class compiled under the "no assertion monitoring" option, assertions have no effect on system execution. The subsequent options cause evaluation of assertions at various stages: routine entry for preconditions, routine exit for postconditions, and both steps for invariants.

在"不做断言监控"选项下编译的类,其断言对系统执行无任何效果。其余各档选项会在不同阶段求值断言:前置条件在例程入口处,后置条件在例程出口处,不变量则两头都查。

Under the monitoring options, the effect of an assertion violation is to raise an exception. The possible responses to an exception are discussed later.

在监控选项开启时,断言违反的效果是抛出一个异常(raise an exception)。对异常的可能响应将在后文讨论。

Why monitor?

为什么要监控?

As noted, assertion violations are not special (but expected) cases; they result from bugs. The main application of runtime assertion monitoring, then, is debugging. Turning assertion checking on (at any of the levels previously listed) makes it possible to detect mistakes.

如前所述,断言违反不是特殊的(但可预期的)情形;它们源自 bug。因此运行时断言监控的主要用途就是调试。在前述任一级别打开断言检查,就能让错误浮现出来。

When writing software, developers make many assumptions about the properties that will hold at various stages of the software's execution, especially routine entry and return. In the usual approaches to software construction, these assumptions remain informal and implicit. Here the assertion mechanism enables developers to express them explicitly. Assertion monitoring, then, is a way to call the developer's bluff by checking what the software does against what its author thinks it does. This yields a productive approach to debugging, testing, and quality assurance, in which the search for errors is not blind but based on consistency conditions provided by the developers themselves.

写代码时,开发者会对软件执行各阶段(尤其是例程入口和返回)成立的性质做出许多假设。在通常的软件构造方法里,这些假设停留在非正式、隐式的状态。这里断言机制使开发者能显式地把它们表达出来。所以断言监控就是拿软件实际在做什么和作者以为它在做什么相对照,来揭开开发者的底牌。这带来了一种高效的调试、测试和质量保证途径:错误搜寻不再是盲目的,而是基于开发者自己给出的一致性条件。

Particularly interesting here is the use of preconditions in library classes. In the general approach to software construction suggested by the Eiffel method, developers build successive "clusters" of classes in a bottom-up order, from more general (reusable) to more specific (application-dependent). This is the "cluster model" of the software life cycle [10]. Deciding to release a library cluster l for general use normally implies a reasonable degree of confidence in its quality — the belief that no bugs remain in l. So it may be unnecessary to monitor the postconditions of routines in the classes of l. But the classes of an application cluster that is a client of l (see Figure 7) may still be "young" and contain bugs; such bugs may show up as erroneous arguments in calls to routines of the classes of l. Monitoring preconditions for classes of l helped to find them. This is one of the reasons why precondition checking is the default compilation option.

这里特别值得一提的,是库类中前置条件的用法。按 Eiffel 方法所建议的整体软件构造路径,开发者自底向上地构建一系列"簇"(cluster)——从更通用(可复用)的到更具体(应用相关)的。这就是软件生命周期的"簇模型"[10]。决定把一个库簇 l 公开发布以供通用,通常意味着对其质量有合理程度的信心 —— 相信 l 中已无遗留 bug。所以对 l 中类的例程监控后置条件可能没必要。但作为 l 客户方的应用簇中的类(见图 7)可能还很"年轻"、仍带着 bug;这样的 bug 可能就表现为对 l 中例程的调用里出现错误实参。这时监控 l 中类的前置条件就帮到了你找出它们。这正是为什么前置条件检查是默认编译选项的原因之一。

Introducing inheritance

引入继承

One of the consequences of the contract theory is a better understanding and control of the fundamental object-oriented notion of inheritance and of the key associated techniques: redeclaration, polymorphism, and dynamic binding.

契约理论的推论之一,是对面向对象核心概念 —— 继承,以及与之相关的关键技术(重新声明、多态、动态绑定)—— 有了更好的理解与掌控。

Through inheritance, you can define new classes by combining previous ones. A class that inherits from another has all the features (routines and attributes) defined in that class, plus its own. But it is not required to retain the exact form of inherited features: It may redeclare them to change their specification, their implementation, or both. This flexibility of the inheritance mechanism is central to the power of the object-oriented method.

通过继承,可以通过组合已有的类来定义新的类。继承自另一个类的类,会拥有那个类定义的全部特性(例程和属性),再加上自己的。但它不必保留继承特性的原样:它可以重新声明(redeclare)以改变其规约、其实现,或二者兼改。继承机制的这种灵活性是面向对象方法威力的核心。

For example, a binary tree class could provide a default representation and the corresponding implementations for search and insertion operations. A descendant of that class may provide a representation that is specifically adapted to certain cases (such as almost full binary trees) and redeclare the routines accordingly.

举例来说,一个二叉树类可以提供一种默认表示,以及搜索和插入操作的相应实现。该类的一个后代类可以提供专为某些情形(比如几乎填满的二叉树)量身定制的表示,并相应地重新声明这些例程。

Such a form of redeclaration is called a redefinition. It assumes that the inherited routine already had an implementation. The other form of redeclaration, called effecting, applies to features for which the inherited version, known as a deferred (or abstract) feature, had no implementation, but only a specification. The effecting then provides an implementation (making the feature effective, the reverse of deferred). The subsequent discussion applies to both forms of redeclaration, although for simplicity it concentrates on redefinition.

这种形式的重新声明称为重定义(redefinition)。它假定被继承的例程已经有一份实现。另一种形式的重新声明称为实现化(effecting),作用于继承版本尚无实现、只有规约的特性 —— 后者称为延迟(deferred,即抽象)特性。实现化就是提供一份实现(使该特性由延迟变为有效,effective,即 deferred 的反面)。后续讨论对两种重新声明都适用,但为简单起见,重点放在重定义上。

In this way, the new precondition is guaranteed to be weaker than or equal to the original's, and the new postcondition is guaranteed to be stronger than or equal to the original's.

其中 Or Else 和 And Then 分别是"或"和"与"的非交换版本(仅在必要时才求值第二个实参)。这样一来,新前置条件保证弱于或等于原版本,新后置条件保证强于或等于原版本。

The concurrency issue

The theory of design by contract raises important questions regarding the application of object-oriented ideas to concurrent computation. In discussing contracts, this article mentions that clients may view the precondition of a routine not just as an obligation but also in part as a benefit, since the contract implicitly indicates that a call satisfying the precondition will be serviced correctly. This is the No Hidden Clause rule. For example, if the insertion routine put for a BOUNDED_QUEUE class has the precondition not full to state that an insertion operation requires a queue that is not full, then a protected call of the form

q: BOUNDED_QUEUE [T]; x: T; ... if not q.full then q.put (x) end

will succeed, since the client executing this call has taken the trouble to check the precondition explicitly.

In parallel computation, however, things are not so nice. The bounded queue in this example may be used as a bounded buffer, accessible to several processors. The processor in charge of the client, which will carry out the above instructions, and the processor in charge of q, which will carry out the execution of put, could be different processors. Then, even if the test for q.full yields false, between the time the client executes this test and the time it executes the call q.put (x), quite a few events may have occurred. For example, another client may have made the queue full by executing its own call to put.

In other words, a different semantic interpretation may be necessary for preconditions in the context of parallel computation. This observation serves as the starting point for some of the current work on models for concurrent object-oriented programming [1],[2].

然而在并行计算里,情况就没这么理想了。本例中的有界队列可能被当作一个有界缓冲区,供多个处理器访问。负责该客户方(将执行上述指令)的处理器,和负责 q(将执行 put)的处理器,可能不是同一个。那样的话,即使对 q.full 的测试结果为 false,从客户方执行此测试的那一刻、到它执行调用 q.put (x) 的那一刻之间,完全可能发生不少事件。比如,另一个客户方可能通过它自己对 put 的调用把队列塞满了。

换言之,在并行计算的语境下,前置条件可能需要一种不同的语义解释。这个观察是当前一些并发面向对象编程模型工作的起点 [1],[2]。

Invariants and dynamic binding

In addition to the rules on preconditions and postconditions, another constraint ties assertions with inheritance: Invariants are always passed on to descendants.

This is a direct result of the view that inheritance is (among other things) classification. If we want to consider every instance of a class B as being also an instance of B's ancestors, we must accept that consistency constraints on a parent A also apply to instances of B.

例如,如果描述树节点的类 TREE 的不变量中包含子句 child.parent = Current(表达一个节点的当前活动子节点的父节点就是该节点本身),那么这个子句将自动适用于继承自 TREE 的类 BINARY_TREE 的实例。正因如此,语言规约把"一个类的不变量"定义为:将该类不变量子句中的断言,与所有父类的不变量(按此定义递归而得)连接起来所得到的断言 [1]。

Dealing with abnormal situations

应对异常情形

The Design by Contract theory has one more immediate application to the practice of reliable software development: exception handling.

按契约设计理论对可靠软件开发的实践还有一项直接应用:异常处理。

Exceptions — abnormal cases — have been the target of much study, and several programming languages, notably Ada, PL/I, and CLU, offer exception-handling mechanisms. Much of this work is disappointing, however, because it fails to define precisely what an abnormal case is. Then exception handling often becomes a kind of generalized, interroutine "goto" mechanism, with no clear guidelines for proper use.

异常 —— 即异常情形 —— 已是许多研究的对象,Ada、PL/I、CLU 等几门编程语言都提供了异常处理机制。然而这方面的许多工作令人失望,因为它们没能精确地定义"异常情形"到底是什么。于是异常处理往往沦为一种广义的、跨例程的"goto"机制,没有明确的使用指引。

To understand the issue better, I performed a study (reported elsewhere [3]) of Ada and CLU textbooks, looking for examples of exception handling and methodological principles. The results were disappointing, as the books showed many examples of triggering exceptions but few of how to handle them. Furthermore, some of the latter were hair-raising. For example, one textbook proposed an example of a square root routine which, when confronted with a negative argument, triggers an exception. The exception handler prints a message and then simply returns to the caller without notifying the caller that something wrong has occurred — fooling the caller, as it were, into believing that everything is going according to plan. Since a typical use for square roots in a typical Ada program is a missile trajectory computation, it is easy to foresee the probable consequences.

为更好地理解这个问题,我做了一项研究(报告另见 [3]):翻阅 Ada 和 CLU 的教材,寻找异常处理的例子和方法论原则。结果令人失望,因为这些书展示了大量触发(triggering)异常的例子,但很少讲怎么处理(handle)。而且后者中有些还让人头皮发麻。比如有一本教材举了一个求平方根的例程的例子,当遇到负实参时触发一个异常。异常处理器打印一条消息,然后径直返回调用方 —— 根本不告诉调用方已经出事了 —— 于是就在骗调用方相信一切顺利进行。考虑到典型 Ada 程序里一个典型的平方根用途是导弹弹道计算,可能的后果不难预见。

Beyond the bad taste of such individual examples, one may fault the design of the exception mechanism itself for failing to encourage, or even to define, a proper discipline for handling abnormal cases.

抛开这类个例的恶劣趣味不说,异常机制的设计本身也值得批评:它没能鼓励 —— 甚至没有定义 —— 一套处理异常情形的像样规程。

The contract theory provides a good starting point for a more rational solution. If a routine is seen not just as some "piece of code" but as the implementation of a certain specification — the contract — it is possible to define a notion of failure. Failure occurs when an execution of a routine cannot fulfill the routine's contract. Possible reasons for a failure include a hardware malfunction, a bug in the implementation, or some external unexpected event.

契约理论为一个更理性的解法提供了良好起点。如果把例程不仅仅视作某段"代码",而是视作某个规约 —— 即契约 —— 的实现,就可以定义故障(failure)这个概念。故障发生于例程的一次执行无法兑现其契约时。可能的故障原因包括硬件故障、实现中的 bug,或某个外部意外事件。

"Failure" is here the basic concept. "Exception" is a derived notion. An exception occurs when a certain strategy for fulfilling a routine's contract has not succeeded. This is not a failure, at least not yet, because the routine may have an alternative strategy.

这里"故障"是基本概念,"异常"(exception)是派生概念。异常发生于用来兑现例程契约的某个策略未能成功之时。这还不算故障 —— 至少还不算,因为例程可能还有替代策略。

The most obvious example of exception is the failure of a called routine: r's strategy for fulfilling its contract involved a call to s; the call failed; clearly, this is an exception for r. Another example, previously mentioned, is a runtime assertion violation, if assertions are monitored. It is also convenient to treat as exceptions the signals sent by the operating system or the hardware: arithmetic overflow, memory exhaustion, and the like. They indeed correspond to failures of calls to basic facilities (arithmetic operations, memory allocation).

异常最显眼的例子就是被调用例程的故障:r 为兑现自己的契约所用的策略涉及一次对 s 的调用;该调用失败了;显然,对 r 而言这就是一个异常。另一个前面提过的例子,是运行时断言违反 —— 如果断言被监控的话。把操作系统或硬件发出的信号(算术溢出、内存耗尽等等)也当作异常来处理是方便的。它们确实对应于对基本设施(算术运算、内存分配)调用的故障。

Equipped with this notion of failure and exception, we can define a coherent response to an exception. The exception occurs because the strategy used to achieve the routine's contract did not work. Only three possible responses then make sense:

配备了这样的故障和异常概念,我们就能定义一种连贯的异常响应方式。异常之所以发生,是因为用于兑现例程契约的策略没奏效。那么有意义的响应只有三种:

(1) Perhaps an alternative strategy is available. We have lost a battle, but we have not lost the war. In this case the routine should put the objects back into a consistent state and make another attempt, using the new strategy. This is called resumption.

(1) 也许还有一种替代策略可用。这场战役输了,但战争还没输。这时例程应当把各对象恢复到一致状态,并用新策略再试一次。这称为重启(resumption)。

(2) Perhaps, however, we have lost the war altogether. No new strategy is available. Then the routine should put back the objects in a consistent state, give up on the contract, and report failure to the caller. This is called organized panic.

(2) 不过,也可能是整场战争都输了,没有新策略可用。那么例程应当把各对象恢复到一致状态、放弃契约、向调用方报告故障。这称为有序恐慌(organized panic)。

(3) A rare but possible third case is the false alarm. This may occur only for operating-system or hardware signals. On some multiwindowing systems, for example, a process receives a signal (transformed by the runtime into an exception) when its window is resized. In most cases, the process should be able to continue its execution, possibly after taking some corrective actions (such as registering the new window dimensions for use by editors and other tools).

(3) 第三种情形罕见但确有可能,就是虚假警报(false alarm)。这只会发生在操作系统或硬件信号上。比如在某些多窗口系统里,进程在它的窗口被调整大小时会收到一个信号(由运行时转成异常)。多数情况下,进程应当能继续执行,可能还得采取一些纠正动作(比如把新的窗口尺寸登记给编辑器和其他工具)。

The description of both resumption and organized panic mentions putting back the objects "in a consistent state." This is essential if further executions (after an eventual resumption) will use the objects again. The notion of consistent state should be clear from the preceding discussion: Any exception handling, whether for resumption or for organized panic, should restore the invariant.

重启和有序恐慌的描述里都提到了把各对象"恢复到一致状态"。这至关重要,因为后续执行(若最终选择重启)还会再用到这些对象。一致状态的含义从前文讨论应已清楚:任何异常处理 —— 无论是为了重启还是有序恐慌 —— 都应当恢复不变量。

A disciplined exception-handling mechanism

一种有纪律的异常处理机制

It is not hard to devise an exception mechanism that directly supports the preceding method for handling abnormal cases.

不难设计出一种异常机制,直接支持上述处理异常情形的方法。

To specify how a routine should behave after an exception, the author of an Eiffel routine may include a "rescue" clause, which expresses the alternate behavior of the routine (and is similar to clauses that occur in human contracts, to allow for exceptional, unplanned circumstances). When a routine includes a rescue clause, any exception occurring during the routine's execution interrupts the execution of the body (the Do clause) and starts execution of the rescue clause. The clause contains zero or more instructions, one of which may be a Retry. The execution terminates in either of two ways:

为了规定例程在异常后应如何行事,Eiffel 例程的作者可以加入一个"救援"(rescue)子句,用于表达例程的替代行为(与人类契约中用于应对突发、计划外情形的条款类似)。当例程包含救援子句时,执行中任何异常都会中断例程主体(Do 子句)的执行,转而开始执行救援子句。该子句包含零条或多条指令,其中可以有一条 Retry。执行以下两种方式之一结束:

  • If the rescue clause terminates without executing a Retry, the routine fails. It reports failure to its caller by triggering a new exception. This is the organized panic case.
  • 如果救援子句执行完毕且没有执行 Retry,则例程故障(fails)。它通过触发一个新的异常,向调用方报告故障。这就是有序恐慌的情形。
  • If the rescue clause executes a Retry, the body of the routine (Do clause) is executed again.
  • 如果救援子句执行了 Retry,则例程主体(Do 子句)会再次执行。

As an example, here is a solution to a problem found in many Ada textbooks: Using a function getint, which reads an integer, prompt a user to enter an integer value; if the input is not an integer, ask again, unless the user cannot provide an integer after five attempts, in which case a failure occurs. It is assumed that getint is an external routine, perhaps written in C or assembly language, and we have no control over it. It triggers an exception when applied to input that is not an integer; the routine should catch that exception and prompt the user again. Figure 12 shows a solution.

举个例子,这里是许多 Ada 教材里的一个问题的解答:用一个读入整数的函数 getint,提示用户输入一个整数值;若输入非整数,就再问一次 —— 除非用户在五次尝试后仍不能提供整数,此时产生故障。假设 getint 是一个外部例程,可能由 C 或汇编编写,我们无法控制它。当它被施加于非整数输入时会触发一个异常;该例程应当捕获此异常并再次提示用户。图 12 展示了一个解。

            message ("Input must be an integer. Please enter again: ");

交互式用户前五次输入错误时,例程都会凭 Retry 重新启动。这就是重启的直接实现。

The local entity failures serves to record the number of failed calls to getint. Like any integer local entity, it is automatically initialized to zero on routine call. (The Eiffel language definition [1] specifies simple initialization values for every possible type.)

局部实体 failures 用于记录 getint 失败调用的次数。与任何整型局部实体一样,它在例程调用时自动初始化为零。(Eiffel 语言定义 [1] 为每种可能的类型都规定了简单的初始化值。)

In this example, only one type of exception is possible. In some cases, the rescue clause might need to discriminate between possible types of exceptions and handle them differently. This is made possible through simple features of the kernel library class EXCEPTIONS, although it isn't necessary to look at the (straightforward) details here. This class also provides mechanisms for handling the false alarm case by specifying that for certain signals execution may be allowed to resume.

本例中只可能有一种异常。某些情况下,救援子句可能需要对可能的异常类型加以区分并分别处理。这通过内核库类 EXCEPTIONS 的一些简单特性即可做到,此处无需深入那些(简单直接的)细节。该类也提供机制来处理虚假警报情形 —— 通过指定某些信号允许执行继续。

What happens after five successive failures of getint? The rescue clause terminates without executing a Retry and the routine execution fails (organized panic). The key rule in this case is that the caller of get_integer_from_user will get an exception, which it will have to handle by using the same policy, choosing between organized panic, resumption, and false alarm.

getint 连续五次失败之后会发生什么?救援子句执行完毕且未执行 Retry,例程执行故障(有序恐慌)。关键规则是:get_integer_from_user 的调用方会收到一个异常,它得用同一套策略来处理 —— 在有序恐慌、重启和虚假警报之间作选择。

In a typical system, only a handful of routines have an explicit rescue clause. What if an exception occurs during the execution of a routine that has no such clause? The rule is simple: An absent clause is considered equivalent to an implicit clause of the form

在一个典型系统里,只有少数例程带有显式的救援子句。那么当异常发生在一个没有此子句的例程里时呢?规则很简单:缺省的子句被视作等价于一个隐式子句,形如

rescue
    default_rescue
rescue
    default_rescue

where default_rescue is a general-purpose procedure that, in its basic form, does nothing. Then an exception simply starts the rescue clause, which, executing the empty default_rescue, causes failure of the routine; this triggers the rescue clause, explicit or implicit. If exceptions are passed in this manner all the way back to the "root object" that started the execution, that execution halts after printing an exception history table that clearly documents the sequence of recorded abnormal events. But, of course, some routine in the call chain may have a rescue clause, even one containing a Retry that will attempt a resumption.

其中 default_rescue 是一个通用过程,其基本形式什么也不做。这样,异常发生时就只是启动救援子句 —— 它执行空的 default_rescue、使例程故障;这又触发(显式或隐式的)救援子句。若异常就这样一路传回发起执行的"根对象"(root object),执行就在打印一张异常历史表后终止,该表清晰地记录了所记录的异常事件序列。当然,调用链中可能有某个例程带有救援子句,里面甚至可能带一个 Retry 来尝试重启。

Why define the default behavior as a call to default_rescue rather than just as an empty rescue clause? The reason comes from the methodological discussion. In the case of organized panic, it is essential to restore the invariant before conceding defeat and surrendering. A null action would not achieve this for a class with a nontrivial invariant.

为什么把默认行为定义为对 default_rescue 的调用,而不是干脆定义成一个空的救援子句?原因来自方法论上的讨论。在有序恐慌的情形下,在认输投降之前恢复不变量是必不可少的。对一个有非平凡不变量的类,空动作做不到这点。

The solution is provided once again by the coalesced forces of inheritance and assertions. Procedure default_rescue, in its default null form, appears as a procedure of the general-purpose class ANY. This library class, as defined by the language rules [1], is automatically an ancestor of all possible developer-defined classes. So it is the responsibility of designers of a class C, if they are concerned about possible exceptions occurring in routines that do not have specific rescue clauses, to redefine default_rescue so that it will ensure the class invariant of C.

解答再次由继承与断言的合力提供。过程 default_rescue,以其默认的空动作形式,作为通用类 ANY 的一个过程出现。按语言规则 [1],这个库类自动是所有开发者定义的类的祖先。因此,如果类 C 的设计者担心在没有专门救援子句的例程里发生异常,他们有责任重定义 default_rescue,使其能保住 C 的类不变量。

Often, one of the creation procedures may serve as a redefinition of default_rescue, since creation procedures are also required to ensure the invariant.

很多时候,某个创建过程就可以充当 default_rescue 的重定义 —— 因为创建过程本身也要求保证不变量。

This illuminates the difference between the body (the Do clause) and the rescue clause:

这也点明了例程主体(Do 子句)与救援子句的差别:

  • The body must implement the contract, or ensure the postcondition. For consistency, it must also abide by the general law of the land — preserve the invariant. Its job is made a bit easier by the assumption that the invariant will hold initially, guaranteeing that the routine will find objects in a consistent state.
  • 主体必须实现契约,即保证后置条件。为保持一致,它还必须遵守一地通法 —— 保持不变量。它的任务有一点小优惠:可以假定不变量在入口处就已经成立,这保证了例程一进来看到的各对象已处于一致状态。
  • In contrast, the rescue clause may not make any such assumption; it has no precondition, since an exception may occur at any time. Its reward is a less-demanding task. All that it is required to do on exit is to restore the invariant. Ensuring the postcondition — the contract — is not its job.
  • 相反,救援子句不能作出这样的假定;它没有前置条件,因为异常随时可能发生。它的奖赏是任务更轻:出口时只需恢复不变量。保证后置条件 —— 即契约 —— 不是它的职责。

A useful analogy is the contrast between the grandeur and servitude of two equally respectable professions — cook and fire fighter. A cook may assume that the restaurant is not burning (satisfies the invariant) when the workday begins. If the restaurant is indeed nonburning, the cook must prepare meals (ensure the postcondition). It is also a part of the cook's contract, although perhaps an implicit one, to avoid setting the restaurant on fire in the process (to maintain the invariant).

一个有用的类比:两种同样体面的职业的高贵与辛酸之间的对比 —— 厨师和消防员。厨师可以假定餐厅上工时没在着火(满足不变量)。如果餐厅确实没在着火,厨师就必须备餐(保证后置条件)。厨师的契约里还有 —— 虽然也许是隐含的 —— 一条义务:备餐过程中别把餐厅点着(维持不变量)。

When the fire fighter is called for help, in contrast, the state of the restaurant is not guaranteed. It may be burning or (in the case of a wrong alert) not burning. But then the fire fighter's only duty is to return the restaurant to a nonburning state. Serving meals to the assembled customers is not part of the fire fighter's job description.

相比之下,消防员被喊来帮忙时,餐厅的状态没有保障。它可能正在烧,也可能(若是误报)没在烧。但此时消防员的唯一职责是把餐厅恢复到不着火的状态。给满座的客人上菜不在消防员的岗位职责里。
